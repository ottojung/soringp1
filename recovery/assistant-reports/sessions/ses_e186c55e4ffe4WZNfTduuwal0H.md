**Precise front / claim attacked (all `n`, falsifiable)**
- *Objects.* A comparison history `H = ((p1,o1),…,(pd,od))` is a sequence of pairwise comparison outcomes reachable by legal play from the `n`-element antichain `A_n`. Let `P(H)` be the induced partial order (transitive closure of outcomes) and `d(H)=|H|`. Let `F(P)` be the exact minimax remaining comparison number from state `P` (sorting under partial information), so `F(A_n)=S(n)` (problem.md / `S_eq_adv_univ` model).
- *Decision problem (OPT-EXTEND).* Input `(n,H)` (standard explicit encoding of `n` and the compared pairs/outcomes, size `poly(n,|H|)`). Question: can `H` be extended to some adaptive comparison strategy of total height `S(n)` that correctly sorts all `n!` permutations?
- *Target claim under attack.* **(P-time extendability)** OPT-EXTEND `∈ P`: for all `n` and all reachable `H`, decidable in time polynomial in `n` and `|H|`.

**Mathematical finding (deduction, model-level)**
1. **State characterization.** Because the minimax value depends on the current information state, not the path except through that state, `H` is extendable to total height `≤ B` iff `F(P(H)) ≤ B - d(H)`. Therefore
   - `H` extendable to *some* height-`S(n)` optimal strategy iff `F(P(H)) ≤ S(n) - d(H)`.
   - In any fixed optimal strategy, a node at depth `d` satisfies `F(P) ≤ S(n)-d` (node-local/global-optimal observation already on board). Conversely, `F(P(H)) ≤ S(n)-d(H)` is exactly the existence of a completing subtree within budget, so extendability of that reached prefix is a **local-in-state, global-in-threshold** predicate: local in `(P(H),d(H))`, but parameterized by the global optimum `S(n)`.
2. **Complexity implication (as stated in front) is conditional/certificate-structural, not automatic P membership.** A polynomial-time test for OPT-EXTEND would make optimal-play reachability an efficiently checkable *property* of a given reached prefix (given `n,H`), supporting succinct *completeness* arguments for reachable optimal traces in a way enumerative frontier arguments cannot (cf. winning-frontier barrier excludes full enumeration, not selective verification). However, because the threshold is `S(n)` (an exact global value, unknown in general), this does **not** by itself place `SORT-BOUND-U` in NP/P: an NP-style certificate would still need to witness/verify the global optimal height and the full optimal strategy structure, and optimal trees may be exponentially large (problem.md). Distinguish **soundness** of a local test (`F(P(H)) ≤ S(n)-d(H)` implies the suffix can be optimal if the prefix depth is respected) from **completeness/effectiveness** (a poly-time procedure that decides it for all `n,H` without an infeasible oracle).
3. **Equivalence pressure / why disproof is hard.** OPT-EXTEND subsumes the exact optimum at the root: for the empty history `H_ε`, extendability to height `k` is `S(n) ≤ k`, and extendability to height `S(n)` is tautological but deciding the threshold family is the central problem. More sharply, any uniform exact method that maintains/recognizes optimal play must, on reachable prefixes, separate `F(P(H)) ≤ S(n)-d(H)` from `> S(n)-d(H)`; thus a general poly-time OPT-EXTEND oracle would be an efficiently checkable optimality-maintenance predicate for all antichain-reachable states, a strong structural capability not known to follow from or imply standard class separations except through `S(n)`-evaluation power.

- `GEN-SORT` / arbitrary-poset `S(P)` is **not** the same family: inputs here are antichain-reachable histories (a special, structured subfamily). Upper/lower bounds do not transfer both ways (hardness-boundaries Lemma 1.1/1.2/1.3).
- **Hyafil–Rivest 1976** (arbitrary subset tests, NP-complete decision trees) does **not** imply hardness of pairwise-comparison OPT-EXTEND/`S(P)` restrictions.
- **Cardinal et al. 2013** give polynomial-time *constant-factor* (information-bound-approximate) comparison strategies for sorting under partial information, **not** exact `S(P)` or exact threshold/extendability certification; exact `S(P)` can exceed `log₂ e(P)` (e.g. `S(12)=30>29`).
- **Brightwell–Winkler (#P-completeness of counting linear extensions)** concerns `e(P)`, not `F(P)`; `e(P)` is sound for information lower bounds but incomplete for exact minimax height (and `F(P)` may be larger). - **Mahaney/sparsity** constrains `SORT-BOUND-U` (tally/sparse) against routine many-one NP-hardness, not a proof of membership; circuit/nonuniform barriers irrelevant for uniform time here (problem.md). Unary `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` (budgeted DFS) is known; binary is a different encoding.
