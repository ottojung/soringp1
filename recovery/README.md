# SoringP1 research archive

The project is suspended. This archive preserves its research knowledge,
including unsuccessful approaches, counterexamples, corrections, unfinished
proofs, experimental code and historical discussions. It is now part of the
default branch. The established Lean library is unchanged; archived claims
and successful agent exits are not mathematical certificates.

## Boundaries of recovery

The archive stores recoverable research text and source, not every transient
artifact. Identical baseline files, caches, binaries, hidden reasoning,
credential-like text and unrelated projects are excluded or redacted. A
large regenerable poset input was not copied; its generation procedure and
checksum are preserved with the experiment. The missing scratch-reference
list records 570 paths that did not exist at the cited locations; these
are not necessarily 570 distinct lost results. Fourteen have candidate
archive filename matches, but identity could not be established.

All discovered SoringP1 commit objects in the audited stores are represented
by the canonical history or archived patches. The archive cannot establish
that already-deleted material, undiscovered material or missing scratch
files were recovered. No historical result was silently promoted to a
theorem, and no research agent was restarted.
