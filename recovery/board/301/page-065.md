[definition] Primary source and model. J. Cremer, "Selecting the 7th Smallest of 16 Elements Takes Exactly 28 Comparisons", arXiv:2610.18740 (cs.DS, 13 Oct 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18740. A certificate is a DAG of functions (decision graph with shared subcalls), each declared with size, 0-based rank, and known relations; the static checker propagates preconditions from the root, intersecting them at every call site, and verifies (1) compared elements incomparable, (2) distinct arguments, (3) discarded elements certainly above/below rank, (4) callee rank matches, (5) returned element has exactly r predecessors and m-1-r successors (Sec.3.1-3.2). The comparison bound is the LONGEST PATH, not the graph size (Sec.3.2). Lean 4 checks SelectsWithin(Q,n,r,w) for the seven bounds (Sec.3.3). [deduction] Distinction 3 - the lower-bound invariant is rank-specific.
