Front minimum-split-imbalance-root (beta(n) = min over optimal strategies T of max(F(T_lt),F(T_gt))/(F(T_root)-1) at the n-element antichain root): the front as literally stated is DEGENERATE at the antichain root under both natural readings of F; a nontrivial reformulation is needed before an all-n claim is falsifiable.

1. Reading F = minimax comparison cost (as in Minimax.lean / scripts): any single relation added to the antichain is cost-optimal and gives F(child) = F(antichain_n) - 1, so the ratio is identically 1 for every n and every first move. Verified numerically n=3..6 (minimax_values + linear search); hence beta(n)=1 trivially and no separation beta(n)<1 can exist at the root. The question reduces to nothing.

2. Reading F = e(P) (linear extension count): by the S_n symmetry of the antichain, every first comparison splits exactly n!/2 - 1 vs n!/2 extensions, so max(e_lt,e_gt)/(e_root - 1) = (n!/2)/(n!-1) -> 1/2 for all n. Also trivial; verified n=3..10.

3. Nontrivial reformulation tested (the only reading where the front content survives): among COST-OPTIMAL first moves pick the most extension-balanced one, let the adversary take the worse branch, and ask whether the worst e-branch ratio along an optimal path is forced toward 1 as n grows. Traces from the antichain root for n=5,6,7 (full minimax state enumeration): early depths stay ~0.5 (n=7: 0.50010, 0.50020, 0.50040, 0.50079 at depths 0-3, rising to 0.535-0.6 at mid depths 4-10). The ratio reaches 1.0 only at the FINAL nontrivial ply (fP=2, eP=4), which is a boundary artifact of near-total orders (3-4 extensions left), not an all-n family; states with forced e-ratio 1 exist (e.g. n=5 poset succ=(30,24,8,0,8), eP=3, fP=2) but lie on no cost-optimal path from the root in these traces.
Experiment-conjecture (n<=7): on cost-optimal root paths the max e-imbalance stays bounded away from 1, with ratio-1 plies only at the last one or two before a total order. No counterexample family and no proof; n>=8 full enumeration exceeds available compute (minimax state enumeration is exponential).

Obstacle/next: the front statement needs a sharpened definition of "optimal strategies" and of whether the ratio is over cost-optimal trees only; as written, neither branch of the falsifier (construct beta<1, or prove beta -> 1) can be stated nontrivially at the antichain root. Recommend restating the front as the worst extension-imbalance along cost-optimal root paths over depths 0..S(n)-2, which is nontrivial, all-n, and untouched by the degeneracies above.
