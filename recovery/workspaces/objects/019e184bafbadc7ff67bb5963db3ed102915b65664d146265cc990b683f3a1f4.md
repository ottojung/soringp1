## 1. Precise definitions

All posets are finite strict partial orders on labelled distinct keys (comparison-sorting states, `docs/problem.md`, `docs/upper-bound.md`).

- **Linear extensions.** `e(P)` = number of linear extensions of `P`.
- **Minimax cost.** `F(P)=0` if `P` is total, else `F(P)=1 + min_{x‖y} max(F(P+x<y), F(P+y<x))`. `S(n)=F(A_n)` where `A_n` is the `n`-antichain.
- **Information bound.** `I(P)=ceil(log2 e(P))` (with `log2 0` irrelevant). Node/leaf counting gives `F(P) >= I(P)`.
- **Information defect (defect measure).** `δ(P) = F(P) - I(P) ∈ Z_{>=0}`. (For global `n`, `d(n)=S(n)-ceil(log2 n!)` is the same measure at the root; `δ` generalizes to intermediate states arising in sorting strategies.)
- **Disjoint union (parallel composition).** For `P` on disjoint `A∪B`, `P = A ‖ B` iff every cross pair is incomparable and restrictions are `A,B`.
- **Component reversal.** `R_A(A ‖ B) = A^op ‖ B`, where `A^op` reverses every relation internal to `A`. (Well-defined as a poset operation on a presented disjoint-union decomposition; for a fixed `P` we consider any decomposition into nonempty parallel components when relevant.)
- **Posets arising in optimal sorting strategies.** Any `P` on the minimax game tree (reachable from `A_n` by legal comparison outcomes), in particular those on some height-`S(n)` optimal strategy/certificate path. The algebraic identities below hold for *all* disjoint-union states; reachability is discussed in §4.

## 2. Falsifiable question

> **Q (all `n`, all disjoint-union states `P=A‖B` on `n` keys).** Does component reversal admit a nontrivial, uniform defect bound relating `δ(A^op ‖ B)` to `δ(A ‖ B)` that is independent of (or strictly stronger progress than) the value-invariance question `F(A^op ‖ B)=F(A ‖ B)` — e.g. an inequality `|δ(A^op ‖ B)-δ(A ‖ B)| <= g(n)` with a consequence for bounding/compressing/certifying `S(n)`?

"Consequence if true" (examples): a bounded defect distortion would yield a transferable lower/upper control on `F` across reversed components; a uniform zero defect distortion would be a symmetry usable for state/certificate compression beyond known cases.

## 3. Claim (deduction) — reduction to the open `F`-symmetry; no independent defect leverage

**Claim.** For every disjoint union `P=A‖B` with `|A|=a,|B|=b, a+b=n`,
1. `e(A^op ‖ B)=e(A ‖ B)` and `I(A^op ‖ B)=I(A ‖ B)`.
2. `δ(A^op ‖ B) - δ(A ‖ B) = F(A^op ‖ B) - F(A ‖ B)`.
3. Therefore any "defect bound" under component reversal is equivalent to the corresponding bound on `F` under component reversal: in particular `δ` is invariant iff `F` is invariant, and the information/defect term provides **no compensating or independent degree of freedom**.
4. (Corollary, using prior theorem) If `B` is self-dual up to relabelling, `δ(A^op ‖ B)=δ(A ‖ B)` (by `docs/research-parallel-duality.md` Thm 1 / Cor. 2 transported through (2)).
5. (Obstruction to `S(n)`/certificate progress) Component-reversal defect measures do not yield an independent route to improved bounds or succinct certificates for `S(n)`: (i) nontrivial disjoint-union reversal does not act nontrivially on the root `A_n` in a way that changes `S(n)=F(A_n)` except trivial singleton permutations; (ii) any compression/certificate quotient based on identifying `P` with `R_A(P)` requires `F`-preservation as a necessary condition, which by (2) is exactly the same as defect-preservation and is exactly the unresolved general symmetry (open for general non-selfdual complement, no sound state quotient established).

**Status:** (1)-(3),(5i) **deduction** from definitions/standard combinatorics; (4) **deduction** conditional on cited prior theorem; (5ii) **deduction/obstruction** (necessary-condition argument). No new asymptotic bound, no complexity separation.

## 4. Argument

**Proof of (1).** A linear extension of `A‖B` is a shuffle of a linear extension of `A` and one of `B` preserving each internal order: choose positions for `A` in `C(a+b,a)` ways and order `A,B` internally in `e(A)e(B)` ways, so `e(A‖B)=C(a+b,a)e(A)e(B)` (standard; also the identity checked in `scripts/parallel_module_probe.py` and used in `docs/research-structural-lower-bounds.md`). Reversal is an anti-automorphism on `A`: `π ↦ π^rev_on_A` bijects linear extensions of `A` with those of `A^op`, hence `e(A^op)=e(A)`. Substituting gives `e(A^op‖B)=C(a+b,a)e(A^op)e(B)=e(A‖B)`. Taking `ceil(log2 ·)` yields `I(A^op‖B)=I(A‖B)`. ∎

**Proof of (2),(3).** By definition, `δ(A^op‖B)-δ(A‖B)= (F(A^op‖B)-I(A^op‖B)) - (F(A‖B)-I(A‖B)) = F(A^op‖B)-F(A‖B)` using (1). Hence `δ` is invariant ⇔ `F` is invariant, and any inequality on one side transfers verbatim to the other. No cancellation or tightening can come from `I/e`. ∎

**Proof of (4).** `docs/research-parallel-duality.md` Theorem 1: if `B^op ≅ B` then `F(A^op‖B)=F(A‖B)`. Apply (2). ∎

**Argument for (5).** (i) `S(n)=F(A_n)`. `A_n` is a disjoint union of `n` singletons; reversing any singleton (or any sub-antichain component, up to relabelling) yields an isomorphic antichain, so no nontrivial `F`/`δ` information about `S(n)` is produced at the root. (ii) A "defect-based" certificate/state reduction that merges `P` with `R_A(P)` is sound only if it preserves the quantity needed by the minimax/certificate objective; for exact `F` (hence for optimal height and for exact defect) this requires `F(R_A(P))=F(P)`, i.e. by (2) `δ`-invariance, which is the already-open general parallel reversal symmetry. A looser defect bound without `F` equality cannot certify exact optimality `S(n)` nor a tight defect accounting. Therefore the defect framing does not create a new independent sufficient condition: it reduces to the unresolved value/quotient problem. ∎

## 5. Falsification attempts

- **Algebraic falsification of "independent defect term":** tried to find room for `I` to offset `F` changes. Falsified by (1): `I` is identically invariant under component reversal for all `n,a,b,A,B` (bijection on linear extensions). No parameter regime exists where defect moves independently of `F`.
- **Small-`n`/computational falsification of invariance:** `docs/research-parallel-duality.md` reports exhaustive isomorphism-class checks for `n ≤ 7` (1048 pairs at `n=7`, 612 restricted, 0 restricted violations, 0 general violations, 0 `e`/block violations; see its table, "experiment"). Since `δ` differs iff `F` differs, these are equally 0 violations for `δ`. I did not re-run the probe (would require importing repo scripts with their dependencies/runtime); I treat these as **prior experiment in-repo** consistent with (2), not as a proof.
- **Falsification by `S(n)` consequence:** checked root case `A_n` — component reversal gives no nontrivial state/invariant (5i), so cannot falsify-or-support a route to `S(n)` except vacuously. Consistent with obstruction, not a progress route.
- **What would falsify the obstruction claim (5ii)?** Exhibiting either (a) an explicit general proof that `F(A^op‖B)=F(A‖B)` for all disjoint unions (would turn the "defect/`F` symmetry" route positive, collapsing it to that theorem) or (b) a certificate/compression method using reversal classes that is sound for exact `S(n)` despite non-invariant `F` on some class (would contradict the necessary-condition argument; seems impossible for exact minimax height but would refute (5ii) if found). Neither is produced here.

## 6. Viability assessment

- **Viable as a proved structural fact:** yes for (1)-(4) — component reversal preserves `e,I` and reduces defect variation exactly to `F` variation (a clean, rigorous relation; the self-dual-complement defect invariance follows immediately from the prior theorem). Worth stating when reasoning about defect/slack on parallel modules.
- **Not a viable independent route to progress on `S(n)` / certificates / algorithm complexity:** the defect measure adds no leverage beyond the open general parallel component-reversal symmetry of `F` and no nontrivial action at the root. Any positive consequence for exact evaluation or succinct optimal certificates would require settling (or circumventing, which (2) obstructs for exact objectives) that same unresolved symmetry/state-quotient question. In particular it does not shrink the exponent, improve space, produce an NP/coNP certificate, or explain the bound gap by itself.
- **Concrete obstruction/counterexample status:** no counterexample to defect invariance found (and none possible in checked range given prior `F` data); the **concrete obstruction** demonstrated here is the **equivalence/reduction** `Δδ = ΔF` with `ΔI ≡ 0` + no nontrivial root action, which shows defect-bounding cannot be an easier or independent target than `F`-invariance for this front.
- **Recommended disposition:** treat "defect under component-reversal in disjoint unions" as subsumed by the open parallel-reversal `F`-symmetry question (`docs/research-parallel-duality.md` §3/next hypotheses). If pursued further, the decisive step is proving/refuting general `F(A^op‖B)=F(A‖B)` or a sound equivariant state quotient — not further defect accounting.
