## Overall assessment

The note is **cautious and mostly model-faithful**. It frames the question as the *form* of possible short existential certificates, states “no complexity-class separation claims,” and repeatedly distinguishes open certificate existence from `SORT-BOUND-U ∉ NP`. No unconditional separation or NP-membership claim is made.

I found **no critical model error** that invalidates the main obstruction framing, but there are **minor sourcing/precision issues** and a few places where claims should be tightened or attributed to the cited sub-notes rather than restated as standalone fact.

## 4. Complexity-class / NP-membership overclaims

- **No separation claimed.** The header states it makes no complexity-class separation claims and lists `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP`, `∉ NP`, `NP = PSPACE`, `NP ≠ PSPACE` explicitly. §7 and §9 repeat this.
- **No NP membership claimed.** It states “not an NP existential witness of polynomial size” for game transcripts and treats succinct exact-height certificates as open.
- **Critical caveat present.** §7 L103 correctly notes `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` is already known and an NP lower bound for it would imply `NP ≠ PSPACE`; therefore current obstructions must not be read as proof outside NP. This matches `AGENTS.md`/`problem.md` realism constraints.
- **No `P`, `NP`, `coNP`, hardness claims.** The note does not assert membership or hardness beyond citing known upper bounds contextually.

**Overclaim conclusion:** **Pass.** The note avoids the prohibited complexity-class conclusions.
