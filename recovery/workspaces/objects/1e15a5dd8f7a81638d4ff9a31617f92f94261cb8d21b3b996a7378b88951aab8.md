## 1. Summary judgment

The artifact provides an explicit JSON oracle-trace format, a mechanical checker with recomputed exhaustive budgeted-DFS oracle constraints, and small replayable examples that the checker accepts. `[observation, replay]` It does **not** present a full `G(12,29)` refuter trace or a full `G(12,30)` height-30 yes trace; it reports `n=12` as blocked and gives measured/resource and structural reasons. `[observation]` It does **not** make a separation, NP, coNP, or PSPACE claim. `[observation]` The main audit risks are **semantic over-reading**: an accepted oracle-root trace is a checked lemma-carrying object, not a total-coverage explicit strategy/certificate; and the blocked `G(12,29)` trace is accepted as structurally valid but is explicitly not a proof/certificate of `S(12)>29`. `[deduction, risk]`

## 2. Oracle-trace format/checker claims

### 2.1 Format
`check_oracle_trace.py` documents and consumes JSON with `kind` (`refuter`/`sorter`), `n`, `k`, `root`, and `nodes` keyed by id. Node fields include `state` (transitive-closure successor bitmasks), `budget`, `type` (`internal`/`leaf`/`oracle`), and kind-specific edges/rules/oracle payloads. `[observation]`

State representation is plausible for the model: each `state[i]` is a bitmask of successors `j` with `i < j` or `i > j` represented by bit positions, checked by `tc_ok` for self-loops, antisymmetry, and transitivity. `[observation]` The fragment example matches `budgeted_dfs.extend(antichain(4),0,1) -> [2,0,0,0]`. `[replay]`

### 2.2 Mechanical checks claimed/performed
The checker validates, as applicable: TC/antisymmetry (`tc_ok`), root antichain/budget/TC/non-total/total constraints, `tc_edge` child equality and budget decrement, refuter `covers_all_incomparable` and internal total move coverage over incomparable pairs, sorter comparable-pair rejection and both `lt/gt` TC children, and leaf semantics. `[observation]`

For exhaustive oracle lemmas, `exhaustive_can_sort` / `exhaustive_cannot_sort` call `budgeted_dfs.can_sort` and reject on node-cap hit or value mismatch. `[observation]` This is materially stronger than trusting a raw search transcript: the verifier recomputes the bounded exhaustive claim. `[deduction]`

### 2.3 Checker limitations / audit caveats
- **No global reachability pruning requirement:** the checker iterates all nodes in `doc["nodes"]` and validates them; it does not require that every node is reachable from `root` except indirectly through structure the author chose. Unreachable junk nodes could still be checked; not observed as exploited here. `[risk]`
- **Oracle nodes do not imply total strategy coverage:** a refuter `oracle` node is accepted if its listed constraints pass. It is not required to contain `rule` entries for all incomparable pairs. This is appropriate for lemma-carrying fragments but means `ACCEPT refuter ... nodes=1` must not be read as “complete explicit refuter strategy”. `[deduction, risk]`
- **Refuter `leaf` requires `budget==0` and non-total:** this is stricter than “adversary wins by forcing failure later” unless failure is encoded structurally; internal/oracle representations carry most of the interesting cases. `[observation]`
- **Exhaustive oracle is only as strong as `budgeted_dfs.can_sort`:** the audit reran the checker successfully on provided traces and spot-checked `can_sort` for `G(4,4)`/`G(4,5)`, but did not formally verify `budgeted_dfs` against the Lean/complete model. `[replay, risk]`
- **`tc_edge` checks the stated move/child but `covers_all_incomparable` is a separate constraint:** in `g4k4_refuter_fragment.json`, root lists only the `tc_edge` to `c01`; it does not claim total root coverage. That matches the artifact text (“fragment”, total coverage not materialized). `[observation]`

## 4. Resource measurements

The artifact reports measured budgeted-DFS node-cap behavior (exact for small `G(4,4)`, `G(5,6)`; cap hits for `G(6,9)`, `G(7,12)`, and `G(12,30)` probes) and explains why exhaustive replay is infeasible for `n=12`. `[observation]` This audit did **not** independently rerun the large timing/RSS table; it only reran small checks. The measurements are presented as feasibility evidence, not as a complexity proof. `[observation, risk]`

Derived/combinatorial blockers are used appropriately as context: root has `C(12,2)=66` incomparable pairs `[deduction]`; refuter total-coverage semantics and explicit/DAG blowup are cited from prior/sibling measurements and literature-scale state counts. These should be read as reported blockers, not as this audit’s independent full reproduction. `[observation]`

## 5. Raw search output vs. short certificate

The artifact explicitly requires that oracle claims be stated checkable lemmas/constraints and not raw search output treated as a certificate. `[observation]` The implemented mechanism follows that discipline for the exhaustive cases by recomputing `budgeted_dfs.can_sort` under a node cap; on cap hit it rejects, and for the blocked `G(12,29)` object it omits the exhaustive cannot-sort claim. `[observation, deduction]`

However, root-only traces whose only game-theoretic content is a recomputed exhaustive DFS are **not short certificates** in the complexity sense; they are bounded exhaustive rechecks. The artifact appropriately frames small examples as replayable trace-format demonstrations/fragments, not as succinct `n=12` certificates. `[deduction]`

No raw solver/search dump is accepted on faith by the checked examples reviewed here. `[observation]`

## 6. Separation / NP / complexity-claim check

No separation claim (`P != PSPACE`, `NP != PSPACE`, `P != NP`, etc.) and no NP/coNP/PSPACE membership-or-hardness claim for `SORT-BOUND-U`/`S(12)` is made in the audited note. `[observation]` The conclusion states “No separation/NP claim. This is an obstruction/feasibility report…” which matches AGENTS/problem guardrails. `[observation]`

**Bottom line:** the s12k30 oracle-trace artifact is a reasonable feasibility/trace-format prototype: it makes its format explicit, provides a replayable checker that recomputes bounded exhaustive lemmas, and clearly marks the `G(12,29)` case as blocked rather than certified. Consumers should not interpret accepted root-oracle or blocked-structural traces as succinct complete refuter/sorter certificates, and any future `n=12` claim would require either total-coverage explicit/checkable strategy objects or independently verified stronger lemmas under a clearly stated proof system.
