## 0. Verdict

`[deduction,falsification]` **Neither route (a) nor route (b) is discharged here.**

- **Route (a), restricted domain:** every natural candidate for `D_{n,k}` either fails the `|D_{n,k}| <= |W|^r` capacity bound for fixed `r` or lacks a proof that it is sufficient for alternating-acceptance obligations (2)–(4). In particular, the full valid state space, the reachable state space, and the all-labelled-poset projection are exponentially large; the acceptance-relevant/optimal-play domain has no proved polynomial bound (§4). No explicit `Code/Decode` with a discharged sufficiency proof is produced.
- **Route (b), standard Vardi/AV normal form:** the local `M_G/G(n,k)` formulas over `N_G` are explicit and fixed-arity (§2, §3). A standard FO(PFP)=PSPACE capture *exists in principle* for the PSPACE property `SORT-BOUND-U` on ordered `A(w)` (`[literature]`, Vardi 1982; Abiteboul–Vianu 1991; plus Claim A). However, a *concrete instantiation for local `M_G` formulas with complete simulation/correspondence proof* requires either (i) a direct fixed-arity global encoding of alternating acceptance over `N_G` configurations, or (ii) an explicit deterministic PSPACE simulation of `M_G` whose tape/head/state relations are defined from the local `M_G` formulas and whose FO(PFP) reachability/acceptance proof is carried out. Form (i) hits the same tuple-packing/second-order-over-configurations barrier unless configurations are represented as relations; form (ii) can represent a *single DTM configuration* as fixed-arity relations, but proving equivalence between that DTM and the *alternating* `M_G` acceptance defined by the local formulas is a nontrivial global simulation proof not completed here (§5).

**Single minimal failing lemma (isolated):**
> **[open:ALT-PACK] (Alternating acceptance under fixed-arity global coding).** There exist a fixed standard FO(PFP)/FO(IFP) normal form and fixed-arity relations/codes over `A(1^n#1^k)` such that, using only the local FO formulas `Init, YieldE, YieldA0, YieldA1, ConfigAcc, PostBudget, StepPos` of §3 as transition truth conditions, obligations (2)–(4) hold:
> (2) the intended initial `N_G` configuration is encoded;
> (3) encoded existential/universal moves preserve and reflect `YieldE/YieldA0/YieldA1`;
> (4) the global fixed point accepts the encoded initial configuration iff the alternating game `G(n,k)` is a sorter win iff `S(n)<=k`.
>
> Moreover, the proof must be complete for the alternating (existential–universal) acceptance semantics of `M_G`, not merely for existence of some PSPACE DTM deciding the same language.

This is narrower than an unqualified appeal to FO(PFP)=PSPACE but stronger than the discharged local layer: it requires a concrete, proved bridge from local `M_G` formulas to global alternating acceptance in a fixed syntactic normal form.

## 1. Fixed objects and local interface (retained)

`[definition]` Input `w=1^n#1^k`, `N=n+k+1`,
```text
A(w)=(W,<,One,Hash), W={1,...,N}, Hash={n+1}, One=W\{n+1}.
Key(x):=∃h(HashPos(h)∧x<h).      Ctr(t):=∃h(HashPos(h)∧(t=h∨h<t)).
Zero(t):=HashPos(t).             HasBudget(t):=∃s SuccCtr(t,s).
Φ_SORTU := WellFormed_w ∧ Φ_game.
```

`[definition]` `G(n,k)`: states are transitively closed strict partial orders `P` on `Key`; initial antichain; budget `k`. Sorter picks an incomparable pair (existential), adversary answers one orientation (universal), state becomes `TC(P∪{answer})`, budget decreases by 1. Sorter wins iff a total order is reached within `k` comparisons. `[literature,deduction]` `S(n)<=k` iff sorter wins `G(n,k)` (`docs/problem.md`, `research-certificates-barriers.md`).

`[definition]` `N_G` state signature (fixed arities, independent of `n,k`):
```text
Pos(u,v)   arity 2  : current strict partial order on Key
Used(t)    arity 1  : used-comparison marker in Ctr
Sel(a,b)   arity 2  : selected sorter pair in adversary mode
Ctrl(q)    arity 1  : finite control (QSort/QAdv/QAcc/QRej)
```
A semantic configuration is `C=(Pos,Used,Sel,Ctrl)` interpreting these relations over `A(w)$.

## 2. Discharged local layer (retained from prior artifacts)

The following are concrete FO formulas over `A(w)$ and `N_G$; they are the local truth conditions that any global instantiation must preserve/reflect.

`[definition]` Core predicates:
```text
ValidPos(Pos):=∀u∀v(Pos(u,v)->(Key(u)∧Key(v))) ∧ ∀x ¬Pos(x,x)
             ∧ ∀x∀y∀z((Pos(x,y)∧Pos(y,z))->Pos(x,z)).
Incomparable(Pos,x,y):=Key(x)∧Key(y)∧x!=y ∧ ¬Pos(x,y) ∧ ¬Pos(y,x).
Total(Pos):=∀x∀y((Key(x)∧Key(y)∧x!=y)->(Pos(x,y)∨Pos(y,x))).
ExactlyOneUsed(Used), NoSel(Sel), OneSel(Sel), ExactlyOneCtrl(Ctrl).
ValidConfig(Pos,Used,Sel,Ctrl).
```

`[definition]` Initial/terminal:
```text
Init(Pos,Used,Sel,Ctrl):=(∀u∀v ¬Pos(u,v))
  ∧ (∃t(Used(t)∧Zero(t)) ∧ ∀t∀s((Used(t)∧Used(s))->t=s))
  ∧ NoSel(Sel)
  ∧ ((Total(Pos)∧QAcc(Ctrl)) ∨ (¬Total(Pos)∧QSort(Ctrl))).
ConfigAcc:=QAcc(Ctrl)∨Total(Pos).   ConfigRej:=QRej(Ctrl).
```

`[definition]` Incremental transitive closure:
```text
StepPos(Pos,x,y;u,v):=Pos(u,v) ∨ ((u=x∨Pos(u,x)) ∧ (v=y∨Pos(y,v))).
```
`[deduction, proof obligation]` If `ValidPos(Pos)` and `Incomparable(Pos,x,y)$, `StepPos` gives `TC(Pos∪{(x,y)})` and preserves validity.

`[definition]` Local transitions:
```text
YieldE(Pos,Used,Sel,Ctrl;Pos',Used',Sel',Ctrl') := QSort(Ctrl) ∧ ¬Total(Pos)
  ∧ (∃t(Used(t)∧HasBudget(t)))
  ∧ (∃a∃b(Sel'(a,b)∧Incomparable(Pos,a,b)∧∀x∀y(Sel'(x,y)->(x=a∧y=b))))
  ∧ Pos'=Pos ∧ Used'=Used ∧ QAdv(Ctrl').

PostBudget(Pos',Used',Ctrl') := (Total(Pos')∧QAcc(Ctrl'))
  ∨ (¬Total(Pos')∧∃s(Used'(s)∧HasBudget(s))∧QSort(Ctrl'))
  ∨ (¬Total(Pos')∧¬∃s(Used'(s)∧HasBudget(s))∧QRej(Ctrl')).

YieldA0(...):=QAdv(Ctrl)∧∃a∃b(Sel(a,b)∧Incomparable(Pos,a,b))
  ∧ (∀u∀v(Pos'(u,v)<->StepPos(Pos,a,b;u,v)))
  ∧ (∃t∃s(Used(t)∧SuccCtr(t,s)∧Used'(s)∧∀r(Used'(r)->r=s)))
  ∧ NoSel(Sel') ∧ PostBudget(Pos',Used',Ctrl').
YieldA1 analogous with StepPos(Pos,b,a;u,v).
```

`[deduction, proof obligation]` **Local correctness target.** From `Init`, legal `YieldE/YieldA0/YieldA1` steps preserve `ValidConfig`; each full comparison consumes exactly one budget unit; `PostBudget` accepts any total state within budget and rejects exhausted non-total states. Consequently the local alternating move relation is faithful to `G(n,k)$.

## 3. Semantic alternating acceptance operator

`[definition]` For semantic `N_G$ configurations `C$ and configuration-set variable `AC$:
```text
C ∈ T(AC) iff ConfigAcc(C)
  ∨ (QSort(C) ∧ ∃C'(YieldE(C,C') ∧ C'∈AC))
  ∨ (QAdv(C)  ∧ ∃C0,C1(YieldA0(C,C0)∧YieldA1(C,C1)∧C0∈AC∧C1∈AC)).
```
`[deduction]` `T$ is monotone in `AC$. Every legal play is finite (budget decreases on universal phases), so the least fixed point `LFP(T)$ exists and captures alternating acceptance:
```text
Init(C0) ∧ C0 ∈ LFP(T)   iff   sorter wins G(n,k)   iff   S(n)<=k.
```
`[caution]` This is a semantic operator over configurations. It is **not** by itself a fixed FO(LFP)/FO(PFP) sentence over the word structure `A(w)$, and it implies no membership in `P`, `FO(LFP)$ on the bare word, `NP`, or `coNP$.

## 4. Route (a): restricted coding domain `D_{n,k}`

Route (a) asks for a restricted coding domain `D_{n,k}$ of configurations sufficient for obligations (2)–(4), with explicit `Code/Decode$ and proof/size bound showing sufficiency and `W^r$ capacity.

### 4.1 Requirements

A route-(a) discharge would need:
- A fixed arity `r$ and explicit FO (or fixed PFP-compatible) maps
  ```text
  Code(C;z̄),  z̄∈W^r,  C∈D_{n,k},
  DecodePos/DecodeUsed/DecodeSel/DecodeCtrl(z̄,...)
  ```
  or an equivalent representation with proved faithfulness on `D_{n,k}`.
- **Capacity:** `|D_{n,k}| <= |W|^r = N^r$ (or a directly equivalent bounded-index argument).
- **Obligation (2), initial sufficiency:** the intended `Init$ configuration is in `D_{n,k}$ and has the intended code.
- **Obligation (3), transition sufficiency:** for all `C∈D_{n,k}$ relevant to the acceptance computation, every `YieldE$ witness used by an accepting existential move is represented (or the coding preserves the existential choice), and both `YieldA0,YieldA1$ successors are in/represented by `D_{n,k}$ with faithful decoding.
- **Obligation (4), acceptance sufficiency/soundness/completeness:** the global PFP acceptance computation restricted to codes of `D_{n,k}$ accepts the initial code iff `C0∈LFP(T)$ iff sorter wins `G(n,k)$.

### 4.2 Candidate domains and status

| Candidate `D_{n,k}` | Capacity `<=N^r` for fixed `r`? | Sufficient for (2)–(4)? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| All valid `N_G$ configurations | No: poset component alone is `A001035(n)=2^{Θ(n^2)}$ | Yes by definition | `[falsification]` (prior `[open:PACK-REPR]`) |
| All `G(n,k)$-reachable configurations from `Init$ | No in general: reachability can generate many partial orders; not bounded by polynomial in `N$ | Yes by definition | `[open/fails capacity likely]` no bound proved; counting not ruled polynomial here only by A001035, but no polynomial bound known |
| All configurations on any accepting run (if accepting) | Unknown; strategy-dependent, may still be large | Insufficient unless all adversary branches covered | `[open]` existential strategy is insufficient for universal `YieldA0/YieldA1` completeness |
| All configurations on some fixed optimal/accepting strategy tree, all adversary branches | Unknown; full strategy tree has `2^{O(k)}$ leaves but internal states up to `2^{O(n^2)}$ kinds; optimal-play distinct-state count is open | Would be sufficient if fixed | `[open]` no polynomial bound; `research-certificates-barriers.md` §8 lists optimal-play state count as open |
| Bounded-width / interval / dimension-2 / N-free / series-parallel posets, etc. | Some classes are small or structured; not asserted here | No: adversary can force arbitrary labelled posets consistent with comparisons; restricting `Pos$ breaks completeness of `G(n,k)$ unless closure proved | `[falsification target]` not sufficient for exact `S(n)$ |
| Configurations whose `Pos$ is an antichain/chain/near-threshold local pattern | polynomially many patterns possible only with strong invariant | No known invariant preserved by arbitrary adversary answers in exact `G(n,k)$ | `[open]` no proof |

### 4.3 Why the obvious restriction is insufficient

`[deduction]` The universal branch is the hard constraint. Obligation (4) requires that if control is `QAdv$ and the selected pair is `(a,b)$, acceptance holds only when **both** `YieldA0$ and `YieldA1$ successors are accepting. Therefore a restricted domain cannot merely include a favorable existential branch; it must include every adversary successor reached by the strategy being certified, and the coding/proof must show that no accepting adversary-closed strategy escapes the domain.

A domain `D_{n,k}$ consisting of “all adversary-closed descendants of `Init` under some accepting strategy” is semantically sufficient if such a strategy exists, but:
1. it is defined relative to the very acceptance predicate being decided;
2. its size is exactly the succinct-certificate/optimal-play compression question, which is open (`research-certificates-barriers.md` Claims C, §8);
3. giving explicit `Code/Decode$ over that strategy-relative domain requires a uniform, FO/PFP-definable description of the domain or a proof that a fixed polynomial-capacity domain always suffices.

No such description or proof is supplied.

### 4.4 Route (a) conclusion

`[open]` Route (a) is **not discharged**. The total form is falsified by capacity. No restricted `D_{n,k}$ has been given with explicit `Code/Decode`, a proved size bound `|D_{n,k}|<=N^r$, and proved sufficiency for obligations (2)–(4). The minimal missing ingredient inside route (a) is:

> **[open:DOMAIN-SUFF]** An explicit, FO/PFP-definable (or otherwise fixed) family `D_{n,k} ⊆ N_G$ with `|D_{n,k}| <= N^{O(1)}$ (or fixed `r$) such that `Init∈D_{n,k}` and alternating acceptance of `G(n,k)$ is sound and complete when restricted to `D_{n,k}$.

This is at least as strong as a uniform succinct alternating-certificate normal form and is open.

## 5. Route (b): standard Vardi/AV global FO(PFP)/IFP capture normal form

Route (b) asks for a different standard global FO(PFP)/IFP capture normal form (Vardi/AV) instantiated concretely for local `M_G` formulas with complete simulation/correspondence proof.

### 5.1 Standard theorem (literature boundary)

`[literature]` Vardi (1982) proved that FO with least fixed point captures P on ordered finite structures; with partial fixed point (or inflationary fixed point, equivalent in expressive power on finite structures in the relevant sense; FO(PFP)=FO(IFP)=PSPACE on ordered structures in the standard descriptive complexity presentation — see Abiteboul–Vianu 1991; Immerman 1986/1999; Ebbinghaus–Flum) it captures PSPACE on ordered finite structures. Chandra–Kozen–Stockmeyer (1981): APTIME=PSPACE. Claim A gives `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE`.

`[deduction]` Therefore there exists *some* FO(PFP) sentence defining `SORT-BOUND-U$ over ordered word structures of the appropriate form. **This note does not claim that this existence theorem, by itself, instantiates the local `M_G` formulas with a complete project-specific simulation/correspondence proof.** Route (b) requires that instantiation.

### 5.2 Concrete DTM-configuration normal form (Vardi/AV style)

`[definition]` The standard PSPACE-capture normal form represents a DTM configuration whose tape has `N^r$ cells by fixed-arity relations:
```text
Head(x̄), State_q, Sym_a(x̄),   x̄=(x1,...,xr)∈W^r,
```
with lexicographic successor `LexSucc(x̄,ȳ)$ FO-definable from `<$. One DTM step is FO: read `Sym_a(x̄)$ and `State_q` at `Head(x̄)$, write `Sym_{a'}(x̄)$, move head to lex predecessor/successor, update state. Acceptance of a polynomial-space DTM can be expressed by FO(PFP) as reachability of an accepting configuration (or by the standard stage/closure construction over the configuration graph encoded through these relations).

**Why this helps with representation but not directly with route (b):** a *single* DTM configuration over `N^r$ tape cells is encoded by relations of fixed arity `r$ over `W$ — this is unlike packing an arbitrary `Pos$ relation (arity 2, content `2^{Θ(n^2)}$ in general, but representable as a relation value) into a tuple name. In fact `Pos,Used,Sel,Ctrl$ are already fixed-arity relations over `W$ and can be present as relation variables/state relations in a simultaneous PFP. The obstacle is not representing one `N_G$ state; it is representing the **alternating acceptance predicate over all relevant `N_G` states/configuration transitions** in a fixed PFP syntax and proving its correspondence to `M_G$.

### 5.3 Direct alternating acceptance normal form (attempt)

A direct instantiation would use simultaneous PFP over state relations plus an acceptance component, e.g. schematically
```text
[PFP_{AccState, State} Δ(AccState,State,Pos,Used,Sel,Ctrl,...)](InitArgs)
```
with `Δ$ positive in its fixed-point variables and encoding:
```text
AccState(C-state) if ConfigAcc(C-state)
  or QSort ∧ ∃ move to C' with AccState(C')
  or QAdv  ∧ both YieldA0,YieldA1 successors have AccState.
```
But standard PFP binds relations of fixed tuple arity. To make `AccState(C-state)$ a fixed-arity relation over domain tuples, `C-state$ must be encoded as a tuple. Encoding the *name* of a whole relation interpretation `Pos$ as a tuple returns to `[open:PACK-REPR]`. Encoding `AccState$ not as a table of configuration names but as an evolving global property of the current state relations is a different semantics (it would track one computation, not all alternating descendants).

Thus the direct form splits into:
- **Tuple-named alternating graph acceptance:** blocked by capacity for all states; restricted-domain version is route (a), open.
- **State-relation evolution:** expresses deterministic/local evolution, not the full universal–existential acceptance closure unless augmented by a proved encoding of alternating branches.

### 5.4 Deterministic PSPACE simulation of `M_G` (attempt)

By APTIME=PSPACE, `M_G$ has an equivalent polynomial-space DTM `M_D$. A Vardi/AV instantiation can be carried out for `M_D$ *if* `M_D$ is explicitly constructed:
1. Choose `r$ with `N^r$ bounding `M_D$ space.
2. Define FO formulas for initial tape/head/state of `M_D$ on `A(w)$.
3. Define FO transition formulas for one `M_D$ step using lex successor on `W^r$.
4. Define FO(PFP) reachability of an accepting halting configuration.
5. Prove: `A(w)$ satisfies the resulting sentence iff `M_D$ accepts iff `M_G$ accepts iff `S(n)<=k`.

Steps 1–4 are the standard Vardi/AV capture pattern and are concrete *given* an explicit `M_D$. Step 5's final equivalence to `S(n)<=k$ is discharged if the equivalence `M_D accepts iff M_G accepts$ is proved. The project artifact supplies explicit local formulas for `M_G/G(n,k)$ (§2 above), but **does not supply an explicit polynomial-space deterministic simulation `M_D$ derived from those formulas**, nor a proof that such `M_D$ preserves `M_G$ acceptance. The APTIME=PSPACE theorem supplies existence of `M_D$, but using that existence without instantiating `M_D$ against the local `M_G$ formulas leaves the route-(b) request (complete simulation/correspondence proof instantiated concretely for local `M_G$ formulas) incomplete.

### 5.5 Route (b) conclusion

`[open]` Route (b) is **not discharged**. The standard Vardi/AV normal form is the right global target, and the local `M_G$ layer is explicit, but the complete concrete instantiation is missing one of:
- an explicit fixed-arity alternating-acceptance encoding/proof for `M_G$ over `N_G$ (hits `[open:ALT-PACK]` / `[open:PACK-REPR]$ or needs a proved restriction), or
- an explicit deterministic PSPACE machine `M_D$ defined from the local `M_G$ formulas plus a complete `M_D ~ M_G ~ G(n,k)$ correspondence proof and the standard FO(PFP) DTM-reachability instantiation for that `M_D$.

## 6. Isolated minimal failing lemma

The smallest precise blocker, after retaining the explicit local layer in §2–§3, is:

> **[open:ALT-PACK]** Provide a fixed standard global FO(PFP)/FO(IFP) normal form over ordered `A(w)$ and a complete proof of obligations (2)–(4) for the alternating `M_G` acceptance of `G(n,k)$, using `Init, YieldE, YieldA0, YieldA1, ConfigAcc, StepPos, PostBudget$ from §2 as the local transition semantics.
>
> A discharge must include: (i) a fixed signature/arity choice; (ii) explicit initial-state formulas/relations; (iii) explicit one-step/move preservation-reflection for existential and both universal outcomes; (iv) explicit alternating acceptance fixed-point formula(s); (v) soundness and completeness proving acceptance of the encoded initial object iff sorter wins `G(n,k)$ iff `S(n)<=k$.

This isolates the failure away from local rules (already explicit), away from the false total tuple-packing claim (falsified), and away from an uninstantiated appeal to FO(PFP)=PSPACE.

## 7. Falsification targets

1. Any proposed `D_{n,k}$: exhibit either `|D_{n,k}|>N^r$ for the claimed fixed `r$, or a legal `YieldA0/YieldA1$ successor outside `D_{n,k}$ on an acceptance-relevant path, or an accepting `G(n,k)$ strategy with no representation in `D_{n,k}$.
2. Any tuple `Code(C;z̄)$ over all valid `Pos$: counting falsifier `A001035(n)=2^{Θ(n^2)} > N^r$.
3. Any DTM simulation `M_D$: show `M_D$ accepts/rejects differently from alternating acceptance of `M_G$ on some well-formed input, or show a local `Yield*` case not reflected.
4. Boundary cases: `n<=1$, `k=0$, total `Pos$ at initialization, `Used=MaxK$ with total/non-total result, `Zero=Hash$ not read as a key.
5. Overclaim check: no sentence here is asserted to place `SORT-BOUND-U$ in `P$, `FO(LFP)$ on the bare word, `NP$, or `coNP$.

## 8. Final assessment

The local alternating game/ATM layer for `SORT-BOUND-U$ is explicit as fixed FO formulas over `A(w)$ and fixed-arity `N_G$ state relations. The remaining gap is global: route (a) lacks a polynomial-capacity, acceptance-sufficient restricted coding domain with a proved `Code/Decode`; route (b) lacks a complete concrete Vardi/AV-style FO(PFP)/IFP instantiation that carries the local `M_G$ formulas through alternating acceptance to `S(n)<=k$. The single minimal failing lemma is `[open:ALT-PACK]`: a fixed global normal form plus complete obligations (2)–(4) for alternating `M_G$ acceptance.
