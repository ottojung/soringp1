## 1. Problem / baseline

- **Question (front `framed-dag-witness-n15-exact`, falsifiable/independent):** Does there exist an explicit, checkable permutation-framed strategy DAG certificate of exact height `S(15)=42` for `n=15`, and what is its concrete size/depth/profile compared to polynomial envelopes?
- **Model/certificate format:** permutation-framed strategy DAG as checked by `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py` (acyclic/topologically numbered nodes, root 0; each internal node compares a pair and has `lt`/`gt` edges carrying `next` and a slot permutation `perm`; leaves marked `{"leaf": true}` and must sort all pairs under propagated/intersected guaranteed strict partial orders). Soundness/checkability is polynomial in explicit graph size and `n` (checker). Polynomial-size completeness for optimal strategies at exact budget is **not** known (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`).
- **Known value:** `S(15)=42` (literature, e.g. Jing et al. as cited in `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`; repo notes also record `S(15)=42`). This is 1 comparison above the information-theoretic lower bound.
- **Reference bounds for `n=15`:**
  - Information bound: `ceil(log2(15!)) = 41`.
  - Ford–Johnson / merge-insertion upper bound: `F_FJ(15)=sum_{j=1}^{15} ceil(log2(3j/4)) = 42` (equals `S(15)` for this `n`; not equal for every `n` per frontier doc).
  - Standard balanced merge-sort worst-case comparisons (top-down recurrence `M(n)=M(⌊n/2⌋)+M(⌈n/2⌉)+n-1`): `45`.
  - Triangular/bubble path: `n(n-1)/2 = 105`.

### 3.2 Exact-height `S(15)=42` witness: not achieved

- **Outcome:** No explicit, checkable permutation-framed strategy DAG certificate of exact height 42 for `n=15` was constructed or derived in a checkable form.
- **Evidence/obstruction (precise, falsifiable):**
  1. **No generator for exact/FJ framed DAG in tooling:** `check_framed_strategy.py` provides only `--bubble` (quadratic-depth) generation; no Ford–Johnson/optimal framed-certificate generator exists. `scripts/upper_witness.py` implements Ford–Johnson counting/reference logic (literature algorithm) but does not emit the checker’s framed DAG JSON format.
  2. **Explicit optimal tree is infeasible:** any correct reduced comparison strategy distinguishing all permutations has `n!` leaves; for `n=15`, `15! = 1,307,674,368,000` leaves, so an explicit tree witness is not materializable. Single-policy hash-consed DAGs coincide with trees in observed small-`n` experiments (`docs/research-strategy-certificates.md`, Thm 2.6/Cor 2.7, `DAG(1)=2n!-1` for `n<=6`), giving no evidence of a compact exact single-policy DAG.
  3. **Known polynomial framed constructions do not reach 42:** frontier doc gives an all-`n` `O(n^2)`-node framed merge-sort construction with path length `O(n log n)` but notes balanced merge exceeds `log2(n!)` for large powers of two and cannot be exact-optimal in general; for `n=15` standard merge sort worst case is 45 (>42). Rank-envelope/Ford–Johnson framing is described as a *plausible* all-`n` polynomial framed certificate for the standard Ford–Johnson upper bound `F_FJ(n)` subject to checking complete recursive frame construction and odd case (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, §3/§5) — i.e. **not an audited/implemented all-`n` theorem or artifact here**. Since `F_FJ(15)=42=S(15)`, a correct framed Ford–Johnson certificate at budget 42 would answer the question affirmatively for `n=15`, but constructing/auditing its explicit permutation frames and join accounting is the missing step.
  4. **State/DAG scale context:** all-optimal DAG and symmetry-quotient sizes grow rapidly in experiments (`DAG(all)=65551`, `quotient(all)=134` at `n=6`, with `log2(DAG(all))/n` increasing over `n<=6`; evidence inconclusive asymptotically per `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md`). This does not rule out a specially structured framed certificate but indicates naive enumeration/union DAGs are not a path to an explicit `n=15` witness.

**Best certifiable budget found (explicit, verified framed DAG artifact):** 105 (bubble, 106 nodes). **Best known algorithmic budget matching target:** 42 via Ford–Johnson/merge-insertion (`F_FJ(15)=42`), but **no explicit checker-verified framed DAG artifact** at 42 produced.
