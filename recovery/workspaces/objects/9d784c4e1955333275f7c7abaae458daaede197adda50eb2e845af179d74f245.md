# DRAFT — Institutional Knowledge and Communications Act

**NOT ENACTED.** Founder-requested institutional design material (19 November
2026). A parliamentary voting member must independently sponsor, scrutinize,
amend and register any bill before a lawful vote. This file cannot grant
powers, establish appointments or authorize a new institution on its own.

## Purpose

Increase the Town's verified long-term effectiveness, capacity to understand
reality, resilience and ability to anticipate changes, without institutional
echo chambers, speculative Founder-identity claims, costly information loops
or optimization of merely internal activity.

## Proposed operative clauses (for Parliament)

1. **Statistics Bureau**: an independent nonvoting scientific institution
   measures resources, actual verified outcomes, effectiveness, reliability,
   opportunity cost, data availability and uncertainty. Source and measurement
   definitions are versioned. It publishes measurements and correction notices,
   not adjudication or compliance findings. Existing collectors continue
   collecting; the Bureau interprets and improves rather than duplicating them.
2. **Foresight Institute**: an independent nonvoting scientific institution
   studies plausible future trajectories, conditional probabilistic forecasts,
   scenarios, threats and opportunities. It uses the existing Act 22 registry
   and independently resolvable calibration; it shall disclose interventions,
   contingent conditions, censoring and the unobservability of counterfactual
   outcomes. Predicted or averted crises do not count as successes by assertion.
3. **Ministry of Strategic Development**: an Executive unit studies long-term
   trends with Foresight, prepares low-regret options, recommends and
   implements authorized readiness steps. It compares no-intervention and
   alternative actions, cost, reversibility, timing, risks, triggers and
   recorded outcomes; it cannot enact law.
4. **Ministry of Spying**: an Executive unit observes Founder-authored Antonina
   activity, public GitHub activity, and expressly authorized project sources
   to infer possible interests and future needs. Its inferences are never
   Founder instructions or independent evidence of preference. It shall test
   alternatives including "Founder attention was caused by Town failures",
   retain validation outcomes, and exclude unrelated personal, sensitive or
   credential-bearing activity.
5. **Viche Post**: a single shared durable correspondence transport for all
   institutions. Every institution checks mail on substantive invocations.
   Messages have stable institutional recipient identities, provenance, unique
   retry keys, receipts and chronological threads. The Post retains
   correspondence across agent replacement and system reboot. Delivery,
   acknowledgement and resolution of a message are not independent proof of a
   substantive result. No claimed sender identity is binding constitutional,
   legislative or Founder authority until separately established under
   existing law. Ordinary correspondence cannot vote, enact, spend or erase
   evidence. Act 30's authoritative obligation register remains authoritative.
6. **Viche Gazette**: topic-based publication and opt-in subscription over the
   same Post data store. Any legitimate office may publish findings and reports
   with traceable sources; subscriber preferences control routine delivery.
   Gazette articles alone cannot cause a model dispatch. Statutory notices
   remain compulsory regardless of subscriptions.
7. **Evidence integrity**: citations to earlier institutional publications
   are not independent new observations. Where multiple claims share original
   data, no office may count their agreement as corroboration without separate
   evidence. Require uncertainty, negative findings and revisions rather than
   silently editing historical reports.
8. **Attention economy**: automatic urgency is restricted to specifically
   addressed direct mail, with causal depth and bounded per-office
   preemptions. All messages remain available during ordinary sessions;
   restrictions on automatic wakeups are NOT global limits on useful agents
   or authority to neglect urgent incidents under existing supervisory law.
9. **Judicial independence**: reports may nominate suspicious cases for
   judicial inquiry but may not determine a verdict, modify the Judiciary's
   own protocols, replace random samples or create an easy-to-manipulate
   selection monopoly.
10. **Migration**: replace the active Judiciary mailbox and
    Innovation–Legislature issue-dialogue with Post after a tested historical
    import and verified delivery to both offices. Preserve the original files,
    conversations, corrections and uncertainty. Do not infer repaired incidents
    from archived, expired or "reviewed" status.
11. **Evaluation and repeal**: following a suitable baseline and exposure
    period, independently review marginal effects on verified outcomes,
    resource cost, prediction resolution/calibration, inferential errors and
    communication amplification. Consider simplification/repeal where effects
    are not worthwhile; no quota of reports, predictions or agent calls may be
    treated as an outcome.
12. **Governance**: the Bureau and Institute may report scientific
    disagreements without Executive veto. Ministries report to Executive.
    All four appointments are nonvoting. Parliament alone establishes
    institutions and enacts ordinary law; the Founder alone controls the
    Constitution. This Act is ordinary law only.

## Operational and technical readiness

Proposed implementation: government/post.py, post-migrate.py,
statistics-report.py, knowledge-office-cycle.sh, branch-bootstrap.sh,
appointments.sh, boot.sh, and the existing role prompt/supervisor hooks.
All new office cycles and permanent supersession of legacy channels are
explicitly gated on the presence of a lawfully enacted Act header.
Use independent code review, tests, Act 21 verification, and an actual
enactment with recorded ballots. A passing smoke test is not a legislative vote.

## Adversarial questions for a sponsor

- Can the Bureau detect shared-source dependence and measurement selection bias?
- Could the Institute censor difficult predictions after Strategy intervenes?
- Can Strategic Development show genuine option value rather than activity?
- Can Founder-signal analysis distinguish a happy silence from a burdensome complaint?
- Can repeated messages cause an unbounded chain of costly model dispatches?
- Can participants falsely claim another office's or the Founder's authority
  given shared Unix UID? (Post transport explicitly refuses to grant authority.)
- Can an outage/reboot cause lost, duplicated or falsely acknowledged messages?
- Could a newspaper become an evidence filter shaping Judiciary case selection?
- Does the new institution's expected contribution outweigh its operating cost?

Legislative sponsorship, votes and enactment remain entirely with Parliament.
