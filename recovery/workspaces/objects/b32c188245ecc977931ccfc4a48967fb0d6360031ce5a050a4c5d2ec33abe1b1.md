## 2. `A1_Snapshot_From_Parent`

### 2.1 Obligation (as audited)
For an `I_{M_D}`-reachable state with row 15 guard `Top(c̄) ∧ M_BackA0(c̄) ∧ RetAcc(c̄) ∧ HasSel(c̄) ∧ SelA(c̄,a) ∧ SelB(c̄,b)`, row 15 must construct `PushChildA(d̄,c̄,a,b,1)` only from `c̄`'s preserved `PosF/UsedF/SelA/SelB` (and `a,b,d̄=CurBase`, fixed local formulas), with no read of popped A0-child fields, and those `c̄` fields must equal the snapshot established by `D_adv_push_A0` and preserved through the A0-return discipline.

### 2.2 Row read/write/changed-set check

| Row | Guard/role | `S` | Relevant writes to `c̄` snapshot fields | Relevant reads | Normalized-`Enc` support |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 7 `D_adv_push_A0` | pushes A0 from `M_Eval,CtrlAdv,HasSel,SelA,SelB,Incomp` top `c̄` | `{c̄,d0}` | `SetParentBackA0(c̄)`: `M_BackA0'`, `RetNone'`, preserves `HasSel/SelA/SelB/HasCur/CurA/CurB/PosF/UsedF/Parent/Ctrl` | `PosF/UsedF/SelA/SelB/HasSel` of `c̄` for `PushChildA(d0,c̄,a,b,0)` | §1.4/row 7 |

**Read-scope check.** Row 15 quantifies over `c̄,d̄,a,b` and uses `FreshChild(d̄)=CurBase`; it contains no field access to inactive `d0`. `PushChildA` (§1.4) updates `PosF'/UsedF'/...` of `d̄` from `c̄` and fixed formulas (`StepPosBit_c` side `(b,a)`, `SuccCtr`, `PostBudgetFrom`, `MinKey`). This matches the structural-invariants note's claim (1),(2) at schema level.

**Changed-set check.** While `c̄` is live but not `Top` (after row 7 push, before A0 return makes `c̄` top), every non-push/pop row has `S=∅` or terminal `S`; push/pop rows name only their `Top/child` or `p̄,c̄_top` pair and impose `LowerStackPreservedExcept(S)` giving `FrameEq'` to live frames outside `S`. Thus, absent a violation of the DFS/top discipline, no row writes `PosF/UsedF/Sel` of that lower-stack `c̄`.

### 2.3 Invariant-strengthening precision

The note proposes `BackA0_Snapshot` (row-local) and includes it in `I^{+}_{M_D}`. **Precision issues / exact obligations:**

- **Reachability-history clause is informal.** “Sel/PosF/UsedF/Parent/active-Ctrl of `c̄` are the values preserved since the `D_adv_push_A0` step that made `c̄` BackA0” is not a first-order state invariant; to be inductive it must be reduced to state predicates plus a discipline lemma, e.g. `M_BackA0(c̄) ∧ FrameLive(c̄) -> HasSel(c̄) ∧ ∃!a SelA(c̄,a) ∧ ∃!b SelB(c̄,b) ∧ (RetNone(c̄) ∨ RetAcc(c̄) ∨ RetRej(c̄) from valid child return)` and a preservation lemma that no row with `c̄ ∉ S` changes these fields.
- **Guard consistency.** Row 15 requires `RetAcc(c̄)`; rows 13/14 consume `M_BackA0,RetRej`. `BackA0_Snapshot` should not imply `RetAcc`-only states; it should characterize the snapshot available whenever row 15 is enabled.
- **`Ctrl/Parent` preservation scope.** `MarkRetAccOn/MarkRetRejOn` preserve non-marker `Fld` except `Ret` (`PreserveNonMarkerFields`) and preserve `FrameLive`; this includes `Ctrl,Parent,Mode`. The strengthening must state preservation of the full non-`Ret` frame fields relied on by row 15, not only `PosF/UsedF/Sel`.

The strengthening is directionally right but needs an inductive, state-only form plus a changed-set preservation lemma.

### 2.4 Falsification attempt

No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying transition trace was found.

- **Schema-level no-read-from-popped-child**: row 15 has no inactive-frame read; falsifier shape (ii) in the note requires alias `d̄=x̄` with a preserved live/popped frame — that is a `FreshChild`/alias failure, not an independent A1 snapshot read.
- **Snapshot mutation**: would require a reachable transition writing `PosF/UsedF/SelA/SelB` of a live BackA0 lower-stack parent `c̄` while `c̄ ∉ S`. Every normalized row either includes the modified frame in `S`, is terminal/absorbing, or preserves lower-stack frames by `FrameEq'`. Constructing such a transition contradicts the normalized changed-set discipline; making it `I_{M_D}`-reachable would require an unlisted row or an `I_{M_D}` violation.
- **A0-child non-return / wrong marker**: could prevent row 15 from firing but does not falsify the snapshot lemma's implication when row 15 fires.

`[falsification-shape]` (non-constructed): `Init_{M_D}^{sym} ⊢* E --D_adv_push_A0(c̄,d0)--> E1 ⊢* E2 --R--> E3` with `c̄ ∉ S_R` but `¬FrameEq'(c̄)` on `PosF/UsedF/Sel` across `R`, then `E3 ⊢* E --D_backA0_acc_A1(c̄,d̄)--> E'` uses mutated `c̄`. **No such `R` exists in rows 1–19 under `LowerStackPreservedExcept`; reachability of such a state is not established.**

### 2.5 Verdict

**Supported / no explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable witness.** The normalized row 15/7/acc-return schema supports the read-set and non-marker preservation claims. The obligation remains **open**: prove an inductive `I^{+}_{M_D}` snapshot discipline showing `c̄`'s `HasSel/SelA/SelB/PosF/UsedF` and relevant non-`Ret` fields are unchanged between the `D_adv_push_A0` that installs BackA0 and any later `D_backA0_acc_A1` consuming `c̄`, using unique `Top`, changed-set discipline, and `PreserveNonMarkerFields`/`MarkRetAccOn` behavior.

## 3. `FreshChild_Fresh_On_IMD`

### 3.1 Obligation (as audited)
Every push row (`D_i`, `i∈{6,7,10,15}`) on an `I_{M_D}`-reachable state with `d̄=CurBase(E)` must have `d̄` fresh: not live, not equal to any live frame, not `Top`, and no alias that invalidates `LowerStackPreservedExcept({c̄,d̄})` for live `x̄ ∉ {c̄,d̄}`.

### 3.2 Row read/write/changed-set check

| Row family | `d̄` source / bump | liveness of `d̄` | live lower stack outside `S` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Push 6,7,10,15 | `FreshChild(d̄)=CurBase(d̄)`; `Globals_push(c̄,d̄)` gives `CurBaseBump := ∃b̄,b̄'(CurBase(b̄)∧SuccCell(b̄,b̄')∧CurBase'(b̄'))` | `FrameLive'(d̄)` by `ChildFrameBase`/`PushChild*` | `LowerStackPreservedExcept({c̄,d̄})` |
| Pop/return 4,5,9,12,14,17,19 | no new child; `CurBaseUnchanged` by `Globals_pop` | `FrameInactive'(c̄_top)` only | `LowerStackPreservedExcept({p̄,c̄})` |
| Halt absorbing 1 | `Globals_halt_absorb` preserves `CurBase`/`Top` | none | `LowerStackPreservedExcept(∅)` |
| Root/non-root terminal 2,3,8,11,13,16,18 | `Globals_root_halt_*` has `CurBaseUnchanged`, `¬∃z Top'(z)`; `Terminal_ClearFrames` | all cleared | terminal convention |

Realization `Init_{M_D}^{sym}`: `Top=ZeroCell`, only `ZeroCell` live, `CurBase=SuccCell(ZeroCell)`. So initial freshness holds under the intended `ZeroCell != SuccCell(ZeroCell)` and functional `SuccCell`.

**Allocator-discipline support.** The normalized schema specifies bumping and uniqueness of `CurBase` as a register discipline, but `I_{M_D}` as stated in §0 names `Top/EmptyStack/Halt/ExactlyOneMode/Ret/Ctrl/Parent/UsedF/Sel/Cur/path/[SYM-TOP]`; it does **not** explicitly list `∃!b̄ CurBase(b̄) ∧ ¬FrameLive(b̄) ∧ separation from live frames`. The realization's encoding/`E(Rbar)` carries `CurBase`, but freshness is an invariant obligation, not automatic from bump syntax alone.

### 3.3 Invariant-strengthening precision

The note's candidate `CurBase_Fresh(E) := ∃!b̄ CurBase(E,b̄) ∧ ¬FrameLive(E,b̄) ∧ ∀x̄(FrameLive(E,x̄)->x̄!=b̄)` is the right minimal shape. **Precision issues:**

- **`x̄ != b̄` is necessary but not inductive by itself.** After a push with `d̄=b̄`, `FrameLive'` includes `d̄` and `CurBase'` becomes `b̄'=SuccCell(b̄)`. Preservation needs `¬FrameLive'(b̄')`, i.e. `b̄' != d̄` and `b̄'` not equal to any previously live frame. This requires an `SuccCell`/reachability fact: no finite push sequence from `CurBase(Init)=SuccCell(ZeroCell)` produces a value in `FrameLive(E) ∪ {d̄}`.
- **Lex-monotonicity is stronger than needed and not stated.** `∀x̄(FrameLive(E,x̄)->x̄ <_{lex,Cell} b̄)` would suffice given a strict lex `SuccCell`, but `Cell=W^r` finiteness, lex wrap behavior at max, and functional/total `SuccCell` assumptions must be made explicit (realization gives `SuccCell` as FO/DTM-definable over `<`, not a full order-theoretic monotonicity proof here).
- **Bounded-push argument is available but external.** Realization Theorem 5.1 gives active depth `<=2k+1<=2N+1`, hence `O(N)` pushes total on reachable accepting/rejecting runs in that analysis; `|Cell|=N^r` is much larger. This supports a no-collision strengthening, but it is an `[inherited assumption]`/`[open]` derived from the machine/space argument, not an immediate row-matrix consequence.
- **Terminal rows.** `CurBaseUnchanged` on root-halt/absorbing rows is fine; if `Terminal_ClearFrames` is used, freshness becomes vacuous, but `CurBase` should still be well-defined by convention.

A sufficient `I^{+}_{M_D}` addition is: `CurBase_Unique ∧ CurBase_NotLive ∧ Allocator_Separated`, where `Allocator_Separated` is either a monotone counter/initial-segment fact (`CurBase` is the next unused `SuccCell` iterate above all ever-live frames) or a concrete `NoReuse` history/separation invariant compatible with bounded reachable pushes.

### 3.4 Falsification attempt

No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying transition trace was found in the audited normalized/realization schema.

- **Schema-only countermodel (not `I_{M_D}`-reachable).** If `SuccCell` were interpreted as collapsing/wrapping so that `SuccCell(CurBase(E)) = x̄` for some live `x̄`, then a push row would set `FrameLive'(d̄)`/write `d̄` while also making `CurBase'` alias; or more directly, a bad `CurBase` value with `FrameLive(E,CurBase(E))` would let a push guard `FreshChild(d̄)` select a live frame. This contradicts the intended `Init_{M_D}^{sym}`+bounded lex allocator discipline and is outside the stated realization semantics; it is **not** produced as an `I_{M_D}`-reachable trace.
- **Row-matrix contradiction test.** A push requires `Globals_push` + `ChildFrameBase` writing `d̄`; if `d̄` aliases live `x̄ ∉ {c̄,d̄}`, `LowerStackPreservedExcept({c̄,d̄})` would demand `FrameEq'(x̄)` while row updates target `d̄=x̄`. No normalized row resolves this by case split; the only prevention is the freshness precondition/invariant.

`[falsification-shape]` (model/semantic, not established `I_{M_D}`-reachable): `I_{M_D}(E) ∧ D_i(E,c̄,d̄,...)` is a push row, `CurBase(E,d̄)`, and `FrameLive(E,d̄)` or `FrameLive(E,x̄)∧x̄=d̄∧x̄∉{c̄,d̄}`. Equivalently, an allocator collision `SuccCell`-chain reaches an already-live frame under the reachable push discipline.

### 3.5 Verdict

**Supported / no explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable witness.** The bump/unchanged allocator discipline and `Init_{M_D}^{sym}` support the intended freshness story. The obligation remains **open**: add and preserve an explicit allocator separation invariant (minimal `CurBase` unique, non-live, disjoint from all live frames, and preserved under `SuccCell` bumps given bounded/reachable push count or monotone `SuccCell` semantics). The note's strengthening is imprecise until `SuccCell`/no-wrap or bounded-allocation semantics are fixed.

## 4. `Parent_Functional_Active_Preserved`

### 4.1 Obligation (as audited)
From `Init_{M_D}^{sym}` and over `I_{M_D}`-reachable row applications: active non-root frames have unique live parents; root/live are disjoint as needed; every non-root pop target is the unique DFS-active parent supplied by `Parent(c̄,p̄)`.

### 4.2 Row read/write/changed-set check

| Row family | `Parent`/`FrameLive` effect | `Top`/target effect | Preservation argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| Init (realization §2.1) | root `ZeroCell` live, `¬∃p̄ Parent(root,p̄)`, no other live frames | `Top=root` | base case holds |
| Push 6,7,10,15 | `PushChildE/A` sets `Parent'(d̄,c̄)`; `FrameLive'(d̄)`; `c̄` remains live; `SetParentBack*` preserves `Parent` of `c̄` | `Top'(d̄)` by `[SYM-TOP]` push globals | only new active non-root is `d̄` with parent `c̄`; others `FrameEq'` outside `S` |
| Pop 4,5,9,12,14,17,19 | no new `Parent'` links; `FrameInactive'(c̄)`; `MarkRet*On(p̄)` preserves non-marker `Fld` incl. `Parent,FrameLive` of `p̄` | guard has `Parent(c̄,p̄)`; `Globals_pop(p̄,c̄)` gives `Top'(p̄)` | removed frame is `c̄`; `p̄` becomes top and keeps prior parent |
| Halt 1 | `Globals_halt_absorb`, `LowerStackPreservedExcept(∅)` | `Top'` preserved | preserved if frame model unchanged |
| Terminal 2,3,8,11,13,16,18 | `Globals_root_halt_*` no `Top'`; `Terminal_ClearFrames` | empty stack | vacuous after clearing; convention-dependent |

**Critical check.** No row in 1–19 assigns `Parent'(x̄,·)` except push-child creation on `d̄`. No row makes an existing non-root live frame root except by not touching it; no row makes root have a parent. Therefore functionality/root-disjointness preservation reduces to: (a) initial state, (b) pushed `d̄` gets exactly one parent `c̄`, (c) popped `c̄` is inactivated and not referenced as active parent, (d) untouched live frames keep `Parent` by `FrameEq'`.

**Pop target check.** Non-root pop guards include `Parent(c̄,p̄)` (rows 4,5,9,12,14,17,19). Under functional active `Parent` in `I_{M_D}`, this picks the unique parent. The structural-invariants note correctly identifies the remaining proof: tie the guard's `Parent(c̄,p̄)` to the DFS active-path parent, not merely some `Parent` edge in an arbitrary model.

### 4.3 Invariant-strengthening precision

The proposed `DFSPath` is useful but currently mixes graph and discipline language. **Precision issues:**

- **“Every intended active stack frame” is informal.** A state predicate should quantify over `FrameLive` and `Parent` reachability, plus the DFS discipline fact that only the root-to-`Top` path is active/returnable.
- **Acyclicity is needed if parent chains can be cyclic in a nonstandard model.** `I_{M_D}` states functional `Parent` on active non-root frames and root-to-top active path discipline; an inductive proof should derive acyclicity on live frames from `Init` (no parents) and push-only parent creation to the current live `Top`.
- **Terminal consistency matters.** The candidate `Terminal_Consistent` (`Halt* -> no Top, no FrameLive`) is stronger than necessary for parent functionality on live active frames but avoids stale-frame obligations. It must match the normalized terminal rows (`Terminal_ClearFrames`, no `Top'`) and realization `q_halt` behavior.
- **`ExactlyOneRootLive` should follow from `ExactlyOneTop` + DFS path discipline**, not be assumed independently unless made part of `I^{+}_{M_D}` with preservation proof.

### 4.4 Falsification attempt

No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying transition trace was found.

- **Two parents for one active frame:** would require some row to add `Parent'(c̄,p1)` and `Parent'(c̄,p2)` for live `c̄`, or preserve conflicting parents. Rows assign `Parent'` only for fresh `d̄` in push templates (`Parent'(d̄,c̄)`); no row updates `Parent` of an existing live frame except via `FrameEq'` preservation outside `S`. A falsifier needs either a non-normalized row or aliasing `d̄` to an existing live frame (covered by `FreshChild`).
- **Wrong pop target:** non-root pop rows require `Parent(c̄,p̄)` in the guard. A state with `Top(c̄)`, `Parent(c̄,p̄)`, but actual DFS parent `q̄!=p̄` contradicts functional active `Parent`/active-path discipline in `I_{M_D}`. The schema does not create that state; constructing reachability would require breaking parent functionality before the pop.
- **Parent to inactive/non-live frame on active path:** push sets parent to `c̄=Top`, live. Pop inactivates child `c̄` and makes `p̄` top; `p̄`'s parent remains preserved. No row inactivates a frame that remains the parent of a live active frame except impossible DFS violations.

`[falsification-shape]` (contradicts stated `I_{M_D}`/schema discipline): `I_{M_D}(E*) ∧ FrameLive(E*,c̄) ∧ Parent(E*,c̄,p1) ∧ Parent(E*,c̄,p2) ∧ p1!=p2`; or `I_{M_D}(E*) ∧ D_pop(E*,c̄,p̄)` with `Parent(E*,c̄,p̄)` but `p̄` not the DFS-active parent. **No such `I_{M_D}`-reachable state/step is constructed from the normalized rows.**

### 4.5 Verdict

**Supported / no explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable witness.** Row 1–19 parent/liveness/top discipline supports the claim structurally. The obligation remains **open**: prove an inductive `DFSPath`/active-parent invariant from `Init_{M_D}^{sym}` across all rows, including `LowerStackPreservedExcept` preservation, push-only `Parent'(d̄,c̄)` creation on fresh `d̄`, pop consumption of the active parent link, and terminal frame-clearing conventions.

## 5. Cross-cutting audit findings

1. **No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying trace found** for any of the three obligations. All candidate falsifiers identified are either blocked by normalized changed-set/lower-stack rules, blocked by `[SYM-TOP]`/guard structure, or require semantic violations (allocator collision, non-functional parent) not licensed by `I_{M_D}`/realization semantics.
2. **The structural-invariants note is directionally sound** on row-local read/write arguments, but its `I^{+}_{M_D}` clauses need inductive tightening:
   - `BackA0_Snapshot`: remove trace-history prose from the state invariant; add an explicit state-only form plus changed-set/preserved-field induction.
   - `CurBase_Fresh`: add `SuccCell` no-collision/monotonicity or bounded-allocation semantics; `¬FrameLive(b̄)∧∀x̄ live -> x̄!=b̄` alone is not one-step inductive across `SuccCell` bump.
   - `DFSPath`/`Terminal_Consistent`: make root/top/path/clearing conventions first-order and align with terminal `S`/`Globals_root_halt_*` behavior.
3. **Dependencies between obligations:** `A1_Snapshot` relies on no aliasing (so depends on `FreshChild` for the `d̄`/lower-stack non-alias argument) and on preserved lower-stack frames (shared with `Parent`). `Parent` pop correctness is independent of A1 snapshot values but uses `Parent(c̄,p̄)` guards consistently with `I_{M_D}`. A combined `I^{+}_{M_D}` induction should treat them together.
4. **Realization alignment:** realization P1 and Theorems 4.1–5.2 are not re-audited here; they are compatible assumptions for the deterministic macro-step/space context but do not discharge these `Enc`-level invariants.
