## Prior-level `cap_children` labeling (minor reporting note)

The script stores `cap_children = 1<<(C-d-1)` for prior level `depth=d+1`
(`scripts/s13_wells_depth7_cong.py:113,128`), while the depth-7 analysis stores
`cap_children = 1<<(C-depth-1)=2^26` for the S6→S7 transition. These values
match the *transition/child* cap `2^(C - child_depth)`? Check:
level 1 gives `2^32` and child depth is 1 so `2^(33-1)=2^32` ✓; level 6 gives
`2^27` and child depth 6 gives `2^(33-6)=2^27` ✓; depth7 gives `2^26` for child
depth 7 ✓. Thus `cap_children` is consistently `2^(C - produced_child_depth)`.
The field name could be read ambiguously, but the numeric values and script
semantics are coherent. **Not a model error; a documentation-label caveat.**
