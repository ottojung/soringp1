## 1. Target (a): `Δ_{M_D}` symbol-level/helper-marker closure and no-spurious transitions relative to `M_D^alt`

**Synthesis claim.** `Δ_{M_D}` is an explicit case-closed DFS stack-discipline schema (tape-formulas §5, delta-d-dfs-stack §§2,5,6) with local move math explicit, but helper/marker/addressing predicates are schematic; no-spurious is intended but not proved at complete symbol level relative to an explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}`. `[open:CKS-AV-MDALT-REALIZE]` part (1) requires symbol-level closure, fixed explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}`, equivalence `Δ_{M_D}(Conf;Conf') ↔ Conf --δ_{M_D^alt}--> Conf'`, functionality/totality on non-halting `I_{M_D}` states, invariant preservation, and no-spurious.

**Audit assessment: correct and supported.**

- The delta-d-dfs-stack artifact gives the right case split: `Δ_halt, Δ_root_acc, Δ_root_rej, Δ_eval_acc_ret, Δ_eval_rej_ret, Δ_sort_pick_push, Δ_sort_exhaust_rej, Δ_backE_acc_ret, Δ_backE_rej_next, Δ_adv_push_A0, Δ_backA0_rej_ret, Δ_backA0_acc_push_A1, Δ_backA1_ret` (synthesis §1.3 / delta-d-dfs-stack §5). The delta-d-dfs-stack audit accepted this as a plausible paper-level stack-discipline schema covering init/terminal/root-non-root/existential lex cursor+push+backtrack/universal `A0`/`A1` sequencing/pop.
- The same audit flagged schematic helpers as the reason totality/mutual-exclusion/invariant/no-spurious are not formally discharged: `ParentOf, PopTo, ReturnAccTo/ReturnRejTo, FreshChild, SetParentBackE/BackA0/BackA1, ResumeSortSearch', NoActiveFrames', BackEMarker, ChildReturned*`, plus exact marker/offset/boundary wiring. Synthesis §2.2 lists these and correctly states mutual exclusion/totality/invariant/no-spurious require concrete encoding and that “relative to `M_D^alt`” requires explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}`.
- Tape-formulas audit reached the same narrower residual as `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D]`: complete, total, functional, invariant-preserving, no-spurious FO stack/control `Δ_{M_D}`. PACK-REACH audit, after discharging the whole-configuration acceptance normal form, isolated `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/SYM]` as the remaining symbol-level closure of case-closed `Δ_{M_D}`/helpers with those properties. Synthesis’ MDALT-REALIZE.1 is consistent with and slightly stronger in packaging (it adds explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}` realization/equivalence), which is appropriate for an end-to-end closure statement.

**Conclusion (a).** Not discharged. The synthesis correctly reports the case schema as explicit/paper-level and the symbol-level/helper-marker/no-spurious realization relative to explicit `M_D^alt`/`δ` as open.

## 2. Target (b): `M_D^alt` halting/space/semantics and alternating-DFS equivalence

**Synthesis claim.** Intended semantics are explicit (DFS evaluator over `M_G/G(n,k)` frames; existential `CtrlSort`/`YieldE` lex-next choices, universal `CtrlAdv` `ChildA0/A1` both evaluated, `PostBudget/CfgAcc/CfgRej` terminals, budget `k`). Space/halting have a paper-level `O(N^p)`/finite-search argument (frame closure `n^2`, metadata `O(log N)`, depth `<=2k+1`, finite lex cursor and budget consumption), but not a fully discharged DTM halting proof from explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}`. Equivalence `M_D^alt` accepts `w` iff alternating DFS accepts initial `M_G/G(n,k)` is inherited semantic/DTM assumption conditional on (a)+(b) realization.

**Audit assessment: correct and appropriately qualified.**

- The intended DFS evaluator semantics match CKS/AV §6.3, tape-formulas §§4-5, and delta-d-dfs-stack §§4-5: existential sorter enumerates incomparable pairs and accepts on some child; universal adversary evaluates `A0` then `A1` and accepts only if both accept; terminals by total/budget/reject rules. Synthesis §3.1 states this clearly.
- The space/halting argument is the standard one for this evaluator: each comparison consumes one `SuccCtr` budget step on universal children, so depth is `<=2k+1<=2N+1`; current closure is `n^2<=N^2`; total `O(N^3)` with fixed-addressing margin. CKS/AV gave the same bound; delta-d-dfs-stack/tape-formulas used the same frame layout. This is a valid **paper-level** argument, not a discharged tableau proof from a complete symbol-level `δ`.
- The alternating-DFS equivalence is the right semantic bridge but depends on (a): the machine must implement the alternation with no missed/spurious branch and correct return propagation. Synthesis §3.3 states this dependency. Prior audits treated this as inherited/conditional: PACK-REACH audit accepted the normal form conditional on deterministic whole-config `Δ_{M_D}` and inherited `M_D^alt` halting/semantics; PACK audit inherited `M_D^alt` semantics/halting.

**Conclusion (b).** Intended semantics, `O(N^3)` space bound, and finite-halt argument are paper-level plausible and correctly referenced. Full discharge requires explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}` plus symbol-level proof of (a)/(b) behavior; synthesis correctly treats this as open/inherited within MDALT-REALIZE.

## 3. Target (c): `M_G/G(n,k)` game equivalence and `SORT-BOUND-U`

**Synthesis claim.** `G(n,k)` is the comparison game on antichain initial state with incomparable-pair selection, adversary orientation, transitive closure, budget consumption, total-order accept, budget-exhausted reject. `S(n)<=k iff sorter wins G(n,k)` is the standard adaptive-comparison-tree/adversary-argument bridge; `w=1^n#1^k ∈ SORT-BOUND-U iff S(n)<=k` by definition. Compatibility with `M_D^alt` is conditional on MDALT-REALIZE part (2).

**Audit assessment: largely discharged as model bridge; machine realization remains coupled to (a)-(b).**

- `docs/problem.md` defines `S(n)` as minimum height of a deterministic adaptive comparison decision tree correctly sorting every permutation of `n` distinct labelled keys (§§6-24) and defines `SORT-BOUND-U = {1^n # 1^k : S(n) <= k}` (§41). It explicitly uses the minimax/partial-order recurrence and game-style characterization context (and `research-certificates-barriers.md` states `G(n,k)` and “`S(n)<=k` iff sorter wins `G(n,k)`” as the Claim A/game characterization).
- Synthesis §4.2 gives the standard two-direction bridge: a height-`<=k` correct adaptive comparison tree yields a winning sorter strategy in `G(n,k)`; a deterministic winning sorter strategy unfolds to a correct comparison tree of height `<=k`. This is correct at the paper/model level under the fixed comparison model (distinct labelled keys, pairwise comparisons, adaptive). The `SORT-BOUND-U` equivalence for well-formed unary `w` is definitional.
- Caution: the bridge must use the same move semantics realized by `M_D^alt` — antichain start, incomparable-pair selection, adversary orientation, transitive closure (`StepPosBit` on incomparable edge), budget consumption, `PostBudget`/totality terminals. Synthesis §1.2/§4.1/§4.3 states this compatibility requirement and ties it to MDALT-REALIZE. That is appropriate.

**Conclusion (c).** The semantic/model equivalences `sorter wins G(n,k) iff S(n)<=k iff w ∈ SORT-BOUND-U` are **paper-level discharged** as the project’s fixed comparison-game characterization, given the standard tree/strategy unfolding. The remaining work is not to prove a new model theorem but to ensure the explicit `M_D^alt/Δ_D` realization uses exactly these semantics. Synthesis is correct to include this compatibility in the coupled open lemma, but (c) alone is not the blocking mathematical gap.

## 4. `[open:CKS-AV-MDALT-REALIZE]`: minimality and confirmation

**Synthesis statement.** Single minimal failing lemma/formula: complete symbol-level realization of `M_D^alt` by `Δ_{M_D}` (fixed `A(w)/Cell=W^r` encoding, explicit helper/marker/addressing predicates, fixed explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}`, and proofs of (1) symbol-level closure/no-spurious, (2) halting/space/DFS semantics, (3) model bridge compatibility). Synthesis argues PACK Lemma 3.2 and PACK-REACH reduce global FO(PFP)/IFP acceptance to a deterministic whole-config operator induced by `Δ_{M_D}`, so once (1)-(2) are discharged the packed PFP sentence is standard/explicit; (c) attaches the model bridge to the same explicit machine.

**Audit assessment: confirmed as the coupled blocker; refinement on strict minimality.**

- **Supported by prior audits.** Tape audit isolated `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D]` (full DFS stack `Δ_{M_D}`). Delta-d audit replaced it by narrower `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK]` at schema level, but still left symbol-level helper/marker closure and complete no-spurious/tableau not fully discharged. PACK audit discharged one-step correspondence but rejected whole-configuration acceptance; PACK-REACH audit discharged whole-configuration acceptance normal form **conditional** on deterministic FO operator induced by case-closed `Δ_{M_D}` and inherited assumptions, and isolated `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/SYM]` as the symbol-level closure of `Δ_{M_D}` helper/marker/return-channel formulas (mutual exclusion, totality, determinism, `I_{M_D}` preservation, no spurious transitions relative to `M_D^alt`).
- **MDALT-REALIZE vs Δ_D/SYM.** MDALT-REALIZE.1 is essentially `Δ_D/SYM` plus explicit `δ_{M_D^alt}` and the equality `Δ_{M_D} ↔ δ_{M_D^alt}` successor. MDALT-REALIZE.2 adds explicit halting/space/DFS-semantics proof for that realized machine. MDALT-REALIZE.3 attaches `G(n,k)/S(n)/SORT-BOUND-U` semantic compatibility. Parts (1)-(2) are the genuine unresolved technical closure. Part (3)'s model equivalence is paper-level available; including the *realization* of those semantics is reasonable, but including the already-standard model theorem as a “failing lemma” overstates the failure.
- **Minimality judgment.** A strictly minimal failing lemma/formula, in the sense used by PACK-REACH audit, is: **complete symbol-level closure and correctness of the explicit deterministic DFS evaluator `M_D^alt`/`Δ_{M_D}`/`δ_{M_D^alt}`** (helpers/markers/offsets/return channels fixed; mutual exclusion; totality on relevant non-halting `I_{M_D}` states; functionality/determinism; `I_{M_D}` preservation; no spurious transitions; halting; and acceptance iff alternating DFS accepts the initial `M_G/G(n,k)` state under the fixed local move semantics `Init/StepPosBit/PostBudget/YieldE/YieldA0/YieldA1/ConfigAcc/ConfigRej`). The `G(n,k) iff S(n)<=k iff w∈SORT-BOUND-U` bridge is then the model consequence stated in synthesis §4. Packaging this as MDALT-REALIZE is acceptable and faithful to the end-to-end target.

**Conclusion.** `[open:CKS-AV-MDALT-REALIZE]` is **confirmed** as the synthesis’ single coupled blocker and is consistent with prior audit lineage. It should be read with the refinement that target (c)’s model equivalence is paper-level established; the failing component is realization/closure/correctness of `M_D^alt/Δ_D` and its exact implementation of `G(n,k)` DFS semantics.
