# Exec report: root-composition-exact-recurrence

## Question addressed

**Front (research-frontier `root-composition-exact-recurrence`).** Seek a compositional exact recurrence for optimal sorting from chain or ordinal-product interfaces that preserves the adversarial root and both sibling branches, not merely the post-transcript minimax `F(P)`.

**Fixed model / implication context (definition/literature, per `docs/problem.md`, `AGENTS.md`).** `S(n)` is the worst-case height of an optimal adaptive comparison tree sorting `n` distinct labelled keys, equivalently the partial-order minimax `S(n)=F(∅)` (`S_eq_adv_univ`, kernel-checked correspondence). A uniform, polynomial-state exact root recurrence that preserves the antichain-root optimum would improve the time bound for computing `S(n)` and could, in principle, place `SORT-BOUND-U` in `P`; this report makes no such algorithmic claim.

**Basis consulted.** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/research-compression-next.md`, `docs/research-orientation-modules.md`, `docs/research-series-decomposition.md`, `docs/research-frontier.json`, `docs/research-packets.json`, and related notes (`docs/research-structural-lower-bounds.md`, `docs/research-parallel-duality.md`). The front basis cites the established root-prefix sibling obstruction and prior failed naive direct-sum transfer in the research ledger; the concrete obstruction stated in `docs/research-packets.json` is used as the named witness below.

## Candidates examined

All candidates below target an **exact** recurrence at the adversarial **antichain root** (global optimum `S(n)`), not merely evaluation of `F(P)` for an already-reached poset. Claim types are labelled.

### C1. Ordinal-sum / series interface: factor `P = P_1 ⊕ … ⊕ P_k`, summarize by component values (literature + deduction, known)

- **Interface idea.** Use the unique maximal ordinal-sum decomposition and `F(A ⊕ B)=F(A)+F(B)` (deterministic direct-sum / adversary-potential argument; `docs/research-series-decomposition.md`, Jain–Klauck–Sántha 2010 context).
- **What it preserves.** The post-transcript minimax value of an ordinal-sum **state** `P`: knowing components and their `F` values is exact for `F(P)`.
- **Why it is not a root recurrence.** (deduction) The initial antichain on `n≥2` keys is series-prime (its incomparability graph is connected/complete), so the identity does not decompose the root `F(∅)=S(n)`. Moreover, `F` is not additive over parallel/disjoint composition (`F(a ⊔ b)=1` but `F(a)+F(b)=0`, `docs/research-series-decomposition.md`), so comparisons that interleave incomparable components cannot be recovered from per-component ordinal sums. Therefore ordinal-product/series interfaces give compositionality only after reaching a compatible state, not a composition that generates or certifies the antichain-root optimum.

### C2. Local post-state summary `σ(P)=F(P)` (or `σ(P)=(P/!\sim, F(P))`) (deduction)

- **Interface idea.** Treat any reachable poset as summarized by its minimax remainder `F(P)` (optionally modulo exact symmetries: relabelling/duality are sound state quotients for `F`, `docs/research-compression-next.md`).
- **What it preserves.** `F(P)` itself and, for sound quotients, the recursive minimax transition on classes (`F`-constant plus equivariant move/transition structure in the sense made precise in `docs/research-compression-next.md`).
- **Why it fails for root composition.** (deduction, using the stated obstruction) Optimality at the root depends on a **history** `h`: after depth `d` reaching `P`, the transcript is root-viable only relative to the budget consumed and to the outcomes not taken. The reachability functional recorded in the packet is
  `R_h(P)=max(d+F(P), max_i (i+F(Q_i^{sibling})))`,
  and root reachability requires control over **both** the chosen branch and every sibling branch along the prefix. `F(P)` collapses `d` and all sibling obligations, so it cannot distinguish histories with equal local remainder but different global viability. Hence any recurrence that factors only through `F` of the current poset (or any function of `P` alone that omits prefix/sibling data) is insufficient for the adversarial root.

### C3. Chain/ordinal-product “interface” enriched by transcript depth: `σ_h(P)=(d, F(P))` (deduction, falsified as root-sufficient)

- **Interface idea.** Combine local minimax remainder with depth to account for budget already spent.
- **Falsification (from stated packet witness).** `docs/research-packets.json` records: for `n=5`, target/state described as `C2 ordinal A3` admits a transcript with `d+F = S(5)` yet `min_h R_h = 8 > S(5)=7`. Thus even when the post-state term matches the global budget, some sibling along every realizing prefix forces a worse height. This is an explicit **sibling-incompatibility obstruction** to summaries that ignore sibling branch values: `d+F(P)` is necessary locally but not sufficient for root-prefix reachability.

### C4. History-sensitive root reachability interface: `σ(P)=R(P)=min_h R_h(P)` or full prefix state (deduction)

- **Interface idea.** Preserve exactly the root-relevant quantity by carrying the minimal worst-case root height over all histories reaching `P` (equivalently, track enough transcript/sibling information to recompute `R_h`).
- **What it preserves.** Root-prefix viability by definition: `P` is compatible with some root-optimal strategy of budget `k` iff `R(P) ≤ k` under the stated functional.
- **Limitation.** (deduction) This is not, by itself, a polynomial-state or chain/ordinal-product compositional recurrence; it packages the sibling constraint rather than eliminating it. Exhibiting a uniform succinct representation or checkable decomposition of `R(P)` over all `n` is precisely the missing step identified by this front (and is not supplied here).

### C5. Parallel/disjoint-union or comparability/module-style factorization as a root substitute (literature + deduction, negative)

- **Interface idea.** Exploit symmetries or graph/module structure of intermediate posets (parallel duality, comparability, Gallai modules, etc.) as a compositional interface.
- **Evidence against.** `docs/research-compression-next.md` shows comparability-graph/state collapses are not sound DP states for `F` in general (`n=7` value/transition obstructions), and `docs/research-orientation-modules.md` shows module reversal/MDT-up-to-reversal is not `F`-exact (`n=7`). These are post-state/value issues; they reinforce that even when strong local structure exists, transferring it to an **exact** global minimax recurrence is delicate, and none of these provides a root+sibling-preserving antichain decomposition.

## Evidence / falsification attempts

1. **Ordinal-sum exactness vs root non-decomposition (deduction/literature-backed).** Verified against `docs/research-series-decomposition.md`: maximal series decomposition is exact for `F` and poly-time detectable, but the antichain root is indecomposable and empirical/structural analysis shows no root decomposition; subset/correlated generalizations fail. This falsifies naive “compose root from ordinal sums” transfer.
2. **Sibling-branch obstruction (stated witness, packet/ledger basis).** Using `docs/research-packets.json`: `n=5`, `C2 ordinal A3`, `d+F=S(5)=7` but `min_h R_h=8>7`. **Falsification of C3:** equality of `(d,F(P))` (and hence equality of any coarser summary depending only on `P,F(P),d` but not sibling maxima) does not imply equality of root-prefix reachability/optimum.
3. **Symmetry/structure quotient checks (literature in-repo).** Reviewed `docs/research-compression-next.md` and `docs/research-orientation-modules.md`: exact quotients found (iso, dual, move-orbits as transitions) are constant-factor or move-level and do not yield a polynomial-state root composition; proposed stronger structural compressions have explicit small-`n` counterexamples. No experiment was run in this worktree; no claim depends on new computation.

**Extensibility assessment (conjecture/argument sketch, not a theorem).** The obstruction in (2) is structural: `R_h` is a max over the chosen path term and all earlier sibling terms. For a family where a budget-tight local remainder `F(P)=k-d` is reachable only through prefixes that force some sibling `Q_i^{sibling}` with `i+F(Q_i^{sibling})>k`, the same incompatibility persists. Embedding or scaling such a gadget inside larger instances would, if realizable as root-relevant reachable states, yield an extensible family defeating any interface that summarizes only `(P,d,F(P))`. Establishing an explicit infinite, root-relevant family with a uniform construction is **open here** (would strengthen the falsifier).

## Findings

- **Negative / falsifying (deduction, relative to named candidates).** C1 does not compose the antichain root (and parallel interleaving breaks additivity). C2–C3 do not preserve adversarial root viability because they omit sibling-branch obligations; the `n=5` `C2 ordinal A3` witness (`d+F=S(5)` but `min_h R_h=8>S(5)`) is an explicit sibling-incompatibility obstruction to post-state or depth+value summaries.
- **Positive (definitional/deductive clarity).** An exact root recurrence must be **history/sibling-aware**: it needs information sufficient to evaluate (or bound in the correct direction) `max(d+F(P), max_i i+F(Q_i^{sibling}))` over admissible transcripts, not merely `F(P)` or ordinal factors of `P`. C4 states the correct target object but not a polynomial-state compositional realization.
- **Inconclusive.** No compositional exact recurrence from chain or ordinal-product interfaces preserving root and both sibling branches was found. No proof was obtained that every such interface family must fail on some extensible root-relevant class (beyond the exhibited/recorded small obstruction), nor was a new uniform polynomial-state interface constructed.

## Obstacles

1. **Adversarial root vs local minimax gap.** `F(P)` optimizes the remainder assuming control of future comparisons from `P`; root optimality additionally constrains how `P` was reached and the cost of unchosen siblings.
2. **Non-decomposability at the root.** The natural algebraic decomposition (ordinal sum) applies to special states, not the antichain source; parallel composition lacks an exact additive/minimax law compatible with interleaving.
3. **Representation cost.** Carrying enough sibling/history information to be exact risks reverting to transcript/DAG/state enumeration, which is constrained by known barriers (e.g. large winning-frontier/state counts discussed in project docs) unless a new congruence is proved.
4. **Evidence granularity.** The cited `n=5` obstruction is taken from the recorded packet/ledger context in-repo; an independently reproduced, fully parameterized extensible family was not constructed in this session.

## Next decisive question

Can one define an explicit, checkable **sibling-closed interface** for root-prefixes—e.g. a congruence or invariant `I(h,P)` computable/representable from chain-ordinal local data such that `I(h,P)=I(h',P')` implies `R_h(P)=R_{h'}(P')`—and either (a) prove it has polynomially many classes for all `n` (yielding a genuine root-preserving compositional recurrence/certificate object), or (b) exhibit a concrete infinite root-relevant family forcing superpolynomially many `I`-classes for every sound candidate? A decisive answer to (b) for a broad, precisely specified interface family would upgrade the `n=5` sibling obstruction from a witness to a barrier for this front.
