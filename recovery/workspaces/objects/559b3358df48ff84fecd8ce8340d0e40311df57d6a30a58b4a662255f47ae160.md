## 5. Notable caveats and minor issues

- **Primary-source depth:** This audit did not independently fetch/inspect Wells 1965/1971, Peczarski 2012 PDF, Stober–Weiß 2023, Ford–Johnson 1959, or Hanamura–Iwata 2011. Where the artifact relies on them, the claims are consistent with `docs/literature.md` and `docs/research-n12-nontightness.md`; the Wells `S_24` level is especially dependent on secondary extraction with noted OCR ambiguity.
- **Terminology:** Artifact uses `F(P)`/`m(P)` context across cited notes; local notes define both (`F` in several notes, `m` in n12-nontightness). This is consistent, not incorrect.
- **Root vs local:** The artifact carefully distinguishes root antichain obstruction from local poset phenomena (`Merge(2,4)`, `W`). This distinction is important and correctly maintained.
- **No overclaim:** The artifact avoids claiming a complexity separation, a universal impossibility theorem for all invariants, or a verified `L1`/`Lk` explanation of `n=12`. These omissions/hedges are correct.
