## Findings by requested row/trace locus

### Row 5 preservation and row 12 pop: `BackE_Cursor_Preservation`

**Definition / deduction.** In v2, `CurExhausted` is a member of `Fld` and
`FrameFieldsEq'`; `MarkRetRejOn(p̄)` preserves every non-`Ret` field, expressly
including `HasCur, CurA, CurB, CurExhausted, PosF, UsedF, HasSel/SelA/SelB,
Parent, Ctrl, Mode`, and frame liveness. Both row 5 and row 12 put `{p̄,c̄}` in
the changed set, inactivate `c̄`, and mark `p̄`. Thus T-pop5 and T-pop12 preserve
the receiving parent’s complete cursor on the producer transition. Row 12’s
`NNI_c` is tested on the popped child, not the receiving parent; it does not
establish an NI/NNI fact about `p̄`.

**Open.** This is not yet the full preservation-until-consumer lemma. It needs
the all-row `Enc_Component_Closure_cursor`, `I_{M_D}` stack/parent/alias and
marker-receiver invariants, and no intervening overwrite before the next
consumer. Crucially, the realization’s `Rbar`, `E(Rbar)`, init and pop/marker
macro-steps still omit `CurExhausted` (realization §§1.2, 2.2, 3.2). Therefore
the schema proof does not establish preservation in that realization.

**Falsification-shape.** A reachable row-5/12 producer changes a parent cursor
field despite `MarkRetRejOn`, or a reachable intervening non-advance step changes
that field before consumption; alternatively, a realized macro-step drops the
exhaustion component while claiming to implement the v2 update. No concrete
`I_{M_D}`-reachable instance is shown. The realization mismatch is documented,
not a witness trace.

### Rows 10–12: `BackE_NI_NNI_Total_Complement`

**Definition / deduction.** V2 changes the consumers, not merely the cursor
encoding:

```text
NI_c  := ¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ ∃a∃b(CurNormalState(c̄,a,b) ∧ ∃x∃y NextIncomp_c(a,b;x,y))
NNI_c := CurExhausted(c̄) ∨ (¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ ∃a∃b(CurNormalState(c̄,a,b) ∧ NoNextIncomp_c(a,b)))
```

Given `CursorCoherent`, exhaustion implies `NNI_c ∧ ¬NI_c`. In the normal
case, the unique cursor and finite strict-total `Lex2` make the candidate set
empty or give it a unique least element. Hence `NI_c xor NNI_c`, with a unique
NI witness whenever NI holds. Row 10 requires NI; rows 11 and 12 require NNI,
with root/non-root split. So row 10 cannot fire on `CurExhausted`, and the
exhaustion consumer gap in the prior sentinel schema is closed **at schema
level**.

**Open.** The proof assumes reachable `CursorCoherent`, unique cursor and
`PosF`/`Incomp_c` alignment. Row 6 / eval initialization coherence is also
open. The realization still has the older consumers that do not consult
`CurExhausted`; it cannot be treated as an implementation of these revised
guards without an alignment change.

**Falsification-shape.** A reachable `BackE_RetRej_Top` has incoherent cursor,
both or neither NI/NNI, or multiple NI witnesses. No such reachable witness is
present in the audited sources.

### Rows 6/10 advance and rows 10–12 consume: `BackE_AfterSelected_NoReselect`

**Definition / deduction (T-sel).** Both selecting rows 6 and 10 require NI.
V2’s `SetParentBackE_Advance` has an explicit split:

- `¬IsLastLex2(x,y)` stores the unique `SuccLex2(x,y,x',y')` cursor with
  `¬CurExhausted'` and a normal cursor state. Strict `Lex2` order then puts any
  subsequent NI witness strictly after `(x,y)`, assuming this cursor is
  preserved and the successor predicates have their stipulated semantics.
- `IsLastLex2(x,y)` stores `CurA'=CurB'=MinKey` and
  `CurExhausted'`, yielding `CurExhaustedState'`. Since NI requires
  `¬CurExhausted`, row 10 is disabled; NNI holds directly, so rows 11/12 (root /
  non-root) consume it.

This resolves the old last-pair ordinary-sentinel reselect shape in the v2
schema. Row 10 cannot fire on an exhausted cursor; T-consume’s normal case
selects row 10 iff NI, otherwise row 11/12 iff NNI, conditional on matrix
exclusion and root determinacy.

**Open.** Need `SuccLex2` totality/functionality off the last pair,
`IsLastLex2` exactness, cursor preservation until consumption, no intervening
overwrite, reachable coherence, and realization alignment. These are not
established by the trace argument.

**Falsification-shape.** A reachable last-pair selection later reaches a
BackE-reject consumer with NI enabled, or a non-last selection’s preserved
successor cursor permits the selected pair again, or an intervening step
overwrites the cursor. No explicit reachable trace of these forms is given.

## Cross-check and conclusion

The checklist and normalized `Enc` define the earlier ordinary-pair
`SentinelLast` convention, with NI/NNI reading only `CurA/CurB`; the earlier
refutation correctly identifies that as under-instantiated. V2 is a substantive
schema revision: it adds `CurExhausted` to `Rbar/Fld` and frame equality,
defines normal/exhausted coherence, makes last-pair advance set exhaustion, and
revises NI/NNI and rows 6,10–12. The independent v2 audit confirms those
schema-level edits and independently leaves realization alignment and
`I_{M_D}` preservation open. Thus the old ordinary-sentinel counterexample does
not refute the revised schema. Conversely, the old normalized `Enc` and current
MDALT DTM realization do not instantiate the revised schema as-is.

**Final verdict:** supported conditionally at BackE v2 schema level; **no
explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable witness** for rows 5,6,10,11,12 was found. All three
requested obligations remain open as reachability/component-closure and
realization-alignment results. This audit does not discharge DET-SUCC and makes
no FO(PFP)/IFP, `P`, `NP`, or `coNP` claim.
