## 1. Question (falsifiable)

For small `n` (at least `n <= 6`, try `n = 7` if tractable), determine exact or lower-bound sizes of minimal permutation-framed DAG certificates among all optimal height-`S(n)` strategies (or among optimal-play canonical quotients), and compare to the optimal decision-tree size `2*n! - 1`. Identify whether compression is possible, how much, and what structural features enable or limit it; extrapolate carefully to all-`n` only as conjecture/obstacle with evidence.

**Why it matters for `S(n)` (per AGENTS/problem):** a polynomial-size, polynomial-time-checkable certificate family for `SORT-BOUND-U = {1^n#1^k : S(n) <= k}` at budget `k=S(n)` would place `SORT-BOUND-U` in `NP`; conversely, structural lower bounds/obstacles on certificate compression constrain that route. This is an upper-bound/certificate-frontier question, not an unconditional lower-bound question for the unary language.

## 2. Definitions and baseline

- `[definition]` `S(n)` is the minimum worst-case number of comparisons of a deterministic adaptive comparison decision tree sorting every permutation of `n` distinct labelled keys (`docs/problem.md`).
- `[literature]` Known values used: `S(1..6)=0,1,3,5,7,10`, `S(7)=13` (`docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/literature.md` context in repo docs).
- `[definition]` **Optimal decision tree size:** a correct comparison tree has `n!` leaves (one per input order) and `n!-1` internal binary comparison nodes, total `2*n! - 1` nodes, independent of height except correctness/optimality of the strategy. An optimal height-`S(n)` strategy is such a tree with depth `S(n)`.
- `[definition]` **Permutation-framed DAG certificate** (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py`): an acyclic directed graph, nodes numbered topologically with root 0. Internal node: `compare [a,b]` and edges `lt`/`gt` each giving `next` and slot permutation `perm`. Leaf: total guaranteed order. Verifier propagates guaranteed strict partial orders, intersects incoming branch relations at joins, applies outcome + transitive closure + permutation along edges, rejects impossible/cyclic/non-sorting cases, checks depth `<= budget`. Soundness and polynomial-time checking in explicit graph size are implemented/claimed for the checker; **all-`n` polynomial-size completeness at `S(n)` is open**.
- `[definition]` **Poset-state hash-consed DAG** (`scripts/strategy_dag_probe.py`): nodes are transitive-closure poset states reached by optimal play; identical states share a node. This measures state-sharing, not necessarily a valid framed instruction-merge in the checker grammar without an explicit shared compare/perm witness.
- `[definition]` **Optimal-play canonical quotient:** reachable optimal states modulo label relabelling (`S_n` isomorphism/canonical form) (`strategy_dag_probe.py`, `tests/test_strategy_dag.py`).

## 5. Minimal permutation-framed DAG certificate size: analysis

### 5.1 Lower-bound/equality evidence for optimal strategies (`n<=6`)

`[deduction]+[experiment]` **Claim A (poset-distinctness barrier for state-sharing).** In a permutation-framed strategy where each feasible execution path corresponds to a root-to-leaf comparison history, a *sound merge into the same instruction node* requires that the intersected guaranteed relation at that join still admits the same next comparison/permutation behavior and completion within the remaining budget. A necessary condition for reusing an identical reached *state* by simple hash-consing is that the same transitive-closure poset recurs. For one optimal policy, experiments show no poset recurrence for `n<=6` (`dag1_nodes = 2*n!-1`), hence no compression from poset-state identification is possible for those strategies.

`[deduction]` **Claim B (join difficulty in framed grammar).** Even if different histories reach a join node, the checker sets `relations[child] = intersection(incoming relations)` (after local closure/rename along edges). Intersection weakens knowledge (fewer guaranteed comparisons). Therefore a join can be valid only when the downstream framed subgraph is correct under strictly weaker information; it cannot rely on branch-specific disjunctions except those preserved by intersection. This is a structural limitation on compression, consistent with the note that arbitrary correct comparator networks may fail if outcomes are joined immediately (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §1).

`[conjecture, evidence-backed for small n]` **Minimal framed DAG among all optimal height-`S(n)` strategies.** For `n<=6`, no certificate smaller than the full optimal decision tree was found or constructively indicated: single optimal policies already force `2*n!-1` distinct states; all-optimal unions show shared states but exploiting them as a single framed instruction DAG of height `S(n)` would require a common sound compare/perm continuation under intersected relations, which is highly constrained. **Best supported size for an explicit optimal framed strategy certificate in this study is `2*n!-1` nodes for `n<=6`** (the tree itself, which is a valid framed DAG). I did **not** prove this is an absolute minimum over all framed DAG encodings/joins for `n>=4`; it is reported as the exact observed/constructed optimum within examined optimal-policy representations and a lower-bound pressure point from state-distinctness.

### 5.2 Canonical quotient view

`[experiment]` Canonical quotients compress state *descriptions* dramatically (e.g. 134 classes for `n=6` all-optimal union vs 65551 states vs 1439-node optimal tree). `[deduction]` However, a verifier-checkable permutation-framed certificate over labelled elements must still represent enough comparison/permutation/control information to justify every feasible execution path to a total order within budget. Quotienting by `S_n` can suggest a schematic/parameterized witness, but converting a quotient into an explicit framed DAG certificate generally requires either:
- explicit orbit/coset or relabelling data on edges (not free), or
- a proof that one canonical representative's local move is uniformly sound after intersection/permutation.

Thus small quotient size is **necessary-but-not-sufficient evidence** for small framed certificates and does not by itself establish polynomial-size framed completeness.

## 7. Structural features enabling/limiting compression

**Enabling (state/description level, `[experiment]+[literature/deduction]`):**
- Large overlap of reachable posets across different optimal move choices (all-optimal union much smaller than unshared all-optimal tree).
- Strong `S_n` symmetry (small canonical quotients).
- Non-optimal or structured algorithms can admit polynomial framed DAGs: e.g. triangular bubble witness in checker format has `n(n-1)/2 + 1` nodes but larger depth; `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` describes paper constructions for merge-sort-like and rank-envelope/join schemes with polynomial node counts (pending full audits as noted there).

**Limiting (for optimal-height framed certificates, `[deduction]+[experiment]`):**
- **No repeated poset states in single optimal policies for `n<=6`**, so hash-consing of states alone yields zero compression.
- **Intersection weakens guarantees:** framed joins cannot preserve branch-specific disjunctive information except what survives intersection, making height-`S(n)` completion after a merge hard.
- **Leaf/output resolution:** a complete sorting certificate must certify a total order for every input permutation; compressing below tree form requires reusing certified subcomputations without losing the permutation-specific result.
- **Optimality gap vs structured methods:** constructions with easy joins (merge/bubble/rank envelopes) may exceed `S(n)` or be unproved at exact optimum for all `n`; exact optimal strategies may lack the regular structure those joins exploit.

## 8. Extrapolation to all `n` (conjecture/obstacle only)

`[conjecture]` **C1.** For optimal height-`S(n)` adaptive strategies, minimal explicit permutation-framed DAG certificates remain exponential-scale in `n` (on the order of `n!` up to lower-order factors), i.e. comparable to `2*n!-1`, because path/state distinctness and intersection-limited joins prevent systematic sharing.

`[conjecture]` **C2.** Symmetry quotients and state-union overlap do not by themselves yield polynomial-size explicit framed certificates at height `S(n)` without additional all-`n` structural theorems supplying sound joinable control states.

**Evidence:** exact/observed behavior for `n<=6` (no single-policy state sharing; joins constrained), exponential leaf set `n!`, and the open status of optimal-size completeness in `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`. **Counter-evidence/uncertainty:** polynomial framed constructions exist for some non-optimal or specially structured procedures; no general lower-bound theorem for all framed encodings of all optimal strategies is proved here. `n=7` was not computed, so extrapolation beyond `n=6` remains tentative.

## 10. Bottom line

- `[experiment]` For `n<=6`, one optimal policy yields a poset-state DAG of size `2*n!-1` (no hash-consing compression) and `n!` leaves; canonical quotient classes are small (`<=27`) but not direct framed certificates.
- `[experiment]` Union of all optimal strategies shares many states (`65551` nodes at `n=6`) with tiny canonical quotient (`134`), but converting this into a smaller **sound permutation-framed DAG certificate at height `S(n)`** is obstructed by intersection/weakening at joins.
- `[deduction/conjecture]` Within this evidence, minimal explicit framed certificates among optimal height-`S(n)` strategies appear to remain at tree scale `~2*n!-1` for small `n`, with no demonstrated compression; extrapolation to all `n` is an obstacle/conjecture, not a theorem.
- `n=7`: not tractable/computed in this session.
