# Succinct certificates and ESO definability for `SORT-BOUND-U` (paper-level assessment)

**Scope (definition).** `SORT-BOUND-U = { 1^n # 1^k : S(n) <= k }` over the fixed adaptive comparison-tree model; input length `N = n + k + 1` (`docs/problem.md`). This report addresses two questions from the prompt: (Q1) an explicit succinct NP certificate/verifier beyond explicit framed-DAG enumeration, or a concrete structural obstruction for optimal budgets; (Q2) feasibility of an explicit ESO definition on ordered unary structures as a new upper bound, with encoding/resources measured in `N`.

**Labels.** Claims are marked **[definition]**, **[literature]**, **[deduction]**, **[conjecture]**, **[experiment]** (reported from repository experiments/notes, not newly run here), or **[open]**. No Lean, no repository edits beyond this report.

---

## 1. Precise claim / answer

### Q1 (succinct NP certificate, optimal budgets)

- **[open; no theorem either way]** It is not known whether, for every `n`, some height-`S(n)` (or height-`k >= S(n)`) strategy for sorting `n` elements admits an explicit certificate of size `poly(N)` verifiable in time `poly(N)` for `SORT-BOUND-U`. Consequently `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP` is not established by a succinct certificate, and no proof of `SORT-BOUND-U ∉ NP` is available.
- **[deduction/literature, from `docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`]** The naive explicit witness (a reduced correct comparison decision tree) is *not* a polynomial NP certificate on informative unary inputs: it has exactly `2*n! - 1` nodes, while `m = n + k + 1 = Θ(n log n)` when `k` lies in the informative window, hence `n! = 2^{Θ(m)}`. Its standard verification is polynomial in the tree size, yielding at most a naive `NEXP` verifier, not `NP`.
- **[deduction/literature, from `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`]** The permutation-framed DAG/strategy grammar is a sound, locally checkable certificate format (polynomial-time checkable in explicit graph size and `n`). Polynomial-size framed DAGs exist for some all-`n` *upper-bound* procedures (merge sort, and plausibly a fully audited Ford–Johnson/rank-envelope construction for its standard bound), but **exact-optimal-budget completeness** (`S(n)` for all `n`) is open.
- **[literature/deduction] Concrete obstructions/constraints for optimal budgets (not a proof of impossibility):** (i) any optimal or near-optimal certificate must be compatible with `S(n) - log2(n!) = o(n)` (Sergeev; `docs/literature.md`, `docs/research-sergeev-asymptotic.md`), while fixed merge/FJ-style architectures incur a linear or otherwise nonzero comparison surplus and cannot be exact for all sufficiently large relevant `n` (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §2). (ii) The winning-frontier barrier gives `2^{(1/4-o(1)) n log^2 n}` nonisomorphic winning states at a fixed depth even at budget `S(n)` when `S(n)-C(n)=o(n)`, ruling out **full winning-frontier/transcript enumeration** at the optimum (`docs/research-winning-frontier-barrier.md`, summarized in `docs/research-plan.md`); it does **not** by itself rule out a compressed DAG whose joins forget distinctions. (iii) Sergeev's group-insertion method stores pending keys in coupled interval containers; no polynomial-state, sound join/grammar realizing its `o(n)` surplus as an explicit all-`n` framed certificate is known (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §5). (iv) `SORT-BOUND-U` is sparse, so a many-one NP-hardness proof would imply `P = NP` (Mahaney) — a barrier to hardness, not evidence for/against membership (`docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`, `docs/problem.md`).

**Net assessment (deduction from above).** Beyond explicit tree/frontier enumeration, framed DAG compression is the main *candidate* succinct-certificate route, but no explicit all-`n` construction at exact optimal budgets is known and known optimal/near-optimal algorithmic structures have not been certified in a polynomial-state grammar. There is no identified concrete structural theorem that forces every sound optimal-budget certificate to be super-polynomial in `N`; the obstructions are to specific enumeration and specific architectures.

### Q2 (explicit ESO on ordered unary structures)

- **[literature/deduction]** By Fagin's theorem, existential second-order logic (ESO) on finite structures captures `NP`. Therefore an explicit ESO sentence defining `SORT-BOUND-U` on an appropriate ordered finite structure encoding inputs of length `N` would be equivalent, in strength, to proving `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP` via an explicit polynomially verifiable witness. It would be a **new upper bound relative to the currently known `PSPACE`** upper bound only in the sense of improving the class to `NP`; it is not a separate weaker consequence than NP membership.
- **[literature/deduction]** `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` is established (`docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`, `docs/problem.md`), and on ordered structures `PSPACE` corresponds to `FO(PFP)` / `FO(IFP)` under standard descriptive-complexity capture theorems; `docs/research-plan.md` notes that building and validating an explicit `FO(PFP)` formula is separate work and that one must not infer `FO(LFP)`/`ESO` from a compact informal recursion.
- **[open/conjecture-level feasibility]** An explicit ESO definition specialized to the exact minimax value `S(n)` is not currently feasible as a completed construction: it would require existentially quantifying polynomially-bounded relations (in `|U| = Θ(N)`) that encode a sound, complete, height-`<=k` sorting strategy/certificate and a first-order (on the ordered structure, with those relations) verification that every comparison outcome/legal permutation path reaches a total order within budget `k`. This is essentially Q1 in logical form. No such explicit formula/witness encoding is known.

## 2. Argument / construction

### 2.1 Q1: what "succinct certificate beyond explicit enumeration" must do

**[definition]** For a yes-instance `x = 1^n # 1^k`, `N=|x|`. An NP-style certificate `w` must satisfy `|w| <= p(N)` and be checkable by a deterministic verifier in time `poly(N,|w|)` with `V(x,w)=1 iff S(n)<=k`.

**[deduction]** The minimax/alternating characterization is: sorter (existential, per reached state) chooses a comparison, adversary (universal) chooses outcome, depth `<=k`, all leaves total orders (`docs/research-certificates-barriers.md` Claim A). An NP certificate can remove alternation only by committing to the sorter's choices as a single object (strategy/tree/DAG/program/invariant) that the verifier can check against *all* adversary outcomes.

**[deduction, explicit-tree baseline]** A reduced correct tree distinguishes all `n!` permutations, so `>=n!` leaves and `2*n! - 1` nodes total (`docs/research-certificates-barriers.md` Claim C; consistent with small-`n` experiment there: leaves `n!`, nodes `2n!-1` for `n<=6`). For informative `k` with `S(n)<=k=O(n log n)`, `N=Θ(n log n)` and `log(n!) = Θ(n log n)=Θ(N)`, hence the witness is exponential in `N`. This rules out explicit tree enumeration, but not alternative encodings.

**[deduction, framed-DAG candidate]** A permutation-framed DAG represents comparison nodes, outcome arcs carrying slot permutations, and joins with an intersection/guaranteed-relation invariant; soundness and polynomial-time local checking in explicit graph length are argued in `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §1. "Beyond explicit framed-DAG enumeration" can be read as: beyond listing every reached state/transcript individually (the full frontier). A polynomial-size *DAG with joins* is precisely a non-enumerative compression attempt: many histories may merge if a common sound guarantee suffices. Thus framed DAGs are the relevant explicit succinct format; completeness at `S(n)` is the missing theorem.

**[deduction, why optimal budgets are hard]**
- **Architectural incompatibility:** merge-sort framed DAGs achieve `O(n^2)` nodes but path length `Θ(n log n)` (not optimal in the tight sense needed as `n` grows), and the split-merge-of-halves architecture has comparison count `n log2 n - n + 1` for powers of two, exceeding `log2(n!)` by `(log2 e - 1)n + O(log n)`; Sergeev gives `S(n)=log2(n!)+o(n)`, so that architecture cannot witness exact optimality for all large powers of two (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §2, `docs/literature.md`).
- **Rank-envelope/FJ:** a plausible polynomial framed certificate for the Ford–Johnson upper bound (not `S(n)`) is described, with Jacobsthal schedule and relaxed windows, pending full audit (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §3). This is evidence that *some* nontrivial adaptive strategies admit compact DAGs, but not evidence for optimal `S(n)`.
- **State explosion vs compression:** reachable labelled posets are `2^{Θ(n^2)}` in general (`docs/research-certificates-barriers.md` §5, OEIS A001035 for `n<=6` in experiment); the winning-frontier barrier shows even the optimal-budget relevant frontier can be `2^{Ω(n log^2 n)}` in count (`docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/research-winning-frontier-barrier.md`). Forgetful joins can in principle represent many extensions/states implicitly (the rank-envelope note shows exponentially many history types may collapse with small information loss), but no theorem guarantees such collapse for *some* optimal root strategy for all `n`.

**[conjecture]** A plausible but unproved route would be a polynomial-state framed/grammar certificate for a Sergeev-compatible or otherwise `o(n)`-surplus optimal construction; falsifying any specific proposed grammar (showing forced super-polynomial control states or unsound joins) would be informative, but does not settle Q1 globally.

### 2.2 Q2: ESO on ordered unary structures — encoding and resources

**[definition] Encoding/structure.** Let the input be the word `w = 1^n # 1^k` with `|w|=N=n+k+1`. A standard choice is the finite relational structure
`A_w = (U, <, U_n, U_k, Sep)` where `U = {0,...,N-1}` in order `<` corresponds to positions of `w`, `U_n(i)` (resp. `U_k`, `Sep`) holds iff position `i` is in the `1^n` block (resp. `1^k` block, the `#`). This is an *ordered* structure of cardinality `N`; all unary predicates are directly definable from the string and have total size `O(N)` bits. (Equivalent encodings—e.g. two unary predicates marking block lengths, or a successor structure—are first-order interdefinable on ordered domains and do not change NP/ESO status; precision here is: domain size and all basic relations are `Θ(N)`.)

**[literature/deduction]** An ESO sentence has the form `∃R1 ... ∃Rm ψ(R1,...,Rm,<,U_n,U_k,Sep)` with `ψ` first-order. Existentially quantified relations over `U` have arity-fixed tables of total size `|U|^{O(1)} = N^{O(1)}`; `FO` model checking on a fixed sentence over an explicitly given ordered structure of size `N` is in polynomial time in the structure size. Hence any explicit ESO definition of `SORT-BOUND-U` on `A_w` yields a polynomial-size witness (the relations) and polynomial-time verification, i.e. `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP` (Fagin correspondence).

**[deduction] Resource accounting in `N`.**
- To encode comparisons over `n` keys, element indices require `O(log n) <= O(log N)` bits; `k` is the depth budget and `k <= N`.
- A strategy certificate must constrain choices for all adversary branches up to depth `k`. A direct tree/table has size exponential in `N` in the worst/informative case (as in §2.1). A succinct ESO witness would therefore need quantified relations representing a compressed object (e.g. framed DAG nodes/edges/permutations, or an invariant system) of total arity-bounded size `poly(N)` such that `ψ` checks: initial state empty/antichain, each comparison is between valid elements, each feasible outcome transition/permutation is sound, joins preserve only justified guarantees, depth `<=k`, and terminals imply a total order.
- **[open]** Constructing such `R_i, ψ` explicitly for the exact predicate `S(n)<=k` is exactly the succinct-certificate/design problem: the logical formalism does not by itself supply the compression. Checking "for all permutations / all adversary paths" must be *implicit* in the certified invariant, not enumerated, to stay within `poly(N)` witness + `FO` verification.

**[deduction] Why not a "new" easy upper bound from PSPACE.** `PSPACE` membership gives `FO(PFP)` definability on ordered structures in principle (`docs/research-plan.md`), but `FO(PFP)` captures `PSPACE`, not `NP`. Deriving an explicit ESO (`NP`) sentence would be a strict strengthening in conclusion and cannot be read off from a compact alternation/recursion without proving the underlying existential witness is polynomially bounded.

**[conjecture]** A concrete intermediate milestone (weaker than full all-`n` optimality, but explicit) would be an ESO definition of a *provable upper-bound* language, e.g. membership witnessed by an explicit polynomial framed DAG for merge sort or an audited FJ-bound certificate, parameterized as `1^n#1^k` with `k` above that bound. This would formalize "certificate in NP for those instances" but would **not** define `S(n)<=k` at the exact optimum except where the bound is tight.

## 3. Falsification / small-n checks (where relevant)

- **[experiment, from `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`]** For `n<=6`, reachable posets under minimax DP are `1,3,19,219,4231,130023` (OEIS A001035), optimal reduced trees have `leaves = n!` and `nodes = 2*n!-1`. This falsifies any hope that the generic reachable state space is small and calibrates explicit-tree non-succinctness; it does not address optimal-play-only or DAG-compressed counts.
- **[literature]** Known exact values `S(1..11,20,21)=C(n)=⌈log2 n!⌉`, `S(12)=30>C(12)=29`, `S(16)=46,S(17)=50,S(18)=54`, etc. (`docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/literature.md`, `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`) are consistency constraints: any proposed optimal-budget succinct certificate scheme must reproduce these budgets and, in particular, cannot rely on the information bound alone at `n>=12` cases where it is not tight.
- **[deduction/falsification target]** A proposed explicit all-`n` framed grammar claiming optimality would be refuted by: (a) a soundness failure on any checked instance, (b) a path-length/comparison-count surplus exceeding `S(n)` for some known `n`, or (c) a demonstrated super-polynomial blowup in the grammar state/control size as a function of `n` (hence `N`). No such completed refutation or validation of an optimal all-`n` grammar is recorded in the read documents.
- **No new computational experiment was run** for this report (and none was necessary beyond the cited calibrations for a paper-level answer).

## 4. Consequence for membership / barriers

- **[deduction]** Establishing an explicit polynomial-size, polynomial-time-checkable certificate for all yes-instances of `SORT-BOUND-U` (framed DAG, ESO witness, or other) would prove **`SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP`**, a strict improvement over the known `PSPACE` upper bound and a genuine progress result for the unary frontier (`docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`). Per `docs/problem.md`, proving `SORT-BOUND-U ∉ NP` would imply `NP ≠ PSPACE` and is out of scope as an unconditional separation target here.
- **[deduction]** Establishing an explicit `ESO` definition on the ordered unary structure of length `N` has the same complexity consequence as the previous bullet (`NP` membership) by Fagin's theorem; it would be valuable as an explicit descriptive-complexity witness but not a different class upper bound than NP.
- **[literature/deduction]** Barriers unchanged by this assessment: sparsity blocks many-one NP-hardness of `SORT-BOUND-U` unless `P=NP` (Mahaney); tally/unary nature gives `P/poly` trivially so nonuniform circuit lower bounds cannot separate it from `P`; the winning-frontier theorem blocks exhaustive optimal-frontier enumeration but not all compression schemes (`docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`).
- **[deduction]** Current status remains: `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` known; membership in `NP`, `coNP`, `P` open; no succinct upper/lower-bound certificate known; explicit all-`n` optimal framed-DAG completeness open.

## 5. Next decisive obstacle

1. **[open, decisive for Q1]** Either (a) produce an explicit, sound, all-`n` polynomial-size framed/grammar certificate (or equivalent ESO witness relations) whose verified depth is `S(n)` (or decides `S(n)<=k` on the informative window), with a proof that graph/witness size is `poly(n)=poly(N)` and verification is `poly(N)`; or (b) identify and prove a structural obstruction showing that *every* sound certificate in that explicit grammar class must be super-polynomial at optimal budgets for infinitely many `n`. A partial but sharp target is to formalize a **Sergeev-compatible polynomial-state grammar** with `o(n)` surplus or give a concrete no-go for a precisely specified such grammar (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §5).
2. **[open, discriminating experiment/analysis]** Measure or bound the number/structure of states on *some* height-`S(n)` optimal plays (optimal-play state/DAG width), not all reachable posets, for feasible `n` and asymptotically: polynomial behavior would make succinct DAG/ESO witnesses plausible; provably super-polynomial optimal-play width in every sound compression model would be a concrete obstruction (cf. `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md` §8, `docs/research-plan.md`).
3. **[open, for Q2 specifically]** As a stepping stone, write an explicit ESO sentence over `(U,<,U_n,U_k,Sep)` defining a *certified upper bound* (e.g. merge-sort or audited FJ bound) and verify its resource bounds in `N`; this would calibrate the logical encoding (comparisons, permutations, joins, depth) without claiming optimality, and would isolate what extra ingredient is missing for `S(n)` itself.

**Bottom line (deduction).** A serious paper-level path to `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP` exists in principle only via a genuinely new succinct strategy certificate (framed-DAG/ESO-equivalent) compatible with optimal budgets; no such explicit construction or structural impossibility theorem is currently known. The immediate blocker is not logic/encoding overhead (which is polynomial once a witness exists) but the absence of a proved polynomial-size, all-`n`, optimal-budget representation of comparison-sorting minimax strategies.
