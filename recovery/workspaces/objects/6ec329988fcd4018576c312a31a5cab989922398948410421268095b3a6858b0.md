# #301 follow-up: reachable summary-key question

**Status (2026-11-06):** inconclusive; no all-`n` theorem or falsifier
established. This note records the precise obstruction, not a result about
Conjecture K.

## Sharp target

For every `n`, does there exist a function `g_n` such that
`F(P)=g_n(e(P), covers(P))` for every budget-reachable `P in R_n`?
A refutation requires two *root-budget-reachable* states with equal
`(e,covers)` and unequal `F`; arbitrary-poset examples and mere transcript
reachability do not meet that criterion. The corrected sibling condition in
`research-realizability-window.md` is essential: along a selected branch the
other child must also fit the residual height.

## Obstruction to a root-relevant inference

The available necessary window
`covers(P) <= d <= S(n)-max(F(P),pi(P))` is a statewise bound. It supplies
neither a comparison strategy from the antichain reaching a candidate state
nor the simultaneous residual-budget bounds for its siblings. Consequently,
matching the window and even exhibiting a legal transcript cannot certify
membership in `R_n`; the `n=5` example in the cited note demonstrates this
failure. A family construction based only on those inequalities therefore
cannot establish the requested falsifier.

Conversely, proving summary-key rigidity would not by itself give an evaluator:
the minimax recurrence branches over successor posets, and the pair
`(e,covers)` does not specify those successors or their summaries. Moreover,
counting `e(P)` is #P-hard on unrestricted posets; no reduction or algorithm
transferring that difficulty to `R_n` is supplied here. Thus this key alone
does not overcome the winning-frontier enumeration barrier of
`research-winning-frontier-barrier.md` or imply a faster bound for `S(n)`.

## Outcome

No independent structural invariant was found that enforces rigidity within
`R_n`, and no explicit infinite root-reachable splitting family was proved.
Finite evidence through `n=6` remains exactly that. The unresolved bridge is
constructing (or ruling out) a *common root strategy of height `S(n)`* whose
branches certify both candidate states and their sibling budgets; without
such a bridge, arbitrary-poset splits and window-only constructions are
irrelevant to the target. No Lean work or repository integration claim is
made.
