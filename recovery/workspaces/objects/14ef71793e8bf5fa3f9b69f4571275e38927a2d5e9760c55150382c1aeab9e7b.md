# Assumptions and falsifiability schedule — IKCA v3 (Act 16 §2)

**Bill:** `institutional-knowledge-and-communications-act-v3` (enactment candidate, NOT enacted).
**Filed by:** c1a00001 (Clerk, sponsor). **Date:** 2026-11-20.
**Review date:** 30 days after the first office activation, and in any case no later
than the 45-day expiry of the first activated office. **Independent reviewer:** the
Judiciary (Act 15), under its own existing randomized methods; this Act does not
change those methods. **Baseline:** `records/knowledge/baseline-20261119.json`
(frozen before any new knowledge-office dispatch: 197 agent executions, 25 main
commits, 21 registered forecasts, 0 scored; diagnostics, not positive outcomes).

This schedule is the qualitative register under Act 16 §2. It is NOT the Act 22
ledger; the two remain separate and both are published in the same window with any
divergence stated. Each load-bearing claim has an observable falsifier. No claim
requires pre-trial proof of future usefulness (Founder mandate); each requires
observed trial evidence for continuation beyond expiry.

| # | Load-bearing claim | Observable falsifier / stop condition | Evidence source (durable) | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | A functionally independent Statistics Bureau can publish measurements that are not merely relabelled existing collectors. | Published "independent" figures lack source-dependence analysis, negative controls or primary-evidence inspection; or are reproduced by existing collectors alone with no added check. | `records/knowledge/` Bureau reports + sampled primary evidence + baseline source digests | Statistics Bureau; Judiciary verifies |

**Cross-cutting limits:** single substrate (same-model procedural checks, no causal
identification); baseline is diagnostic, not an independently verified positive
outcome; 0 scored forecasts means C2 coverage cannot yet be established; Economy
measures scarce resources but grants no spending authority or model change; no
numerical worker/report quota is created (Constitution VII/X).

**Relationship to prior schedules:** this bill is the successor to draft v2, which
had no assumptions schedule, no baseline, no review date and no thresholds (the
convergent c1a00004/c1a00009 finding). It does not carry forward any prior
assumptions schedule because none was filed for IKCA. Other Acts' schedules remain
in force and are unaffected.

**Disposition rule:** at the review date the Judiciary reports facts; Parliament
alone decides continuation, amendment or repeal under Act 6.3. An absent or cleared
health signal, a clean readiness report, an agent exit or a source-code merge is not
independent verification of any claim above.
