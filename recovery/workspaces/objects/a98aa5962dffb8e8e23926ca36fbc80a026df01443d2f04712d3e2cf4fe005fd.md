## Question

For `SORT-BOUND-U = {1^n # 1^k : S(n) <= k}` on the intended ordered word interpretation of `w = 1^n # 1^k` (`|W| = N = n+k+1`):

1. Is the claimed blocker sound: `PosOf`/`P_C` cannot decode arbitrary `G(n,k)` poset content from a fixed-arity configuration code `d̄ ∈ W^r` because there are `N^r` codes but `2^{Θ(n^2)}` relevant poset states?
2. Is the monotonicity dependency explicit and correct: the acceptance operator `ψ` is positive in `AC`, but semantic monotonicity / intended fixed-point meaning requires `P_C/t_C/a_C/b_C/m_C`, `Yield*`, and `Acc` to be `AC`-independent (in particular `P_C/PosOf` not depending on `AC`)?
3. Are PFP/IFP/LFP claims stated without overclaiming a smaller class for `SORT-BOUND-U` (no `P`, no FO(LFP) on the bare word, no NP/coNP membership from positivity/stratification)?
4. Are the proposed closure paths concretely discharged or precisely left open?
   - simultaneous PFP on interpreted configuration + cell relations,
   - explicit interpreted domain for exponentially many states,
   - explicit ATM encoding proved equivalent to `G(n,k)` acceptance.

## Executive verdict

**The blocker and dependency diagnoses are largely sound; no closure path is concretely discharged.**

- The `decode-explicit` note correctly identifies the decisive gap left after expanding control components: arbitrary transitively closed partial orders on `Key` are not decodable as complete configuration content from `d̄ ∈ W^r` alone when `r` is fixed, because `|W|^r = N^r = (n+k+1)^r = poly(N)$ in the natural regime while the relevant poset/state space is exponential in `n^2` (and `n$ can be `Θ(N)$ on informative unary instances). This is a valid obstruction to the naive “`PosOf(d̄,u,v)$ gives the whole poset from the fixed control tuple” closure. `[deduction]`
- The monotonicity/`AC`-independence condition is stated in the right form. `ψ$ is syntactically positive in `AC$ in the proposed schema, but that alone does not make an arbitrary decoded operator a monotone acceptance computation unless the decoding/transition predicates are fixed independently of `AC$ (or the full simultaneous operator is proved monotone). `[deduction]`
- The smaller-class caveats are mostly handled appropriately in `decode-explicit`: it explicitly denies `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ P`, FO(LFP) on the bare word, or NP/coNP membership from positivity/stratification, and it limits itself to the established `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` / FO(PFP)-in-principle consequence. `[literature,deduction]`
- No proposed closure path is concretely discharged. `decode-explicit` supplies an explicit control/transition schema conditional on `P_C$, but not a complete configuration representation with semantic equivalence to `G(n,k)$. `fixed-tuple-closure$ moves toward the standard interpreted/tuple-coding normal form but leaves `EncodesPair/EncodesElt/PosOf/TimeOf/AOf/BOf/ModeOf$ and the final simultaneous/PFP semantics unexpanded/unproved; `closure-audit$ correctly classifies it as a strengthened schematic closure, not a finished formula-level proof. `[deduction,open]`

## 1. Blocker: `PosOf/P_C` vs. `N^r` codes

### Assessment: sound as a fixed-tuple content-decoding obstruction

The core claim in `decode-explicit` §7.1 is:

> for fixed `r`, `d̄ ∈ W^r$ gives `|W|^r = N^r$ codes, but `G(n,k)$ may require representing `2^{Θ(n^2)}$ poset states; hence no FO `PosOf(d̄,u,v)$ over the bare word can decode all needed poset states from `d̄$ alone.

This is **sound under the intended reading**.

- `A(w)$ has domain `W={1,...,N}$, `N=n+k+1$. A fixed-arity tuple `d̄∈W^r$ has at most `N^r$ values. `[definition,deduction]`
- For informative unary inputs in the nontrivial regime, `k$ is bounded by the merge/upper-bound window and `N = Θ(n log n)$ or at least `N = Θ(n+k)$ with `n$ linear in `N$ on many relevant instances; in particular the construction cannot assume `n=o(\sqrt{\log N})$. `SORT-BOUND-U$ is the unary threshold language, so the obstacle must be addressed for all well-formed `(n,k)$ pairs, including those with `n$ comparable to `N$. `[definition,deduction]`
- The game state includes an arbitrary transitively closed strict partial order on `Key$, `|Key|=n$. The number of labelled posets / relevant order-theoretic states is exponential in `n^2$ in general (OEIS A001035, Kleitman–Rothschild asymptotics are cited in repo context notes; the exact constant is not needed here). Thus `N^r$ cannot index all such states for fixed `r$ once `n$ is large relative to any fixed polynomial in `N$. `[literature,deduction]`

**What the blocker does and does not establish:**

- It **does** block the concrete closure attempted by “expand `TimeOf/AOf/BOf/ModeOf` and also decode arbitrary `PosOf$ from the same fixed control tuple `d̄\in W^6$ (or `W^r$)”. That route cannot represent the full `G(n,k)$ state space. `[deduction]`
- It **does not** block FO(PFP) definability of `SORT-BOUND-U$ on ordered structures. FO(PFP) may use fixed-arity relations over the input structure and standard tuple/cell/configuration coding; the repo’s `research-certificates-barriers.md` records `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE$, and FO(PFP)=PSPACE on ordered finite structures gives definability in principle. `[literature]`
- It **does not** show that a fixed-arity relation `R ⊆ W^r$ cannot encode a *particular* poset: `Pos(u,v)$ over `Key\times Key$ is already a 2-ary relation. The obstruction is to using a single fixed tuple `d̄$ as the complete configuration name/content source for *all* states while also obtaining a closed PFP over configuration codes without the missing state representation. `[deduction]`

**Precision note.** The argument is strongest as a counting/representation obstruction to complete fixed-tuple content decoding. It should not be phrased as “FO cannot define `PosOf$ over the word at all” in an unrestricted sense; `PosOf$ can be FO relative to an explicit relation encoding a poset. The missing object is the explicit, complete, FO-definable configuration encoding whose acceptance PFP computes the `G(n,k)$ winner.

## 2. Monotonicity dependency (`AC$-independent decoding)

### Assessment: correctly identified and necessary

`decode-explicit` §7 states:

- `ψ(AC,d̄)$ is syntactically positive in `AC$. `[deduction]`
- Semantic monotonicity of `F(X)=\{d̄ \mid (A(w),X)\models ψ(AC,d̄)\}$ holds if predicates used inside `ψ$ except `AC$ are interpreted independently of `X$ / `AC$. In particular `P_C/PosOf$ must not depend on `AC$. `[deduction]`

This is the right dependency.

- Positivity in the relation symbol `AC$ is a syntactic property of the displayed formula; it prevents direct negative uses of `AC$. It is not, by itself, a completed proof that the induced operator on the intended configuration space is the game acceptance operator. `[deduction]`
- If `P_C, TimeOf/AOf/BOf/ModeOf$, `Yield*`, or `Acc$ were defined by auxiliary fixed points, by lookup into `AC$, or by relations whose interpretation varies with the `AC$ iteration in an unstratified way, monotonicity and the intended semantics would have to be reproved. The safe closed form is: all state-decoding and transition predicates are FO on the fixed input structure (or on a fixed FO-interpreted structure) and independent of `AC$; `AC$ is the only PFP/IFP variable of the acceptance computation. `[deduction]`
- The closure-audit’s caution is consistent: the monotonicity claim is too quick unless the final decoding is fixed. `decode-explicit$ is more explicit about the assumption than the fixed-tuple-closure verdict, but it still does not discharge the complete decoding.

**Falsification target.** Any completed construction should explicitly list the free/relational parameters of `ψ$: `AC$ positive and all others `AC$-independent, or else give a simultaneous/stratified fixed-point semantics and prove monotonicity for the whole operator system.

## 3. PFP/IFP/LFP claims and class overclaims

### Assessment: `decode-explicit` is appropriately cautious; no smaller class is established

`decode-explicit` §8 gives the right separation:

- **PFP target form:** `[PFP_{AC,\bar d} ψ(AC,\bar d)]$ is the correct form for the FO(PFP)=PSPACE capture route, but only after a complete explicit configuration encoding and equivalence proof. `[literature,open]`
- **IFP/LFP form (conditional):** if a complete finite game/configuration structure with `Acc, YieldE, YieldA0, YieldA1$ independent of `AC$ is fixed, positivity makes the acceptance operator least-fixed-point/IFP definable as an operator on that structure; the finite alternating game stratifies by comparisons used (`<=2k+2$ control ranks on reachable plays). `[deduction]`
- **No overclaim:** this does not imply `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ P`, FO(LFP) on the bare ordered word, or NP/coNP membership. The representation/simulation gap remains the reason. `[deduction]`

This matches the project constraints in `AGENTS.md`/`docs/problem.md`:

- `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE$ is established in the repo context; showing `∉ P$ or `∉ NP$ would imply major separations. `[literature]`
- `SORT-BOUND-U$ is sparse/tally-like in the unary setting (Mahaney barrier applies to many-one NP-hardness of sparse sets), but this is not membership. `[literature]`
- A positive/stratified acceptance formula alone is not a complexity-class improvement absent the complete encoding and semantics on the actual input structure.

**Small wording caution.** Saying the operator “has a least fixed point” is mathematically fine on a finite complete lattice, but the research-relevant point is the *semantics and complexity consequence*: an IFP/LFP of a positive operator over an explicitly encoded polynomial configuration structure is different from an IFP/LFP sentence over the bare word capturing the language in `P$. `decode-explicit$ avoids that overclaim; any use of the fixed-tuple-closure note should preserve that caveat.

## 5. Artifact-by-artifact reading

### `fopfp-decode-explicit.md`

- **Strengths:** unified control tuple; explicit `TimeOf/AOf/BOf/ModeOf$ access; explicit transition predicates `Inc_C/Total_C/StepPos/Init/Acc/YieldE/YieldA0/A1$; explicit `<=k$ budget discipline; explicit monotonicity dependency; explicit PFP/IFP/LFP caution; clear residual blocker and completion obligations. `[deduction]`
- **Limits:** no complete `PosOf$; `P_C$ is an interface, not a discharged encoding; no simultaneous system, no interpreted exponential-state domain, no concrete ATM encoding/equivalence proof. It correctly does not claim closure.

### `fopfp-fixed-tuple-closure.md`

- **Strengths:** right high-level direction (ordered word interpretation, fixed-arity PFP over tuple coding, game-level alternating acceptance, malformed/budget handling, bare-word clarification). `[literature,deduction]`
- **Limits:** closure is overstated if read as formula-level. It traverses several shapes, leaves `Encodes*/PosOf*` unexpanded, and gives monotonicity/staging justification without the completed decoded operator. The bare-word distinction is useful, but the concrete fixed tuple still does not carry full poset content.

### `fopfp-fixed-tuple-closure-audit.md`

- **Assessment:** its main verdict is consistent with this audit: “partial closure / materially improved, but not a fully closed concrete FO(PFP) sentence.” It correctly asks for a single final shape, expanded decoders, `AC$-independent decoding/monotonicity proof, invariant preservation, edge cases, and small-value sanity checks.

## 6. Bottom line

1. **`PosOf/P_C` blocker:** **confirmed** as an obstruction to decoding arbitrary poset content from a fixed-arity configuration code `d̄\in W^r$ alone. Not an obstruction to FO(PFP) definability in principle. `[deduction,literature]`
2. **Monotonicity dependency:** **confirmed**. Syntactic positivity of `AC$ is present in the schema, but semantic monotonicity/intended acceptance requires `AC$-independent state decoding and transitions (especially `P_C/PosOf$). `[deduction]`
3. **PFP/IFP/LFP without smaller-class overclaim:** **mostly satisfactory in `decode-explicit`**. It states PFP target, conditional IFP/LFP game form, and explicitly avoids `P$/FO(LFP)-on-bare-word/NP/coNP overclaims. `[literature,deduction]`
4. **Closure paths:** **none concretely discharged**. Simultaneous PFP on interpreted configuration+cell relations, explicit interpreted domain for exponentially many states, and explicit ATM encoding with equivalence proof are all **precisely left open** pending a complete encoding and semantic proof. `[open]`

## Required to claim a concrete closure

A future note could claim concrete closure only by supplying all of the following in one fixed construction:

1. A single chosen fixed-arity PFP (or proved-equivalent simultaneous PFP) signature and interpretation on `A(w)$.
2. Explicit FO formulas for all state decoders: `Encodes*`, `PosOf/P_C`, `TimeOf`, `AOf`, `BOf`, `ModeOf`, and any configuration/cell relations.
3. A representation theorem: every reachable legal `G(n,k)$ state is encoded; decoded transitions are exactly legal `YieldE/YieldA0/YieldA1` moves with incremental TC and budget `<=k$.
4. A fixed-point semantics theorem: `AC$-independent decoding (or a proved monotone simultaneous system) and PFP/IFP acceptance equivalent to sorter-winning in `G(n,k)$ / `S(n)<=k$.
5. Edge-case discharge: malformed inputs, `n<=1$, `k=0$, total initial state, total reached at `k$, null-pair inertness.
6. Model-fidelity checks against the intended `G(n,k)$/minimax definition and known small values where available. `[falsification target]`
