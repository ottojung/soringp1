## 2. `CurExhausted` in `Rbar` / `Fld` / frame equality

`[definition]` Proposed revision §1 adds `CurExhausted` to `Rbar` after
`CurA,CurB` and to
```text
Fld(c̄) := ⟨Mode,Ret,Ctrl,UsedF,HasSel,SelA,SelB,
            HasCur,CurA,CurB,CurExhausted,PosF,Parent⟩
```
and defines
```text
CurNormal(c̄,a,b) := HasCur(c̄)∧CurA(c̄,a)∧CurB(c̄,b)∧¬CurExhausted(c̄).
CursorWellFormed(c̄) :=
      (CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ ¬∃a∃b CurNormal(c̄,a,b))
    ∨ (¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ HasCur(c̄) ∧ ∃!a∃b(CurA(c̄,a)∧CurB(c̄,b))).
FrameFieldsEq' includes CurExhausted.
FrameEq' := FrameFieldsEq' ∧ (FrameLive' <-> FrameLive).
PreserveNonMarkerFields preserves all Fld except Ret and preserves FrameLive',
explicitly including HasCur,CurA,CurB,CurExhausted,PosF,UsedF,...
```

`[deduction]` **Supported at schema level.** Relative to normalized `Enc`
(`Fld` had no `CurExhausted`; realization §1.2/§3.2 had no `CurExhausted`), this
closes the exact representation gap identified by the refutation: exhaustion is
now a named frame component and is included in frame equality/preservation.
`LowerStackPreservedExcept(S)` therefore preserves `CurExhausted` for live
frames outside `S`, because it preserves `FrameEq'` and `FrameFieldsEq'`
includes `CurExhausted`.

`[open]` **Under-instantiated: realization alignment.** The MDALT DTM
realization baseline (`fopfp-cks-av-mdalt-dtm-realization.md` §1.2, §3.2 rows
`D_sort_pick/D_backE_rej_next/D_backE_rej_exh_*`) encodes no `CurExhausted`
field and consumes only `NextIncomp_c(CurA,CurB;...)` / `NoNextIncomp_c`.
Applying the proposed correction requires a corresponding realization-side
encoding/update for `CurExhausted` (tape field/bit, initialization, component
closure). The revision states this as `[open Enc_Component_Closure for
CurExhausted]` in §5(1), which is correct.

`[open]` **Under-instantiated: `HasCur'` in exhausted branch.**
`SetParentBackE_Advance`'s `IsLastLex2` branch sets `CurExhausted' ∧ HasCur' ∧
¬∃a∃b(CurA'∧CurB')`. This satisfies `CursorWellFormed` because `CurNormal'`
requires a `CurA/CurB` witness. However it makes `HasCur'` true while there is
no normal cursor witness. The schema does not state whether other readers treat
`HasCur` as "has a normal pair" or "cursor field is populated (possibly
exhausted)". Rows 10–12 and NI/NNI avoid this by consulting `CurExhausted` /
`CurNormal`; any remaining consumer of bare `HasCur/CurA/CurB` must be audited.
Exact missing rule: a total convention for `HasCur` under exhaustion, and an
audit/update of all `HasCur`-reading rows (notably eval-side `RejectEval_c` /
`NextIncompAtCur_c` forms from normalized `Enc`).

## 3. `SetParentBackE_Advance`

Proposed §2:
```text
¬IsLastLex2(x,y)  => ¬CurExhausted'(c̄) ∧ HasCur'(c̄)
                     ∧ ∃x'∃y'(SuccLex2(x,y,x',y') ∧ CurA'(c̄,x') ∧ CurB'(c̄,y'))
                     ∧ unique CurA', unique CurB'.
IsLastLex2(x,y)   => CurExhausted'(c̄) ∧ HasCur'(c̄)
                     ∧ ¬∃a∃b(CurA'(c̄,a)∧CurB'(c̄,b)).
Preserves HasSel/SelA/SelB, UsedF, PosF, Parent of c̄.
Sets FrameLive', M_BackE', RetNone', CtrlSort'; clears other modes/ret/ctrl.
```

`[deduction]` **Supported against the sentinel refutation.** In the non-last
case, `SuccLex2(x,y,x',y')` gives a strict `Lex2` advance; with strict total
`Lex2`, `NextIncomp_c(x',y';x,y)` is impossible, so the same pair `(x,y)` cannot
be NI-witnessed from the preserved post-advance normal cursor. In the last
case, the update produces `CurExhausted'(c̄)` and no normal pair, so revised
`NI_c` (which requires `¬CurExhausted ∧ CurNormal`) cannot hold — directly
addressing the refutation's `IsLastLex2/BackE_NoFurther` consumer gap.

`[open]` **Under-instantiated: totality/representation of exhausted cursor.**
The last branch requires `¬∃a∃b(CurA'∧CurB')`. In a realization where
`CurA,CurB` are functional/total fields over a fixed encoding, this may require
an explicit "no-normal-cursor" encoding convention; the note acknowledges this
as an `Enc_Component_Closure` obligation. Exact missing rule: concrete
`CurA'/CurB'` encoding under exhaustion (e.g., designated null encoding not used
as `CurNormal`, or a component-closure clause proving no normal witness exists),
and proof that this encoding never collides with `CurNormal`.

`[open]` **Under-instantiated: advance from an exhausted cursor is excluded but
not globally stated.** `SetParentBackE_Advance(c̄,x,y)` is parameterized by a
selected witness `(x,y)` from row 6/10. Revised row 10 requires `NI_c`, which
requires `¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ CurNormal(...)`, so the advance is not applied
from exhaustion — this is good. Row 6's full guard is not restated in the
revision (it defers to normalized row 6 plus normal-cursor NI form); the
restated row 6 must explicitly require the normal NI form and cannot be read as
using normalized `Enc`'s old `NI_c` abbreviation alone.

## 4. `MarkRet*` preservation and changed-set discipline

`[definition]` Proposed:
```text
PreserveNonMarkerFields(p̄) preserves all Fld except Ret and FrameLive',
including HasCur,CurA,CurB,CurExhausted,PosF,UsedF,HasSel,SelA,SelB,Parent,Ctrl,Mode.
MarkRetRejOn(p̄) := FrameLive'(p̄) ∧ RetRej'(p̄) ∧ ¬RetAcc'(p̄) ∧ ¬RetNone'(p̄)
                 ∧ PreserveNonMarkerFields(p̄).
MarkRetAccOn(p̄) analogously.
LowerStackPreservedExcept(S) := ∀x̄(FrameLive(x̄)∧¬S(x̄)->FrameEq'(x̄)).
```

`[deduction]` **Supported for the stated purpose.** Rows 5 and 12 (and all
other normalized pop rows) place `p̄` in `S={p̄,c̄}` and apply
`MarkRet*On(p̄)`. Therefore `p̄` is excluded from generic `FrameEq'` for changed
fields, but `MarkRet*On` explicitly preserves all non-`Ret` `Fld` components,
including `CurExhausted` and `CurNormal` witnesses. This is the right
discipline for `T-pop5,12`: a reject-producing pop can move `RetRej` to a BackE
receiver without mutating its post-advance cursor.

`[open]` **Changed-set/producer coverage.** The revision states "Unchanged rows
1–9,13–19 keep their normalized `Enc` guards/updates, with the global revision
that `FrameEq'/PreserveNonMarkerFields/MarkRet*On` include `CurExhausted` in
`Fld`". This is necessary but not sufficient as a complete matrix:
- Row 6 (`D_sort_pick`) creates child `d̄` via `PushChildE` and changes `c̄`
  via `SetParentBackE_Advance`. `PushChildE` in normalized `Enc` §1.4 sets
  `HasCur'/CurA'/CurB'` to `MinKey,MinKey` but does not mention `CurExhausted`;
  child `d̄`'s `CurExhausted'` must be explicitly initialized (normally false).
- `PushChildA` and initial root frame (`Init_{M_D}^{sym}`) must also initialize
  `CurExhausted`.
- Any row that uses `CtrlSort`/BackE cursor in eval mode must initialize or
  preserve `CurExhausted` consistently.
Exact missing rule: explicit `CurExhausted'` initialization/preservation clause
in `PushChildE`, `PushChildA`, `Init_{M_D}^{sym}`, and a fully restated row 6.

## 5. Rows 5,12 producers and rows 10–12 consumers; traces

### 5.1 Row 10–12 consumer partition

Proposed:
```text
NI_c  := ¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ ∃a∃b(CurNormal(c̄,a,b) ∧ ∃x∃y NextIncomp_c(a,b;x,y)).
NNI_c := CurExhausted(c̄) ∨ (¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ ∃a∃b(CurNormal(c̄,a,b) ∧ NoNextIncomp_c(a,b))).
Row 10: Top∧M_BackE∧RetRej∧NI_c,  S={c̄,d̄}, push next NI witness.
Row 11: Top∧M_BackE∧RetRej∧NNI_c∧Root, terminal reject.
Row 12: Top∧M_BackE∧RetRej∧NNI_c∧NonRoot∧Parent(c̄,p̄), S={p̄,c̄}, pop + MarkRetRejOn(p̄).
```

`[deduction]` **Supported conditionally.** Assuming `CursorWellFormed(c̄)`,
fixed total `Lex2`, fixed `Incomp_c` from a coherent `PosF` snapshot, and unique
`CurNormal` when non-exhausted: if `CurExhausted(c̄)` then `NNI_c` holds and
`NI_c` fails; otherwise `NextIncomp_c` exists or `NoNextIncomp_c` holds
exclusively, with unique NI witness on the finite domain. Thus `NI_c xor NNI_c`
is definitional given these antecedents. The consumer partition is the correct
schema-level fix for the sentinel gap.

`[open]` **Reachability obligations remain.** The antecedents
(`CursorWellFormed`, `PosF`/`Incomp_c` coherence, unique cursor, `I_{M_D}`
preservation) are explicitly listed as open in the proposal §4.2/§5 and remain
open.

### 5.2 Trace `T-sel` (last-pair selection)

`[falsification-shape]` Original refutation locus:
```text
E --row 6 or row 10--> selects IsLastLex2(x,y), stores sentinel/ambiguous cursor
... later BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄) and rows 10–12 misread exhaustion.
```
`[deduction]` **Revised trace, schema-supported.**
```text
T-sel-last:
  E ⊨ Top(c̄)∧(M_Eval,CtrlSort,RetNone or M_BackE,RetRej as appropriate)
      ∧ NI_c(E) with unique NI witness (x,y) ∧ IsLastLex2(x,y).
  (NI_c(E) => ¬CurExhausted(E,c̄) ∧ CurNormal(E,c̄,a,b), so selection is from
   a normal cursor.)
  E --row 6 or row 10, S={c̄,d̄}--> E1:
      SetParentBackE_Advance(c̄,x,y) gives
        CurExhausted'(c̄), HasCur'(c̄), ¬∃a∃b(CurA'∧CurB'),
        M_BackE', RetNone', CtrlSort', preserves HasSel/SelA/SelB,UsedF,PosF,Parent;
      PushChildE(d̄,c̄,x,y) as in row discipline.
  Therefore E1 ⊭ NI_c(c̄) (needs ¬CurExhausted) and has CurExhausted'(c̄).
```
No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying witness was found; the bad
sentinel-consumer path is blocked by the schema because `NNI_c` reads
`CurExhausted` directly.

## 6. Exact under-instantiated field/rule loci

The proposed correction is **not falsified** by an explicit audited
`I_{M_D}`-reachable witness, but it is **still under-instantiated** at these
exact loci:

1. **`HasCur` convention under exhaustion.** `SetParentBackE_Advance`
   (`IsLastLex2`) sets `CurExhausted' ∧ HasCur' ∧ ¬∃a∃b(CurA'∧CurB')`.
   `CursorWellFormed` permits this, but the global meaning of `HasCur` under
   exhaustion is not fixed for all consumers. Rule needed: either define
   `HasCur` as normal-cursor presence (and adjust the exhausted branch) or
   define it as cursor-state presence and audit every bare `HasCur/CurA/CurB`
   use.

2. **Concrete exhausted `CurA'/CurB'` encoding.** The exhausted branch states
   `¬∃a∃b(CurA'∧CurB')` but does not give a total frame-component encoding for
   realizations where these relations/fields must be concretely instantiated.
   Rule needed: component-closure clause for exhausted `CurA,CurB,HasCur,
   CurExhausted`.

3. **`PushChildE` / `PushChildA` / `Init_{M_D}^{sym}` initialization of
   `CurExhausted`.** Normalized templates initialize `HasCur,CurA,CurB` but not
   `CurExhausted`. Rule needed: `¬CurExhausted'(d̄)` (or explicit cursor state)
   in `PushChildE`, `PushChildA`, and root initialization; corresponding
   preservation in all non-cursor-changing rows via `FrameFieldsEq'` is already
   implied but should be stated in the full row set.

4. **Full row 6 restatement and eval-side NI/NNI.** The revision references
   normalized row 6 and uses `NNI_c_at_eval` without a complete definition.
   Rule needed: explicit row 6 guard/update with revised `NI_c`; explicit
   `RejectEval_c`/`NoNextIncompAtCur_c` replacement consistent with
   `CurNormal|CurExhausted`; and proof that eval `CtrlSort` states have
   `CursorWellFormed` before row 3/5/6 consume them.

5. **Realization-side `CurExhausted` update.** The MDALT DTM realization
   `E(Rbar)` / `δ_{M_D^alt}^{sch}` table has no `CurExhausted`. Rule needed:
   add `CurExhausted` to the realization encoding and to macro-cases
   `D_sort_pick`, `D_backE_rej_next`, producers/markers, and initialization, with
   component closure.

6. **Inherited DET-SUCC obligations remain open.** Even if (1)–(5) are fixed:
   `BackE_Cursor_Preservation`, `BackE_NI_NNI_Total_Complement`,
   `BackE_AfterSelected_NoReselect` as reachability/component-closure lemmas;
   `Enc_Component_Closure`; full `I_{M_D}` preservation; marker-receiver
   reachability; A1 snapshot; `FreshChild` freshness; `Parent` functionality;
   `CtrlSort` exhaustivity. No DET-SUCC discharge.

## 7. Verdict

`[deduction]` **Verdict: supported directionally; no explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable
falsifying witness found; still under-instantiated (exact missing field/rule
loci in §6).**

The proposed `Normal|Exhausted` revision correctly targets the audited
`IsLastLex2/BackE_NoFurther` sentinel-consumer gap: `CurExhausted` is added to
`Rbar/Fld/FrameEq'`; `SetParentBackE_Advance` makes the last-pair branch produce
`CurExhausted'` and the non-last branch produce a `SuccLex2` normal cursor;
`MarkRet*`/`PreserveNonMarkerFields` preserve `CurExhausted`; and rows 10–12
consult `NNI_c/NI_c` through `CurExhausted|CurNormal`. The revised traces
`T-sel-last`, `T-pop5`, `T-pop12`, and `T-consume` are schema-consistent and do
not reproduce the original sentinel-misread witness.

However, the correction is not yet a complete schema/realization instantiation:
`HasCur`/exhausted encoding, `PushChildE`/`PushChildA`/`Init` initialization,
full row 6 and eval-side NI/NNI, and realization-side `CurExhausted` updates
remain under-specified. **`[CKS-AV-POST-HALT/I_M_D-DET-SUCC]` remains not
discharged.** No FO(PFP)/IFP, `P`, `FO(LFP)` on the bare ordered word, `NP`,
`coNP`, binary `SORT-BOUND-B`, separation, or collapse claim is made.
