## 3. `CursorCoherent`: xor, total `HasCur/CurA/CurB`, exhausted encoding

**Prior audit §6/§9 loci.** §6 items 1–2 required a total `HasCur` convention
under exhaustion and a concrete exhausted `CurA'/CurB'` encoding/component
closure.

**v2.** `[definition]` §1 gives:
```text
CurNormalState(c̄,a,b) := ¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ HasCur(c̄) ∧ CurA(c̄,a) ∧ CurB(c̄,b).
CurNormalState(c̄)     := ∃a∃b CurNormalState(c̄,a,b).
CurExhaustedState(c̄)  := CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ HasCur(c̄)
                        ∧ CurA(c̄,MinKey) ∧ CurB(c̄,MinKey)
                        ∧ ¬CurNormalState(c̄).
CursorCoherent(c̄) :=
      (CurNormalState(c̄) ∧ ∃!a∃!b(CurA(c̄,a)∧CurB(c̄,b)) ∧ ¬CurExhausted(c̄))
    ⊕
      (CurExhaustedState(c̄)).
SentinelNull := (MinKey,MinKey)  -- concrete exhausted encoding, never CurNormal.
```
v2 §1 deduction states `CurNormalState` is excluded from `CurExhaustedState` by
`¬CurExhausted` in `CurNormalState`; NI requires `¬CurExhausted ∧
CurNormalState`.

**Assessment.** `[deduction]` **Closed at schema/instantiation level, with an
explicit encoding convention.**
- `CursorCoherent` is an exclusive xor of normal and exhausted cursor states.
- `HasCur(c̄)` is total/true in both branches (cursor-state presence), which is
  explicit and consistent with v1's exhausted branch having `HasCur'` but gives
  a concrete witness convention for `CurA/CurB`.
- Exhausted `CurA/CurB` are concretely `(MinKey,MinKey)` and cannot be read as
  `CurNormalState` because `CurNormalState` requires `¬CurExhausted`.
- This directly resolves prior audit §6 item 2 (`¬∃ab CurA'∧CurB'` under-
  instantiated for total realizations) and makes item 1's `HasCur` meaning
  explicit (cursor-state populated, possibly exhausted).

**Caveat (schema-consistent, residual proof obligation).** `[open]` The concrete
exhausted encoding `(MinKey,MinKey)` is also the normal initialization cursor
used by `Init_{M_D}^{sym}` and `PushChildE/PushChildA` when
`¬CurExhausted`. The distinction is carried by `CurExhausted`, not by the pair
value. Therefore no NI/NNI consumer may treat `(MinKey,MinKey)` alone as
exhausted or as a special ordinary pair without consulting `CurExhausted`; v2's
`NI_c`/`NNI_c` do consult `CurExhausted` first (closed). The remaining
obligation is `I_{M_D}`/component-closure proof that all cursor readers
(including eval-side paths, row 6, and realization consumers) preserve this
discipline. v2 §7.2(3), §7.1 list this; not discharged.

**No explicit falsifying witness.** `[deduction]` No audited source exhibits an
`I_{M_D}`-reachable state where `(MinKey,MinKey), ¬CurExhausted` is consumed as
exhausted, or where `CurExhausted,(MinKey,MinKey)` is consumed as
`CurNormalState`. The schema rules prevent both readings.
