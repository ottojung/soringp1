## Verdict (summary)

The artifact is a **strong, well-localized paper-level discharge attempt for the DFS stack-discipline formula** `Δ_{M_D}` that was left open as `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D]` by the tape-formulas artifact. It fixes `A(w)`, `r:=4`, `Cell=W^r`, and a fixed finite family of frame/stack/configuration relations (§§1–2); gives explicit `Init_{M_D}` (§3); gives explicit local move semantics `Incomp/Total/Budget/CfgAcc/CfgRej/StepPosBit/PostBudget/YieldE/YieldA0/YieldA1` on encoded frames (§4); and gives a case-closed FO one-step transition schema `Δ_{M_D}` covering halting, root/non-root accept/reject returns, existential lex-cursor search/push/backtrack, universal `A0` push, `A0 -> A1` sequencing, and final return propagation (§5). Section 6 gives paper-level arguments for **determinism/functionality**, **totality on non-halting well-formed states**, **invariant preservation**, and **no spurious transitions**.

It **does not** claim an absolutely complete, end-to-end, first-principles fixed syntactic FO(PFP)/IFP sentence proof. The remaining gap is isolated as **`[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK]`**: the standard fixed-arity/simultaneous PFP packing (or equivalent single-relation normal form) of the explicit configuration-relation family and proof that the packed operator is exactly the operator induced by the case-closed `Δ_{M_D}`. This is narrower than `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D]`: the stack discipline and local cases are explicit, while the residual is normal-form closure/capture plumbing. The FO(PFP) acceptance schema and `S(n)<=k` chain are therefore **conditional** on `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK]` plus the cited standard Vardi/Immerman/Abiteboul–Vianu capture theorem.

The artifact **avoids A001035/`PACK-REPR` total tuple-coding** of all `Pos` states. **No `P`, `FO(LFP)`-on-bare-word, `NP`, `coNP`, or complete-discharged fixed-sentence overclaim** is found.

## 3. Local move semantics

Section 4 defines current-frame accessors `P_c(u,v):=PosF(c̄,u,v)`, `Incomp_c`, `Total_c`, `Budget_c`, `CfgAcc_c`, `CfgRej_c`, and:
- `StepPosBit_c(x,y;u,v) := P_c(u,v) ∨ ((u=x ∨ P_c(u,x)) ∧ (v=y ∨ P_c(y,v)))`.
- `PostBudget` resolves to accept/continue-sort/reject by `Total` and `HasBudget(Used')`.
- `YieldE_c(a,b)` selects incomparable pair under sorter control with budget.
- `YieldA0_c/YieldA1_c` evaluate selected pair as `a<b` or `b<a`, consuming one `SuccCtr` budget step and applying `StepPosBit`.

**Assessment: adequate and aligned with prior CKS/AV semantics.** The local formulas are FO over current frame contents and fixed `A(w)` predicates (`KeyNe`, `SuccCtr`, `HasBudget`, etc.). The existential move preserves `Pos/Used` and enters adversary selection; universal moves update closure and budget exactly once per comparison outcome. This matches the comparison-game/DFS evaluator intent and the tape-formulas audit’s accepted local layer.

**Caution.** As in the prior audit chain, `ValidPos`/closure invariants are assumed/maintained by the evaluator discipline; the incremental TC lemma is cited as standard/prior, not fully reproved here.
