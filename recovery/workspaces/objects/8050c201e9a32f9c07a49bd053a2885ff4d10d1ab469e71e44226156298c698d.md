## 2. Core PACK-REACH repair

PACK audit failed `ψ_reach(t,\bar y,S) := Init^#_{tuple}(t,\bar y) ∨
∃s∃\bar z (S(s,\bar z) ∧ Step^#_{tuple}(s,\bar z; t,\bar y))` because `S`
accumulated tagged tuples across iterations while `Step^#`/`Δ_{M_D}` operate
on whole configurations.

PACK-REACH repairs this by:
1. making the fixed-point state `Rbar^α` a complete current configuration;
2. applying `Ψ^abs` to produce the unique next complete configuration; and
3. accepting only if the `HaltAcc` component holds in the resulting PFP stage.

Thus a stage is never a union of evidence from multiple reached
configurations. This is the right Vardi/Immerman/AV deterministic-operator
form for a functional PSPACE computation. The single-relation normal form
preserves this because `X` at a stage encodes exactly that stage’s `Rbar^α`.
**This discharges the PACK audit’s minimal failing lemma
`[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK-REACH]` at the whole-configuration acceptance
normal-form level, conditional on `Δ_{M_D}` inducing a deterministic FO
operator as assumed.**
