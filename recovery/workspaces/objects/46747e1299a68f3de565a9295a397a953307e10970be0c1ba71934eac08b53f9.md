## 4. Does it give an explicit FO(PFP) definition or only a scheme?

**It gives a scheme with gaps, not a fully explicit FO(PFP) definition.**

Evidence from the artifact itself:
- §5 "Validation obligations not discharged here" requires fixed syntax/signature, fully expanded `Conf/Init/Acc/Yield` including TC, proof of PFP alternating-acceptance semantics on the finite configuration structure, and edge-case/interpretation proofs.
- §6.1 identifies representation/arity as the main concrete obstacle and resolves it only by "explicit FO interpretation" in principle.
- §6.6 states a small natural fully expanded formula remains open.
- §7 states "scheme-level, explicit up to standard encodings" and calls the remainder "encoding completion".

An "explicit FO(PFP) definition" for audit purposes would require, at minimum, a fixed ordered relational signature and a concrete `Φ` (or a mechanically expandable definition with every subformula/arity/interpretation fixed). The artifact provides the semantic operator `ψ`, the sentence shape `Φ`, and the mathematical components, but leaves the concrete logical encoding unspecified.

## 5. Exact missing steps for an explicit definition

To upgrade this artifact from scheme to an explicit FO(PFP) definition, the following must be supplied:

1. **Fix syntax/signature:** choose FO(PFP) syntax (e.g. PFP operator form), fix `σ` for `A(w)` and/or the interpreted structure, and fix all relation symbols/arities for configurations, acceptance, time/control/state, and poset matrix/cells.
2. **Complete interpretation `A(w) ↦ B(n,k)`/configuration domain:** give FO formulas for domain, equality/quotient, component orders/successors/zero/max, `#`-based split, malformed rejection, and the `k+1` third component.
3. **Expand ATM/configuration formulas:** write concrete `Conf(C)`, `Init(C,w)`, `Acc(C)`, `Mode_∃/∀(C)`, `Yield_∃`, `Yield_∀` for the chosen encoding, including pair selection, adversary binary branch, budget increment/comparison, and poset-edge insertion.
4. **Expand transitive-closure update:** supply the explicit FO(LFP) or FO(PFP) TC subformula used inside transitions/acceptance, on the ordered domain, and show it composes with the configuration encoding.
5. **Prove PFP semantics:** show `[PFP_{A,C} ψ(A,C)]` computes alternating acceptance for this (acyclic-in-budget / finite configuration-graph) game and not merely a monotone under-approximation; justify finiteness/stabilization under the chosen PFP semantics.
6. **Prove end-to-end equivalence:** for every well-formed/unformed `w`, `A(w) ⊨ Φ` iff `w ∈ SORT-BOUND-U`, with resource/encoding correspondence stated against `N=|w|`.
7. **Independent validation attempt:** falsify on small cases/edge cases at the paper level (e.g. known `S(n)` values, malformed strings, `k>=U(n)`, `n<=1`) once formulas are concrete.
