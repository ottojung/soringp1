# Exec report: framed-dag-witness-n14-exact

## 1. Problem statement and S(14) baseline

**Front/question (falsifiable, independent).** Does there exist an explicit, checkable permutation-framed strategy DAG certificate of exact height `S(14)=37` for `n=14`, and what is its concrete size/depth/profile compared to polynomial envelopes?

**Model ([definition], `docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`).** `S(n)` is the minimum worst-case number of adaptive pairwise comparisons to sort `n` distinct labelled keys. A framed strategy DAG certificate is an acyclic directed graph with root state (empty guaranteed order), local comparison/outcome edges carrying explicit slot permutations, meet/intersection of guaranteed relations at joins, total-order leaves, and depth `<= k`. Soundness/local checkability is implemented by `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py` (polynomial in explicit graph size and `n`); polynomial-size completeness for optimal strategies at height `S(n)` is not asserted.

**S(14) baseline.**
- [literature/index] `docs/literature.md` cites Stober & Weiß, *Lower Bounds for Sorting 16, 17, and 18 Elements* (ALENEX 2023, arXiv:2206.05597): with those results, exact optimal comparison counts are known for all `n <= 22` (`n=23` open). This places `n=14` in the known-exact range; OEIS A036604 is the index (not a primary proof).
- [experiment/computation] Information lower bound: `ceil(log2(14!)) = 37` (`math.factorial(14)`).
- [experiment/computation, certified upper-bound formula] Ford–Johnson merge-insertion worst-case height `M(n)=sum_{i=1}^{n} ceil(log2(3i/4))` gives `M(14)=38` (termwise `(1,0),(2,1),(3,2),(4,2),(5,2),(6,3),(7,3),(8,3),(9,3),(10,3),(11,4),(12,4),(13,4),(14,4)`; computed via `scripts/upper_witness.py:fj_upper_bound`).
- **Working baseline for this front:** `S(14)=37` [literature-consistent target], bracketed here by `37 <= S(14) <= 38` from the information bound and FJ formula; I did not rederive a primary proof of exact `S(14)=37` in this run.

### 3.3 Evidence/obstruction relevant to the exact-height-37 question

1. **Budget gap to explicit poly-time constructions ([experiment/literature-formula]).** `S(14)` target is `37` (info lower bound / known exact range), while FJ gives certified `M(14)=38` and the only available framed generator (bubble) yields verified depth `91` for `n=14`. Thus available explicit certificate generators in-repo do not reach height `37`.
2. **State/tree explosion for optimal single-policy strategies ([experiment], `data/strategy_dag_probe_max5.txt`, consistent with `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md`).** For a single optimal policy, `DAG(1)=unshared(1)=2*n! - 1` for `n<=5` (hash-consing gives no sharing): `n=5 -> 239` nodes, `n=6` is already `1439` in prior recorded data. Extrapolating the exact single-policy tree form gives `2*14! - 1 ≈ 1.743×10^11` nodes/leaves-scale object, far beyond explicit materialization/checking/storage here; this is a size obstruction for representing an optimal *tree* policy explicitly, not a proof that no polynomial-size framed *DAG* of height `37` exists.
3. **All-optimal DAGs shrink by sharing but remain large/growing ([experiment/prior note]).** `strategy_dag_probe`/`research-strategy-certificates.md` report `DAG(all)` growing rapidly (`n=5 -> 1551`, `n=6 -> 65551`) and `log2(DAG(all))/n` increasing on `n<=6` (evidence inconclusive for asymptotics). This does not establish (or refute) a poly-size height-37 framed DAG for `n=14`, but indicates nontrivial combinatorial growth.
4. **Soundness vs completeness ([definition/literature in-repo]).** Per `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, the framed grammar is sound and poly-checkable in explicit size; all-`n` polynomial-size completeness at exact `S(n)` is open. Therefore absence of a constructed height-37 witness here is an **evidenced obstruction/failure-to-construct in this budgeted run**, not a proof of nonexistence.
