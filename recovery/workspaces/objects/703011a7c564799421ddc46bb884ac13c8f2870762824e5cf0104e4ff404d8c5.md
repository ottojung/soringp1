# Exec report: merge-sort framed DAG for n=8

## 1. Construction basis

Construction follows `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, §2
("All-n polynomial DAG for adaptive merge sort"): each merge of sorted
queues of lengths `p,q` has `pq` comparison states `(a,b)`; the meet
invariant is an ordered output prefix plus two ordered remaining queues;
comparison outcomes use explicit full-slot permutations; when one queue
empties the other queue is appended without further comparisons.

Balanced schedule for `n=8`:

- four merges `(1,1)` at offsets `0,2,4,6`;
- two merges `(2,2)` at offsets `0,4`;
- one merge `(4,4)` at offset `0`.

Comparison-node count identity: `4*1 + 2*4 + 1*16 = 28 = 8*7/2`.
