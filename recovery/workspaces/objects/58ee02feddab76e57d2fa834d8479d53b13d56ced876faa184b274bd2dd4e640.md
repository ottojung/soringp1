# Exec report: root-reachable-certified-quotient

## Scope and question addressed

- **Task (as given).** Front `root-reachable-certified-quotient`: test a candidate exact root-reachable equivalence of optimal comparison histories stronger than relabelled-poset isomorphism, with an explicitly checkable two-outcome congruence and a polynomial bound on distinct classes for all `n`.
- **Falsifier requested.** Exhibit an infinite family of root-optimal decision strategies for which any such equivalence requires superpolynomially many distinct verified classes, or prove a concrete simpler class fails.
- **Implication (motivation).** A correct polynomial root-optimal quotient plus a locally checkable height certificate would yield a polynomial NP certificate for the unary decision threshold `SORT-BOUND-U` (**definition/deduction**, per `docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md`).
- **Sources consulted (primary in-repo).** `AGENTS.md`, `docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`, `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py`, and relevant context in `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md`, `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`, `docs/research-compression-next.md`, `docs/research-state-compression.md`, `scripts/strategy_dag_probe.py`, `scripts/state_compression.py`.
- **Interpretation note (deduction).** “Stronger than relabelled-poset isomorphism” is read here as a **coarser identification** (fewer classes, i.e. identifying some non-isomorphic histories/states while preserving exact optimal root-reachable behavior), because a finer relation would only increase class count and cannot target a polynomial bound below the isomorphism quotient. “Root-reachable” is read as reachable from the antichain root along some comparison history consistent with an optimal (height `S(n)`) strategy/policy. If the intended meaning differs, conclusions below should be re-read accordingly.

## Model anchors (definition / literature / deduction)

- **Definition.** `S(n)` is the minimax height of deterministic adaptive comparison sorting of `n` distinct labelled keys; `SORT-BOUND-U = {1^n#1^k : S(n) <= k}`; states are transitive closures (posets) with minimax value `F(P)` (`docs/problem.md`, `SoringP1/Minimax.lean` correspondence stated complete in `AGENTS.md`).
- **Literature/deduction (baseline quotients).** Relabelling by `S_n` is an exact symmetry: `F(πP)=F(P)` and transitions are equivariant, so isomorphism classes form a sound DP/state quotient (`docs/research-state-compression.md`, `docs/research-compression-next.md`).
- **Deduction (soundness vs completeness for certificates).** The framed permutation-DAG grammar with local intersection/closure checks is sound and polynomial-time checkable in explicit graph size (`scripts/check_framed_strategy.py`, `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §1). No all-`n` polynomial-size completeness theorem at height `S(n)` is known (**open**).
- **Deduction (barriers).** `SORT-BOUND-U` is sparse (Mahaney barrier blocks routine many-one NP-hardness routes) but sparsity does not rule out NP membership; an explicit reduced comparison tree has `2n!-1` nodes, not a polynomial unary witness (`docs/research-encoding-consequences.md` context in `research-strategy-certificates.md`, `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`).

## Candidate equivalences tested

### C0. Baseline: relabelled-poset isomorphism on root-reachable optimal states (exact quotient)

- **Definition.** Identify states `P ~ Q` iff there exists `π ∈ S_n` with `Q = πP`. Restrict to states reachable from root on **some** optimal policy (`quotient(1)`) or on **any** optimal move union (`quotient(all)`).
- **Checkability / congruence (deduction).** With explicit permutations/canonical representatives on transitions, this yields a locally describable quotient transition structure (successor class of an outcome is determined up to isomorphism); canonicalization is the computational cost, not logical ambiguity (`docs/research-compression-next.md`, `docs/research-state-compression.md`).
- **Evidence (experiment, reproduced).** `python3 scripts/strategy_dag_probe.py --max-n 5` gives root-reachable optimal isomorphism-class counts:
  - `n=1..5`: `quotient(1)=1,2,4,7,11`, `quotient(all)=1,2,5,12,19` (matches `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md` Table 2 for reported range; `n=6` values in docs: `27,134`).
- **Assessment.** Exact and checkable in structure, but **not** “stronger/coarser” than isomorphism; class counts are far below all posets yet show rapid growth with no known polynomial bound (**open/inconclusive** for all-`n` polynomiality).

### C1. Comparability-graph isomorphism (coarser candidate), restricted to root-reachable optimal states

- **Definition.** `P ~_{CG} Q` iff their (undirected) comparability graphs are isomorphic.
- **Motivation.** Coarser than poset isomorphism in general and, on some restricted sets, can merge non-isomorphic posets.
- **Evidence (experiment, ad hoc probe in `/tmp/opencode/probe_root_reachable.py`, `n<=6`, root-optimal `ALL` states from `strategy_dag_probe` minimax).**
  - Class counts by CG on root-optimal states: `n=1..6 -> 1,2,4,8,11,58` (coarser than poset-iso for `n>=3` in this range).
  - Minimax value `F` was constant on each CG class **within this root-reachable sample** for `n<=6` (`cg coarser? True` for `n>=3`, `F constant on cg classes? True`).
- **Falsification / negative evidence (literature/deduction from in-repo results).**
  - **Global value non-invariance:** `docs/research-compression-next.md` §6(c) gives an explicit `n=7` witness (two posets with isomorphic comparability graphs but `F=6` vs `F=7`). Therefore CG equivalence is **not** an exact equivalence for the minimax value `F` over all states. This is a concrete failure of C1 as a universal exact state equivalence; unless restricted-and-proved otherwise, it cannot certify optimality.
  - **Transition ill-definedness:** §6(c) also reports a witness at `n=5` where the same comparability-graph class/non-edge leads to successor comparability graphs differing, so CG fails the “equivariant/well-defined two-outcome transition” requirement (Definition of sound state quotient in that note). Hence C1 does **not** provide an explicitly checkable two-outcome congruence in the DP/state-transition sense.
  - **Root-reachable status:** the cited `n=7` mixed-`F` pair is not established here to be root-reachable on an optimal strategy; thus the probe result “constant on root-optimal states for `n<=6`” is **inconclusive** rather than supportive of all-`n` exactness.
- **Assessment.** **Fails** as a general exact certified congruence (value/transition). No all-`n` root-reachable exactness proof; no polynomial class bound established.

### C2. Partition by `(depth, F(P))` or `(depth, F(P), e(P))` on root-reachable optimal states (coarse bookkeeping)

- **Definition.** Merge root-reachable histories/states sharing the same comparison depth and remaining minimax value `F` (optionally also linear-extension count `e(P)`).
- **Evidence (experiment, same probe, `n<=6`).** Partitions by `(F,depth)` gave `1,2,4,6,8,16` classes; by `(F,depth,e(P))` gave `1,2,4,8,10,38` on `ALL` root-optimal states.
- **Checkability / two-outcome congruence (deduction).** Equality of `F` (or `e`) at a node does **not** determine successor classes/values of a chosen comparison outcome pair in a locally checkable way without exhibiting the strategy; `e(P)` is not a sufficient statistic for `F` in general (`docs/research-state-compression.md` falsification at `n=5`, different `F` for same `L=e`). Therefore C2 is not an explicitly checkable two-outcome congruence for certifying a specific strategy, only a coarse annotation.
- **Assessment.** **Fails** the congruence/checkability requirement; polynomial all-`n` bound irrelevant absent exact certifying transitions.

### C3. Framed-history/guarantee-join equivalence (certificate-side quotient notion)

- **Definition (from frontier).** In the framed DAG model, states carry a guaranteed strict order `R` propagated with outcome extension, transitive closure, slot permutation, and intersection at joins (`docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` §1, `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py`).
- **Relation to question.** This is an explicitly checkable local formalism (sound). Identifying histories by identical certified guarantees/frames at joins is a natural “stronger” compression mechanism operationally, but it is a property of a **witness encoding**, not obviously an equivalence on optimal comparison histories independent of the chosen certificate.
- **Evidence (literature-in-repo / deduction).** Polynomial framed constructions exist for some non-optimal/all-n procedures (e.g. merge/bubble/rank-envelope ideas, with audits pending in places) but **optimal-size completeness at height `S(n)` is open**; no theorem gives a polynomial bound on distinct certified classes for all optimal root strategies (§1, §5 of frontier doc).
- **Assessment.** **Inconclusive.** Provides checkable congruence-like local rules for a given graph but does not supply (nor falsify here) an all-`n` exact root-reachable equivalence with polynomial class bound for optimal strategies.

## Falsification attempts summary

- **Attempted/identified concrete simpler-class failures:**
  - **C1 (comparability-graph quotient):** fails global exactness of `F` at `n=7` and fails well-defined transition at `n=5` (in-repo deductions/experiments reported in `docs/research-compression-next.md`), so it cannot serve as a universal explicitly checkable two-outcome congruence. Root-reachable-optimal restriction not disproved by those witnesses but not proved exact either.
  - **C2 (`F`/depth/`e` buckets):** fails to be an explicitly checkable two-outcome strategy congruence (not locally sufficient; `e` not sufficient for `F`).
- **No infinite family exhibited** of root-optimal strategies forcing superpolynomially many classes for every equivalence satisfying the stated properties. Producing such a family (or proving none exists) would itself resolve the frontier question; it was **not** achieved here (**inconclusive**).

## Findings (by claim type)

- **Deduction.** Any equivalence meeting the prompt’s conditions (exact on root-reachable optimal histories, explicit two-outcome congruence, polynomially many classes for all `n`) would, when packaged as a verifiable witness, imply a polynomial NP certificate for `SORT-BOUND-U`. This is a high bar given known barriers/open status.
- **Experiment.** For `n<=6`, root-reachable optimal isomorphism quotients are small (`quotient(all)` up to 134) but growing; CG is coarser on that sample with `F` constant there, while `(F,depth)`/`(F,depth,e)` give other coarse partitions without congruence guarantees.
- **Literature/deduction (in-repo).** CG is not a sound global `F`-state/transition quotient (`n=7`/`n=5` witnesses). Framed DAG checking is sound but completeness at optimum is open.
- **Negative result (concrete).** Tested simpler candidates C1, C2 do **not** satisfy all required properties (C1: exact+congruence in general; C2: congruence/checkability).
- **Inconclusive.** Existence/nonexistence of *any* equivalence satisfying all requested properties for all `n`; polynomial bound for root-reachable optimal isomorphism quotient; whether CG becomes exact when restricted to root-reachable optimal histories for all `n`.

## Precise obstacles

1. **Exactness under restriction:** A candidate coarser than poset isomorphism must preserve the minimax-optimal two-outcome behavior on *root-reachable optimal* histories, not merely be value-coincident on observed small-`n` samples.
2. **Two-outcome congruence:** The equivalence must determine, in an explicitly/poly-time checkable way, that applying the same (or canonically specified) comparison to equivalent histories yields equivalent successor histories/classes for **both** outcomes (a local confluence/certificate condition). Global invariants like `F` or `e` alone are insufficient.
3. **All-`n` polynomial class bound:** Even the exact isomorphism quotient on all optimal plays has unknown asymptotic size; proving polynomially many classes for a coarser exact congruence would be a major structural theorem (and would imply NP membership for `SORT-BOUND-U`).
4. **Distinguish states vs histories/certificates:** Poset-state quotients, move-orbit reductions, and framed-certificate joins live at different abstraction levels; “optimal comparison histories” equivalence must be tied to a verifier model.
5. **Known counterexample pressure:** Comparability-graph collapses already break at small universal instances (`n=5,7` in transition/value senses), limiting easy coarsenings.

## Next decisive question

Can one exhibit, for arbitrarily large `n` (or prove impossible), a **root-reachable** pair of comparison histories/states that are (i) distinguishable by every equivalence admitting an explicit local two-outcome congruence compatible with some height-`S(n)` certificate, yet (ii) numerous enough to force superpolynomially many classes; or conversely construct a concrete congruence (e.g. a rank-envelope/guarantee invariant) with a proved polynomial upper bound on classes reachable by at least one optimal strategy for all `n`? A decisive step would be either a formal congruence proof + class-count bound for a specific candidate, or an explicit infinite root-reachable family refuting that candidate.
