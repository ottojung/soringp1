## Model scope

`S(n)` is the minimum worst-case number of adaptive pairwise comparisons to sort `n` distinct labelled keys. `SORT-BOUND-U = {1^n # 1^k : S(n) <= k}` has unary length `N=n+k+1`. `[definition, AGENTS/problem.md]` Sergeev gives an upper-bound sorting procedure/comparison count; it is not an exact `S(n)` evaluator and gives no complexity-class separation. `[deduction]`
