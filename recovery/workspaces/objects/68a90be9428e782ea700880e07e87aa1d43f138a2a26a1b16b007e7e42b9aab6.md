# Exec report: effective-sergeev-bound-finite-extraction

Date: 2026-11-17, billy-dev worktree `soring301-exec-effective-sergeev-20261117` (HEAD `6a96398`, clean except this report). No repo, Lean, branch, PR, or workflow changes beyond this file.

## Question (all n)

Can an effective finite version of Sergeev's bound be extracted: find the smallest `N` and explicit constant `C` such that for all `n ≥ N`,

`S(n) − C(n) ≤ C·n/(log n)^{1/5}`,  `C(n)=⌈log₂ n!⌉`,

test it against the known values `n ≤ 22`, and decide whether the `n=12` obstruction (`S(12)=30`, `C(12)=29`) belongs to the asymptotic regime or is a separate small-n phenomenon. Falsifier: a known `n` violating a stated `(N,C)`, or a demonstration that the published proof's constants are not extractable.

## Method

1. Primary source: Sergeev, *On the asymptotic complexity of sorting*, ECCC TR20-096 (2020), Theorem 3 and its proof (sections 6.1-7), read directly from the PDF. The journal version (2021, DOI 10.1134/S0965542521020111) is the same theorem.
2. Tracked the parameters that produce the `1/5` exponent: `γ=1/5`, `s=ceil(2γ log a)=ceil((2/5) log a)`, `d=2^s`, `μ=30·2^{-s}`, `λ=20·2^{-s/2}+64·2^{-s}`, `ε=1/(25·2^s)`. Lemma 4 requires `s ≥ 20`.
3. Computed `C(n)=⌈log₂ n!⌉` exactly and used the established exact values `S(n)` for `n ≤ 22` (research-plan baseline; Stober-Weiss 2023 for 16-18). Checked the inequality for candidate constants.

## Findings

### Extraction audit

The proof is a parameterized group-insertion construction. The `1/5` enters only through `s=ceil((2/5) log a)` in Theorem 2, with `a≈log₂ n` (interval lengths are `≈ n`) and group size `m≈√n/log n`. Lemma 4 is proved only for `s ≥ 20`. Hence any extraction that follows the published proof needs

`s ≥ 20  ⇒  (2/5) log a ≥ 19  ⇒  a ≥ e^{47.5} (or 2^{47.5})`,

and since `a≈log₂ n`, the threshold is at least

`N ≥ 2^{2^{47.5}}` (double-exponential in `n`),

and larger if the logarithms in `s=ceil((2/5) log a)` are read as natural logs (the paper mixes `ln 2` and powers of two). So the only explicit finite threshold visible in the published proof is astronomically large, not a "reasonable" `N`.

Moreover, the proof does not track the hidden constants in `Theorem 2`/`Corollary 2` (`O(a^{-1/5}2^a)`) or in `Theorem 3` (`O(n log^{-1/5} n)`), and the `a < 100` branch is dismissed with "sufficiently large constants" without values. Therefore **no explicit pair `(N,C)` can be read off from the published proof as written**. This is an audit obstruction, not a proof that no effective bound exists in principle; a full constant-tracking reanalysis (or a different small-`s` construction) would be new work.

### The `n=12` obstruction

`S(12)=30` vs `C(12)=29` is a gap of 1, the first above the information bound. It is **not** an instance of the asymptotic regime: Sergeev's excess is `O(n/(log n)^{1/5})`, which for `n=12` is `≈ 9-10`, two orders of magnitude larger than the actual gap, and the bound is not even in force until astronomically large `n` under the published proof. The `n=12` (and `n=13-19,22`) gaps are local combinatorial forcing (Wells/Peczarski/Stober-Weiss search obstructions), independent of the group-insertion asymptotics. Hence the `n=12` obstruction is a separate small-n phenomenon, not explained by the asymptotic excess coefficient.

## Proved vs conjectured

- **Proved (literature/paper audit):** Sergeev's asymptotic theorem `S(n)=log2(n!)+O(n/(log n)^{1/5})`; the parameter choices and `s ≥ 20` threshold in Lemma 4; the double-exponential lower bound on any `N` extracted by following the published construction.
- **Proved (exact values):** `S(n)` for `n ≤ 22` as tabulated; `C(n)` exact.
- **Conjectured/unresolved:** existence of an effective finite `(N,C)` with small `N`; whether a small-`s` variant (`s=1`, depth 6, hinted in the paper) can make the bound effective at realistic `n`; whether the asymptotic constant can be made explicit at all without a full reanalysis.
- **Not claimed:** no complexity-class consequence follows; this is an upper-bound soundness/audit statement, not a hardness result.

## Stop condition

Reached the second branch: the published proof's constants and finite threshold are **not extractable** as an effective `(N,C)` without new work (constant tracking plus the `a < 100` branch, and likely a small-`s` construction). Known values `n ≤ 22` cannot falsify any reasonable constant; the binding finite phenomenon is the local `n=12` gap, which is separate from the asymptotic excess.
