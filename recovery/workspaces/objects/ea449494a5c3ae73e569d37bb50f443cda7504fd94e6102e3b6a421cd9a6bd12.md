# Framed DAG witness attempt for n=13 (S(13)=34)

## 1. Problem statement and S(13) baseline

**Front:** `framed-dag-witness-n13-exact` (falsifiable, independent).

**Question.** Does there exist an explicit, checkable permutation-framed strategy DAG certificate of exact height `S(13)=34` for `n=13`, and what is its concrete size/depth/profile compared to polynomial envelopes?

**Certificate model (from `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, checked by `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py`).** A certificate is an acyclic directed graph with topologically numbered nodes (`root=0`). Non-leaf nodes specify a comparison `compare=[a,b]` and two forward edges `lt` (`a<b`) / `gt` (`b<a`), each with `next` and a slot permutation `perm` of `[0..n-1]`. Leaves are `{leaf:true}`. The verifier propagates guaranteed strict partial orders, takes transitive closure, renames by the edge permutation, and intersects guarantees at joins; a leaf must totally order all slots and the longest reachable path must be `<= budget`. This is a *soundness* check for an explicit graph (polynomial in explicit graph size and `n`); all-`n` polynomial-size completeness for optimal strategies is open.

**S(13) baseline.**
- `13! = 6,227,020,800`, so the information lower bound is `ceil(log2(13!)) = 33` (`log2(13!) ≈ 32.5359`).
- The exact sorting number is `S(13)=34` (OEIS A036604; cited in-repo in `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`: "S(13)=34; M. Peczarski, *New results in minimum-comparison sorting*, Algorithmica 40(2):133–145, 2004."). Thus an exact-height optimal framed DAG witness, if it exists, must have budget/depth `34`.

**Model fidelity note.** This concerns deterministic adaptive comparison sorting of `n` distinct labelled keys (not sorting networks). Input-length/complexity implications for `SORT-BOUND-U` depend on witness *size* as a function of the unary input, which is part of the question.

## 3. Concrete results/witness or obstruction with evidence

### 3.1 No explicit exact-height-34 framed DAG witness produced/verified

I did **not** construct or verify an explicit permutation-framed strategy DAG certificate of height `34` for `n=13`.

### 3.2 Checked framed DAG witness (best verified height in this attempt): bubble-sort path, height 78

Using the checker’s generator (`triangular_bubble_certificate(13)`) produced an explicit framed DAG certificate and verified it:

- File: `witness_bubble_n13.json`
- `n=13`, `budget=78`
- **Soundness check:** `ACCEPT: n=13, depth=78, nodes=79` (`check_framed_strategy.py --bubble 13` and programmatic `check_certificate` both accept)
- **Counts/profile:** `nodes=79`, `edges=156` (2 per comparison node, 1 leaf), `depth=78` (a single comparison path with branch frames representing compare-exchange; no DAG sharing/joins beyond the linear chain)
- **Comparison to target/polynomial envelopes:** far above the exact optimum (`78` vs `34`, surplus `+44`, factor `~2.29×`). Explicit size is tiny (`O(n^2)` nodes/edges, here 79/156) but depth is non-optimal.

This is a concrete, checked framed certificate (upper bound witness only), not an optimality witness.

### 3.3 Upper-bound context (constructibility signals, not checked framed DAGs here)

Computed reference bounds for `n=13`:
- `F_FJ(13) = Σ_{j=1}^{13} ceil(log2(3j/4)) = 34` (= `S(13)`), i.e. the Ford–Johnson comparison bound matches the exact optimum at 13.
- Standard top-down merge sort worst-case comparisons: `merge_sort_wc(13)=37`.

Interpretation/evidence: `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md` states a paper construction for an `O(n^2)`-node framed DAG for balanced merge sort (height `O(n log n)`, here consistent with 37 worst-case comparisons) and a *plausible* all-`n` polynomial framed certificate for the standard Ford–Johnson upper bound, **subject to checking the complete recursive frame construction and odd case** (not an all-`n` theorem). No implemented/audited generator for a merge-sort or FJ framed DAG certificate exists in the inspected scripts; therefore I did not produce a checked framed DAG at height 37 or 34 from these constructions within this attempt.

### 3.4 Evidence relevant to obstruction / feasibility of explicit optimal framed DAGs

1. **Optimal strategy discovery/enumeration scale.** Exhaustive minimax over permutation states is infeasible at `n=13` (`13!` permutations). Poset-state DP/hash-consing is the standard exact view, but the labelled poset space grows extremely fast (A001035: n=1..6 → 1,3,19,219,4231,130023). `scripts/strategy_dag_probe.py --max-n 5` (saved as `strategy_dag_n5.txt`) shows for optimal strategies:

   | n | S(n) | unshared(1) | DAG(1) | DAG(all) | quotient(all) |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | 3 | 3 | 11 | 11 | 19 | 5 |
   | 4 | 5 | 47 | 47 | 181 | 12 |
   | 5 | 7 | 239 | 239 | 1551 | 19 |

   Consistent with `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md`, `DAG(1)=2n!-1` for small `n` (a single optimal policy is a tree, no hash-consing gain) and `DAG(all)`/quotients grow rapidly (evidence is finite-`n`, not an asymptotic proof). Extrapolating such explicit state-DAG representations to `n=13` is far beyond what can be materialized/checked here.

2. **Certificate availability gap.** Known exact value `S(13)=34` establishes existence of *some* optimal adaptive comparison strategy/tree in the abstract, but not an explicit permutation-framed strategy DAG in the checker’s format with a verified size/profile. Converting a discovered optimal strategy into this framed, join-aware, permutation-explicit DAG (and checking all branches/joins) is a nontrivial constructive step; no such artifact is present in-repo.

3. **Frontier status (literature/paper).** Per `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`: framed certificates are sound and locally checkable; polynomial-size *all-`n` completeness at exact budget `S(n)`* is unresolved/open (key implication for unary NP membership). This frames, but does not by itself prove, impossibility at `n=13`.

**Falsifiable obstruction/evidence statement (precise):** With available in-repo tools/methods, I found no explicit, checkable permutation-framed strategy DAG of height `34` for `n=13`. The checked explicit witness obtained is the linear bubble framed DAG at height `78` (`79` nodes, `156` edges). The combination of (a) infeasibility of exhaustive optimal-strategy/DAG enumeration at `n=13`, (b) absence of an implemented/audited framed merge/FJ generator yielding an exact-34 certificate, and (c) rapid small-`n` growth of explicit optimal state DAGs constitutes concrete evidence of a *constructive availability/scale* obstruction for an explicit exact-height-34 framed DAG witness at present, while leaving open whether a compact exact-height-34 framed DAG exists.
