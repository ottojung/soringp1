# Exec report: `info-bound-gap-invariant-alln`

**Frontier (as stated).** Can a polynomial-time computable invariant (or a concrete family of invariants) track or upper/lower-bound the information-theoretic gap `g(n)=S(n)-ceil(log2(n!))` for all `n`, or provide a barrier showing no such invariant of a stated class can? State the consequence for evaluating `S(n)` / `SORT-BOUND-U`.

**Status of this pass.** Precise question formulated; primary-literature-aware barriers and refutations recorded for several natural invariant classes; **no all-`n` tracking/lower-bound invariant found, and no new theorem proved**. Main outcome is a **precise blocker decomposition**: exact tracking is equivalent to exact evaluation; slack/leaf-count scalar invariants are information-insufficient; and Sergeev's asymptotic `o(n)` excess does not currently yield an effective, polynomial-time computable all-`n` upper certificate.

**Scope/claims discipline.** Labels used: `[definition]`, `[literature]`, `[deduction]`, `[experiment/support]`, `[blocker]`. No complexity-class separation, no NP/coNP membership, and no Lean result is claimed. A comparison lower/upper bound on `S(n)` is not by itself a lower/upper bound for *computing* `S(n)` except through the explicit consequences stated below.

## 1. Definitions and baseline facts

- `[definition]` `S(n)` is the exact adaptive worst-case comparison-sorting number (model in `docs/problem.md`). `C(n)=ceil(log2(n!))` is the information lower bound. Define the gap `g(n)=S(n)-C(n) >= 0`.
- `[literature]` Known exact values with gaps (`docs/literature.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`): `g(n)=0` for `1 <= n <= 11`; `S(12)=30, C(12)=29, g(12)=1` (Wells); `S(16)=46,C(16)=45`, `S(17)=50,C(17)=49`, `S(18)=54,C(18)=53` so `g=1` there (Stober–Weiss 2023). Values for all `n<=22` are known in the literature; `n=23` open.
- `[experiment/support]` Independent arithmetic check in this worktree confirms `C(12)=29`, `C(16)=45`, `C(17)=49`, `C(18)=53` and the corresponding listed gaps.
- `[literature]` Sergeev (ECCC TR20-096, 2020, Thm. 3): `S(n)=log2(n!) + O(n/(log n)^(1/5))`, hence `g(n)=o(n)`. The note `docs/research-sergeev-asymptotic.md` records the crucial limitation: asymptotic big-O alone does not supply explicit constants or a finite computable upper-bound function for every input; Ford–Johnson remains an explicit finite upper bound.
- `[literature/deduction]` `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` (project baseline) and sparse/tally consequences constrain separations; unconditional super-polynomial lower bounds for unary evaluation are out of scope as separations from open classes (`docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`).

## 2. Sharp, falsifiable all-`n` question

Let the input be unary `1^n` (length `n`).

**Q (effective gap-window invariant).** Is there a function `W : N -> N` computable in time `poly(n)` such that for all `n >= 1`,
1. `C(n) <= S(n) <= C(n) + W(n)` (a uniform upper bound on the gap), and
2. `W(n)` is *effective and sublinear* in the sense needed to shrink the exact-evaluation search window asymptotically beyond the fixed merge-sort/Ford–Johnson window, e.g. `W(n)=o(n)` with an explicit finite definition?

A falsifier/refutation target can be chosen by class:
- exhibit, for a proposed explicit `W`, some `n` with `S(n) > C(n)+W(n)` (disproves soundness of that upper invariant); or
- show that no `W` in a stated computable class (e.g. derived from `n, log2(n!)` only, or from any leaf-count/slack scalar of the initial state alone) can satisfy (1) for all `n`; or
- show that extracting an explicit all-`n` `W` from Sergeev requires an unbridged non-effective step under a stated formalization.

**Why this is sharp for evaluation.** `C(n)` is polynomial-time computable from `n` to sufficient precision/ceil form for the threshold window context. A sound explicit `W(n)` as above would reduce the unknown budget interval for deciding `S(n)<=k` / locating `S(n)` from an `O(n log n)`-scale window to `[C(n), C(n)+W(n)]`, changing the search/verification target for uniform evaluation even if it does not by itself place `SORT-BOUND-U` in `P`.

## 3. Consequences for evaluating `S(n)` / `SORT-BOUND-U`

- `[deduction]` **Exact tracking => exact evaluation:** if `I(n)` is computable in `poly(n)` and `I(n)=g(n)` for all `n`, then `S(n)=C(n)+I(n)` is computable in `poly(n)` (given `C(n)`), hence `S_unary in FP` and equivalently `SORT-BOUND-U in P` (unary threshold equivalence, `docs/problem.md`).
- `[deduction]` **Sound poly-time upper-gap invariant => narrowed decision window:** if `W(n)` is poly-time computable and `S(n)<=C(n)+W(n)` for all `n`, then `SORT-BOUND-U(1^n#1^k)` is immediately YES when `k>=C(n)+W(n)` and immediately NO when `k<C(n)`, leaving only `W(n)+1` candidate budgets. This is an upper-bound/certification aid; it does not alone imply `SORT-BOUND-U in NP`, `P`, or any separation.
- `[deduction]` **Sound poly-time positive lower-gap invariant on infinitely many `n` => nontrivial structural lower bounds:** a poly-time computable `L(n)` with `0 <= L(n) <= g(n)` and `L(n)>=1` on an explicit infinite family would give uniform proofs that the information bound is not tight there. No such all-`n`-useful invariant is established here.
- `[deduction]` **Barrier reading:** proving that a broad natural class cannot provide any nontrivial all-`n` upper/lower gap control would be a *methods barrier* (it would rule out that class as a route to narrowing/evaluating `S(n)`), not a complexity separation.

## 4. Primary-literature-aware reasoning and refutation attempts

### 4.1 Initial-state slack / scalar functions of `n!` only (`class C_leaf`)

`[definition]` Let `C_leaf` be invariants depending only on `n` through `C(n)` or slack ratio `r(n)=2^{C(n)}/n!` (poly-time computable from `n`).

- `[literature/deduction]` Peczarski (2012, cited in `docs/research-n12-nontightness.md`) shows the efficiency refinement used in searches is equivalent to the node-level information bound, not a new scalar strengthening.
- `[experiment/literature synthesis in repo]` `docs/research-n12-nontightness.md` records: `r(5)` is smaller than `r(12)` yet `g(5)=0,g(12)=1`; tightness/gap is not monotone in slack ratio across known values. Therefore no function `f(r(n))` or `f(C(n),n!)` alone tracks `g(n)`; `C_leaf` cannot be a sound+complete gap invariant and provides no nontrivial *lower* witness of `g(n)>=1` beyond cases where leaf counting already fails by exhaustive search.
- **Refutation outcome for Q on `C_leaf`:** `C_leaf` gives the baseline lower side (`g(n)>=0`) but no all-`n` upper control `W(n)` and no predictive lower gap; class refuted as a gap-tracking/lower-bounding family.

### 4.2 Local extension-count / information invariants on posets (`class C_e`)

`[definition]` `C_e`: invariants computable from linear-extension counts `e(P)` (or `ceil(log2 e(P))`) of states.

- `[deduction/literature]` Global bound `S(P) >= ceil(log2 e(P))` is standard. But `docs/research-n12-nontightness.md` and `docs/research-one-ply-obstruction.md` show: at `n=5` there exist posets with same `e=8` but different minimax depths `m=3,4`; sortability is not a function of `e` (and not monotone in `e`, smallest `n=6` witness cited there). Peczarski's `n=13` bottleneck example is the literature-scale instance.
- `[deduction]` Since even local state optimal depth is not determined by `e(P)`, any global `g(n)` predictor obtained solely by propagating/aggregating `e`-information cannot be complete/certifying except where it coincides with full minimax; one-ply strengthening `L1` (`docs/research-one-ply-obstruction.md`) is sound as a local prune/lower bound (`L0 <= L1 <= F`) but is not shown computable in `poly(n)` in general (depends on extension counting / enumeration), and does not yield a uniform all-`n` closed form for `g(n)`.
- **Refutation outcome:** `C_e` is information-insufficient for tracking; computational status blocks it as a stated polynomial-time global invariant.

### 4.3 Certified merge/structural add-on invariants (`class C_merge`)

- `[experiment/deduction]` `docs/research-structural-lower-bounds.md` gives a sound compositional lower bound improving leaf counting on certified merge factors / block-local ceilings (smallest merge strict witness `Merge(2,4), n=6`, `F=5 > ceil(log2 15)=4`), with monotonicity checks for `n<=5` exhaustive/targeted `n=6`.
- `[deduction]` These explain local gaps on special reachable states, but `S(n)` is the antichain root value. A global lower bound `g(n)>=1` would require showing every root strategy encounters an unavoidable certified obstruction for that `n` — a quantified adversary statement over all strategies, not supplied by existence of some loose posets. No reduction from these local merge witnesses to `g(12)>=1` or to an infinite family is established.
- **Refutation outcome:** useful local sound lower bounds, but not an all-`n` root-gap invariant without a missing universal strategy argument.

### 4.4 Effective extraction from Sergeev (`class C_Sergeev-eff`)

- `[literature]` Sergeev proves an algorithmic upper bound `S(n) <= log2(n!) + O(n/(log n)^(1/5))`.
- `[blocker]` The asymptotic form is not an explicit finite `W(n)`: constants and lower-order terms needed for a statement valid for every `n` are not extracted in available project notes/sources consulted here (`docs/research-sergeev-asymptotic.md`). Therefore `C_Sergeev-eff` = "take Sergeev's hidden constant as a computed function of `n` in poly time" is **not currently instantiated**.
- **Falsification attempt (paper):** could one bound all hidden constants by inspecting the constructive proof? Possibly in principle, but this pass did not produce such an extraction and cannot claim it; any claim of effective `W(n)` would require a separate finite audit of the group-insertion construction.
- **Outcome:** asymptotic existence of sublinear excess is known; effective polynomial-time computable all-`n` upper-gap invariant from it remains an open extraction task / blocker.

## 5. Small exact discrimination computation

`[experiment/support]` Trivial arithmetic recomputation of `C(n)=ceil(log2(n!))` for literature-known exact `S(n)` points `{1..12,16,17,18}` was performed (Node in `execute`) and matched the stated gaps (`0` through `n=11`, `1` at `12,16,17,18`). This does **not** discriminate the deep all-`n` question except to confirm the nonzero-gap phenomenon begins at `n=12`; it guards against a reporting error in this note.

## 6. Bottom line

- **Formulated question (Q):** existence of an explicit, polynomial-time computable, sound all-`n` upper-gap window invariant `W(n)` with effective sublinear control (and analogous precise class-based lower-bound/tracking variants).
- **What is supported:** exact gap tracking in poly time would imply `S_unary in FP` / `SORT-BOUND-U in P`; natural scalar classes `C_leaf`, `C_e` are refuted/inadequate for tracking by information and known counterexamples; local merge obstructions are sound but not globally quantified; Sergeev gives asymptotic `o(n)` excess but no effective poly-time all-`n` `W(n)` is extracted here.
- **Precise blocker:** bridge asymptotic group-insertion excess to an explicit finite, polynomial-time computable `W(n)` valid for all `n`, or else prove a class-specific impossibility/barrier for such effective extraction/invariants. Either would materially affect how sharply `S(n)` can be uniformly bounded/evaluated.
- **No plausible bridge found here** to an immediate exact evaluator or complexity-class separation; no claim of theorem completion.
