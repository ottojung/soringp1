**Model context.** Active target is unary `SORT-BOUND-U = { 1^n # 1^k : S(n) <= k }` over `A(w)=(W,<,One,Hash)`, `w=1^n#1^k`, `N=n+k+1`; `S(n)` is the exact adaptive comparison sorting number / minimax height. `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` is already established in project docs, so unconditional super-polynomial lower bounds for this unary language are not the frontier; this map tracks a proposed *upper-bound/capture* closure chain and its obligations. See `docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-certificates-barriers.md`.

**Status labels used.**
- **discharged (paper-level)** — the cited artifact gives the claimed formula/proof at paper level for that link, subject only to explicitly stated upstream assumptions.
- **conditional** — the link is discharged only modulo separately stated semantic/capture/model assumptions or upstream closures.
- **open** — a required proof obligation is not discharged.
- **contradictory** — the cited fix/proof contains an internal inconsistency as written (audit-identified), so the obligation remains open.

**Global non-claims.** Nothing below claims `P`, `FO(LFP)` on the bare ordered word, `NP`, `coNP`, binary `SORT-BOUND-B` progress, a separation, or a complete end-to-end discharged FO(PFP)/IFP theorem unless every link and every inherited assumption below is independently discharged.

## 7. What would still NOT yield complete end-to-end discharged FO(PFP)/IFP or P/NP/coNP

Even after fixing the currently open/contradictory items, the following would **still not** by themselves give a complete end-to-end discharged FO(PFP)/IFP theorem, and none of this would establish `P`, `NP`, `coNP`, or a separation:

- **Discharging SYM-TOP alone**: fixes global `Top` bookkeeping, not full `M_D^alt/δ` realization, halting, or all semantic equivalences.
- **Discharging PACK-REACH normal form alone**: gives the right whole-configuration acceptance *form* only; it remains conditional on a fully closed deterministic `Δ_D/M_D^alt` operator and semantic/capture assumptions.
- **Discharging MDALT halting alone** (`[CKS-AV-MDALT-HALT.BACK_E_RANK]` fix): would complete a termination argument for the stated schema, but not by itself discharge P1 macro-compilation, full machine-checked semantics, or the end-to-end FO(PFP)/IFP capture instantiation unless those are also closed.
- **A paper-level DFS/model bridge**: does not become a kernel-checked theorem without Lean/formal correspondence proofs and axiom/sorry-free checking.
- **An FO(PFP)/IFP sentence for `SORT-BOUND-U` on ordered unary encodings** (if fully discharged): would be a descriptive/capture-style upper-bound/membership articulation in that presentation, **not** a proof that `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ P`, not `FO(LFP)` on the bare ordered word unless separately proved in that weaker form, not `NP`/`coNP` membership/separation, and not any collapse/separation consequence beyond what standard complexity/model facts already imply.
- **No separation/P-vs-NP/coNP claim is supported.** Given `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE` (project docs), `SORT-BOUND-U ∉ P` would imply `P ≠ PSPACE`, and `SORT-BOUND-U ∉ NP` would imply `NP ≠ PSPACE`; neither is targeted or proved here. `SORT-BOUND-U` is sparse/tally, so Mahaney-style and nonuniform-circuit barriers remain relevant as constraints, not as positive hardness proofs.
