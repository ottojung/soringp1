## 1. `I_{M_D}` assumptions used by target 1 `[inherited assumption][open]`

Target 1 is **conditional**. The row-level totality/preservation below uses only
the following `I_{M_D}` assumptions where named; each is a residual obligation
unless discharged elsewhere.

- **(I1) Halt/top exclusivity domain.** On reachable states, either `HaltAcc ∨
  HaltRej` (absorbing) or there is a well-formed active top discipline consistent
  with `ExactlyOneTop` where `¬HaltAcc∧¬HaltRej`.
- **(I2) Unique active top precondition for push/pop.** For push rows
  `Top(c̄)` and for pop rows `Top(c̄)∧Parent(c̄,p̄)∧FrameLive(c̄)∧FrameLive(p̄)`
  hold on the consuming state.
- **(I3) Functional `CurBase`/`SuccCell`.** `CurBase` is unique/functional on
  the intended `Cell` encoding and `SuccCell` is functional where bump is
  applied (`CurBaseBump` yields a unique `b̄'`).
- **(I4) `[SYM-TOP]` applicability / `FreshChild` freshness.** On push rows,
  `FreshChild(d̄):=CurBase(d̄)` produces the intended fresh frame address and
  `ChildFrameBase(d̄,c̄)` is applicable without aliasing a required live/preserved
  frame in a way that breaks `LowerStackPreservedExcept` (`Enc` lemma
  `FreshChild_Fresh_On_IMD` remains `[open]`).
- **(I5) Pop target is explicit live parent.** On pop rows, `p̄` is the explicit
  `Parent(c̄,p̄)` receiver required by the guard (`Parent_Functional_Active_Preserved`
  remains `[open]` as a global reachability invariant; row-level target 1 only
  needs the explicit guard-supplied `p̄`).
- **(I6) Marker clauses are explicit on receivers.** Rows 4,5,9,12,14,17,19
  apply `MarkRetAccOn(p̄)` or `MarkRetRejOn(p̄)` and therefore exclude `p̄` from
  generic `FrameEq'` (fix 2). Target 1 uses only their `FrameLive'(p̄)` effect.

Non-`I_{M_D}` malformed states are outside the deterministic-successor domain.
