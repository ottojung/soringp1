## 2. Trace model for BackE-producing pops and consumers

`[definition]` A **BackE-reject-producing pop to BackE** is a transition step
`E --D_i--> E'` such that:
```text
i ∈ {5,12},  D_i(E,c̄,p̄) holds,
M_BackE(E,p̄) holds (receiver is a BackE frame),
E' satisfies MarkRetRejOn(p̄) ∧ Top'(p̄) ∧ FrameInactive'(c̄).
```
This is the schema-intended case; whether row-5 parents are always Back* is
`[open MarkerReceiver_Is_BackStar]` and is not assumed proved.

`[deduction]` **Immediate row-5 effect (schema).** If `D_eval_rej_ret(E,c̄,p̄)`
and `M_BackE(E,p̄)`, then by `Globals_pop(p̄,c̄)` and `MarkRetRejOn(p̄)`:
```text
Top(E',p̄) ∧ FrameLive(E',p̄) ∧ M_BackE(E',p̄) ∧ RetRej(E',p̄)
∧ CurA(E',p̄,·)=CurA(E,p̄,·) ∧ CurB(E',p̄,·)=CurB(E,p̄,·)
∧ HasCur(E',p̄)<->HasCur(E,p̄)
∧ PosF(E',p̄,·,·)=PosF(E,p̄,·,·) ∧ UsedF(E',p̄,·)=UsedF(E,p̄,·)
∧ HasSel/SelA/SelB/Parent/Ctrl of p̄ preserved from E.
```
Thus row 5, when targeting a BackE parent, produces a state satisfying the
guard shape `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` with the parent's pre-pop cursor/PosF/UsedF.

`[deduction]` **Immediate row-12 effect (schema).** `D_backE_rej_exh_ret`
consumes `Top(c̄)∧M_BackE(c̄)∧RetRej(c̄)∧NNI_c` and, when `Parent(c̄,p̄)` is
used, performs `Globals_pop(p̄,c̄) ∧ MarkRetRejOn(p̄)`. Therefore, if
`M_BackE(E,p̄)` also holds, row 12 produces `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` on `p̄` with
`p̄`'s pre-pop cursor/PosF/UsedF preserved. The NNI condition consumed is the
one on `c̄`'s cursor, not a condition on `p̄`; row 12 does not establish
`NI_{p̄}` or `NNI_{p̄}` except through `p̄`'s preserved fields.

`[deduction]` **Consumers of `BackE_RetRej_Top(c̄)`.** From the 19-row matrix,
the only rows whose guard contains `Top(c̄)∧M_BackE(c̄)∧RetRej(c̄)` are 10, 11,
12. They are partitioned by `NI_c`/`NNI_c` and `Root(c̄)`/`NonRoot(c̄)`:
row 10 needs `NI_c`; rows 11,12 need `NNI_c` and split on root/non-root.
Therefore a state satisfying `BackE_RetRej_Top(c̄)` is consumed by exactly one
of 10/11/12 iff `NI_c xor NNI_c` and root status is determinate (and no other
row overlaps) — i.e. iff `BackE_NI_NNI_Total_Complement` and the surrounding
matrix exclusion/invariant facts hold.

## 3. Obligation 1: `BackE_Cursor_Preservation`

`[open][lemma BackE_Cursor_Preservation]` (target)
If a BackE-reject-producing pop row (`D_eval_rej_ret` or
`D_backE_rej_exh_ret`, under the BackE-receiver case) produces
`Top'(p̄), M_BackE'(p̄), RetRej'(p̄)` from an `I_{M_D}`-reachable state, then
`HasCur',CurA',CurB',PosF',UsedF',HasSel',SelA',SelB',Parent',Ctrl',Mode'` of
`p̄` are preserved until the next consuming BackE `RetRej` step on `p̄`.

### 3.1 Inductive/trace argument from normalized schema

`[deduction]` **Base producer steps.**
- Row 5: `MarkRetRejOn(p̄)` includes `PreserveNonMarkerFields(p̄)`, and the
  normalized row text states preservation of `Mode/HasCur/CurA/CurB/PosF/UsedF/
  HasSel/SelA/SelB/Parent/Ctrl` of `p̄`. `S={p̄,c̄}`; `c̄` becomes inactive;
  `LowerStackPreservedExcept({p̄,c̄})` preserves frames other than `p̄,c̄`.
  Globals use `Globals_pop(p̄,c̄)`. No clause mutates `CurA/CurB/PosF/UsedF/...`
  of `p̄`.
- Row 12, BackE-receiver case: identical marker/lower-stack schema, consuming
  BackE+RetRej+NNI on `c̄` and producing `MarkRetRejOn(p̄)`.

Thus, conditional on the row applying to a BackE `p̄`, the producer step itself
preserves the named fields of `p̄`.

`[deduction]` **Inductive gap (intervening steps).** After producing
`Top'(p̄) ∧ M_BackE'(p̄) ∧ RetRej'(p̄)`, the next consuming step on `p̄` is row
10, 11, or 12 only when `p̄` is the current `Top`. Between the producer step and
that consumption, can any row change `p̄`'s cursor/PosF/UsedF/markers?
- If `p̄` remains `Top`, no other row consumes `M_BackE∧RetRej` except 10/11/12
  (matrix partition). So no non-consuming intermediate step occurs while `p̄` is
  `Top`.
- If `p̄` is not `Top`, only a push/pop touching `p̄` or an ill-formed global
  mutation could change it. Normalized push rows have `S={c,d}` and preserve
  lower stack except that set; pop rows preserve lower stack except `{p,c}` of
  the active pop. Therefore `p̄` is preserved unless it is in the changed set of
  a later step — but being in `S` of a later pop that targets `p̄` would mean a
  child above `p̄` returned to `p̄`, which is exactly the next marker-producing
  step on `p̄`, not a cursor mutation except through `MarkRet*On`.

This argument is a `[deduction]` from the schema shape but depends on
`[open Enc_Component_Closure]`, `[open No_Eval_RetMarker_Top / marker-receiver
coherence]`, and `[open Parent_Functional_Active_Preserved /
FreshChild_Fresh_On_IMD]` (to ensure `p̄` is not aliased, and only intended
stack operations touch it).

### 3.2 Verdict for obligation 1

`[deduction]` **Verdict: not refuted by schema; remains open as an
`I_{M_D}`-reachability/component-closure proof obligation.** The normalized row
clauses explicitly preserve BackE cursor/PosF/UsedF/non-`Ret` fields in rows 5
and 12 producer splits. No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying witness was
found in the audited sources.

`[falsification]` Witness shape (from closure obligations):
```text
I_{M_D}(E) ∧ D_eval_rej_ret(E,c̄,p̄) ∧ M_BackE(E,p̄) ∧ E --D_eval_rej_ret--> E'
∧ E' ⊢* E'' before any consuming row 10/11/12 on p̄
∧ (CurA(E'',p̄,·) != CurA(E',p̄,·) ∨ CurB differs ∨ HasCur differs
   ∨ PosF differs ∨ UsedF differs ∨ Mode(E'',p̄)!=M_BackE).
```
Any such reachable trace falsifies `BackE_Cursor_Preservation` and can make
rows 10/11/12 consult a stale/corrupted cursor.

## 4. Obligation 2: `BackE_NI_NNI_Total_Complement`

`[open][lemma BackE_NI_NNI_Total_Complement]` (target)
For every `I_{M_D}`-reachable `E` with `BackE_RetRej_Top(E,p̄)`:
```text
∃!a∃!b(CurA(p̄,a) ∧ CurB(p̄,b))
∧ (NI_p(E) xor NNI_p(E))
∧ (NI_p(E) -> ∃!x∃!y NextIncomp_p(a,b;x,y)).
```

### 4.1 Mathematical/definitional layer

`[deduction]` For fixed `(a,b)` and fixed frame-local `PosF(p̄,·,·)` (hence
fixed `Incomp_p`/`P_p` interpretation used by `NextIncomp_p`), over the finite
domain `Cell×Cell` with fixed strict total order `Lex2`:
```text
NI_p(a,b)  := ∃x∃y(Lex2(a,b,x,y) ∧ Incomp_p(x,y)
               ∧ ¬∃u∃v(Lex2(a,b,u,v)∧Lex2(u,v,x,y)∧Incomp_p(u,v)))
NNI_p(a,b) := ¬∃x∃y(Lex2(a,b,x,y) ∧ Incomp_p(x,y))
```
are complements by definition (`NNI_p(a,b)` is `¬∃x∃y` of the NI candidate
set's membership before minimality, which is equivalent to the candidate set
being empty; `NI_p(a,b)` asserts a minimal element exists). Moreover, if the
candidate set `{(x,y) | Lex2(a,b,x,y) ∧ Incomp_p(x,y)}` is non-empty, `Lex2`'s
strict total order on a finite domain gives a unique least element, so the NI
witness is unique. Thus, **given well-defined total `Lex2`/`Incomp_p` and a
unique cursor `(a,b)`**, `NI_p xor NNI_p` and NI-witness uniqueness are
`[deduction]`s.

### 4.2 Reachability/schema layer (open)

`[open]` The definitional complement does not by itself discharge the lemma on
reachable machine states. The following are still required:
- **Unique cursor.** `I_{M_D}` must give `∃!a∃!b(CurA(p̄,a)∧CurB(p̄,b))` and
  `HasCur(p̄)` coherence whenever `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` is reachable. This is
  part of global `I_{M_D}` preservation / Enc component closure.
- **Sentinel representation.** Fix 3 introduces `BackE_NoFurther(x',y') :=
  SentinelLast(x',y')` as a cursor state “equivalent to forcing NoNextIncomp_c
  for the stored cursor”. If `SentinelLast` is outside the ordinary `Lex2`
  domain of comparable pairs, `NextIncomp_c`/`NoNextIncomp_c` must be defined so
  their meaning is total on that cursor value. If the implementation instead
  stores an ordinary maximal pair `(x',y')` with no `Lex2` successors, then
  `NoNextIncomp_c(x',y')` holds definitionally. The normalized text states the
  sentinel is equivalent to forcing NNI but does not give the full FO/cell-level
  sentinel semantics.
- **PosF/Incomp coherence.** `NI_c/NNI_c` read `Incomp_c`, which depends on
  `PosF`/`P_c`. Reachable states must keep `PosF` coherent with the intended
  partial-order snapshot for the BackE frame; no-spurious local alignment and
  `I_{M_D}` preservation remain open.

### 4.3 Verdict for obligation 2

`[deduction]` **Verdict: definitional NI/NNI complement and NI-witness
uniqueness hold for a fixed total `Lex2` and fixed `PosF` snapshot with unique
cursor; full `I_{M_D}`-reachability closure remains open.** No explicit
`I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying witness was found in the audited sources.

`[falsification]` Gap/overlap witness shape:
```text
I_{M_D}(E) ∧ BackE_RetRej_Top(E,p̄) ∧
  (¬NI_p(E) ∧ ¬NNI_p(E)
   ∨ NI_p(E) ∧ NNI_p(E)
   ∨ no unique CurA/CurB
   ∨ NI_p(E) with two distinct witnesses (x1,y1),(x2,y2)).
```
A sentinel-encoding form of the first case is especially relevant:
```text
I_{M_D}(E) ∧ BackE_RetRej_Top(E,p̄)
∧ CurA(p̄,s1) ∧ CurB(p̄,s2) ∧ SentinelLast(s1,s2)
∧ NI_p(E)   -- sentinel wrongly treated as allowing a Lex2 successor/incomparable
```
which would falsify the intended BackE exhaustion behavior.

## 5. Obligation 3: `BackE_AfterSelected_NoReselect`

`[open][lemma BackE_AfterSelected_NoReselect]` (target)
After selecting `(x,y)` by `NextIncomp_p(a,b;x,y)` and applying
`SetParentBackE_Advance(p̄,x,y)` (row 6 or row 10), a later `I_{M_D}`-reachable
consuming state `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` cannot NI-witness `(x,y)` again
(including the `IsLastLex2` sentinel case).

### 5.1 Non-last case (`SuccLex2`)

`[deduction]` Assume row 6 or 10 selects unique witness `(x,y)` with
`NextIncomp_p(a,b;x,y)` on BackE parent `p̄`'s current cursor `(a,b)`, and
`¬IsLastLex2(x,y)`. `SetParentBackE_Advance(p̄,x,y)` stores unique cursor
`(x',y')` with `SuccLex2(x,y,x',y')`. Since `SuccLex2` is the immediate `Lex2`
successor of `(x,y)`, `Lex2(x,y,x',y')` and there is no `z` with
`Lex2(x,y,z) ∧ Lex2(z,x',y')`.

Now consider any later consuming `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` whose consulted cursor
is still `(x',y')` (the preservation case of obligation 1). `NI_p` from
`(x',y')` has form
```text
NextIncomp_p(x',y'; x*,y*) => Lex2(x',y',x*,y*) ∧ Incomp_p(x*,y*) ∧ minimal.
```
By transitivity of strict total `Lex2`, `Lex2(x,y,x') ∧ Lex2(x',y',x*,y*)`
implies `Lex2(x,y,x*,y*)`, and `(x*,y*) != (x,y)`. Therefore reselecting
`(x,y)` is impossible while the stored cursor is the after-selected successor.

### 5.2 Last-pair/sentinel case (`IsLastLex2`)

`[deduction]` Assume row 6 or 10 selects `(x,y)` and `IsLastLex2(x,y)`.
`AfterSelected(x,y,x',y')` gives `BackE_NoFurther(x',y')`, i.e.
`SentinelLast(x',y')`. The normalized note requires this sentinel to be
equivalent to forcing `NoNextIncomp_c` for the stored cursor.

Under that intended semantics, any later consuming `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` with
cursor `(x',y')` has `NNI_p`, so row 10 (`NI_p`) cannot fire and rows 11/12
(`NNI_p`) consume it as exhaustion. In particular, `NI_p(x,y)` cannot hold
because NI requires `NI_p` and `NI_p` is false. Thus reselect of `(x,y)` is
prevented **if** `BackE_NoFurther` is semantically equivalent to `NNI_p` on the
stored cursor.

### 5.3 Is the convention sufficient?

`[deduction]` **Sufficient at the trace level, conditional on sentinel
semantics and preservation.** The after-selected convention is the right shape:
- it moves the cursor strictly past the selected pair in the non-last case;
- it moves to an NNI-forcing cursor in the last case.

It is **not by itself a discharged proof** because of the following
falsifiable gaps.

`[open]` **Gap A — sentinel semantics not fully instantiated.** The schema
states “equivalent to forcing NoNextIncomp_c for the stored cursor” but does
not fix the cell-level representation/interpretation. Two concrete realizations
behave differently:
1. **Max-pair realization:** if `IsLastLex2(x,y)` selects the actual `Lex2`
   maximum of `Cell×Cell`, set `(x',y')=(x,y_max?)` — but storing `(x,y)` would
   reselect; so a true max-pair realization must instead set a designated pair
   after which there are no domain elements (impossible inside total finite
   `Cell×Cell`) or use an extra flag/marker.
2. **Sentinel flag/value realization:** add an explicit `BackE_NoFurther` bit
   or a reserved sentinel value in the cursor encoding, and define
   `NI_c := HasNormalCursor(c̄,a,b) ∧ ∃x∃y NextIncomp_c(a,b;x,y)`,
   `NNI_c := NoNormalCursor(c̄) ∨ (HasNormalCursor(c̄,a,b) ∧ NoNextIncomp_c(a,b))`.
   This is a minimal corrected convention and is consistent with the note's
   stated intent.

`[open]` **Gap B — cursor not preserved / wrong cursor consulted.**
Obligation 1 is needed. If a BackE-reject-producing pop, intervening step, or a
wrong frame/field read changes `CurA/CurB` before row 10/11/12, the
after-selected guarantee is moot.

`[open]` **Gap C — row 10/11/12 use a cursor inconsistent with `AfterSelected`.**
The consumers read `NI_c/NNI_c` from the current `CurA,CurB`. This is correct
only if those are the after-selected cursor installed by row 6/10 and preserved
through any row-5/12 production. The schema states this, but `I_{M_D}`
reachability proof is open.

`[open]` **Gap D — `NextIncomp_c` first-argument meaning.** The normalized
formulas use `NextIncomp_c(a,b;x,y)` with `Lex2(a,b,x,y)` (strictly after
`(a,b)`). With `AfterSelected` storing `SuccLex2(x,y,x',y')`, row 10/11/12
correctly search after `(x',y')`. The convention is coherent in that direction.
A realization that mistakenly stores `(x,y)` or interprets `NextIncomp_c` as
`>= (a,b)` would reselect `(x,y)`.

### 5.4 Verdict for obligation 3

`[deduction]` **Verdict: not refuted as stated; the `AfterSelected`/last-pair
convention is trace-sufficient only under an explicit NNI-forcing sentinel (or
equivalent cursor-exhausted flag) and open preservation/reachability lemmas.
The convention is insufficiently instantiated if `IsLastLex2 -> BackE_NoFurther`
is left as an uninterpreted sentinel inside the ordinary `Lex2` domain without
adapting `NI_c/NNI_c`. No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying witness was
found in the audited sources, but a realizability/sentinel-encoding falsifier
shape is identified below.**

`[falsification]` Last-pair reselect witness shape (normalized note):
```text
IsLastLex2(x,y) ∧ SetParentBackE_Advance(p̄,x,y) ⊢*
  BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄) ∧ NI_p(x,y).
```

`[falsification]` Sentinel-realization witness shape (this note):
```text
I_{M_D}(E0) ∧ D_sort_pick(E0,p̄,...) selects NI witness (x,y) ∧ IsLastLex2(x,y)
∧ E0 --D_sort_pick--> E1 stores cursor c1 = (x',y') by BackE_NoFurther
∧ E1 ⊢* E2 with BackE-reject-producing pop to p̄ preserving c1
∧ E2 has BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄) ∧ Cur(p̄)=c1
∧ NI_p(E2) holds because c1 is interpreted as a normal Lex2 position
            (or because NI/NNI do not consult the no-further marker),
causing row 10 to select (x,y) again or another spurious/already-tried pair.
```
This falsifies no-reselect/BackE progress at the last-pair locus.

`[open]` **Minimal corrected convention (if sentinel is not given a full
NI/NNI-consistent encoding):**
