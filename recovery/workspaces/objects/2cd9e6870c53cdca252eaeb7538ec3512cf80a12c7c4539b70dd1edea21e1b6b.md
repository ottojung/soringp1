## 1. Normalized exhausted-cursor component (BackE v2)

`[definition]` BackE v2 extends the normalized realization with an explicit
cursor-exhaustion component; the sentinel is not an uninterpreted ordinary
`CurA/CurB` pair consulted without a state bit.

```text
Rbar adds CurExhausted.
Fld(c̄) := ⟨Mode,Ret,Ctrl,UsedF,HasSel,SelA,SelB,
            HasCur,CurA,CurB,CurExhausted,PosF,Parent⟩.

CurNormalState(c̄,a,b) := ¬CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ HasCur(c̄) ∧ CurA(c̄,a) ∧ CurB(c̄,b).
CurNormalState(c̄)     := ∃a∃b CurNormalState(c̄,a,b).
CurExhaustedState(c̄)  := CurExhausted(c̄) ∧ HasCur(c̄)
                        ∧ CurA(c̄,MinKey) ∧ CurB(c̄,MinKey)
                        ∧ ¬CurNormalState(c̄).

CursorCoherent(c̄) :=
      (CurNormalState(c̄) ∧ ∃!a∃!b(CurA(c̄,a)∧CurB(c̄,b)) ∧ ¬CurExhausted(c̄))
    ⊕ (CurExhaustedState(c̄)).
```

`[definition]` The concrete exhausted encoding is
`SentinelNull := (MinKey,MinKey)` with `CurExhausted=true`; it is **never**
read as `CurNormalState` because `CurNormalState` requires `¬CurExhausted`.
Initialization uses `(MinKey,MinKey)` with `¬CurExhausted` (normal initial
cursor), so cursor readers must consult `CurExhausted`, not the pair value
alone.

`[definition]` `FrameFieldsEq'` includes `CurExhausted`; `FrameEq'`,
`LowerStackPreservedExcept(S)`, `PreserveNonMarkerFields`, `MarkRetAccOn`, and
`MarkRetRejOn` preserve `CurExhausted` as part of non-`Ret` frame fields.

`[open]` Realization alignment: the MDALT DTM realization
`δ_{M_D^alt}^{sch}/E(Rbar)` currently lacks `CurExhausted` in its frame
encoding and BackE/marker/child/init macro-steps (v2 audit §2, §10.1). This is
an explicit residual obligation, not a schema falsification.

## 5. `BackE_NI_NNI_Total_Complement` (rows 10,11,12 consumers)

`[definition/open lemma target]`
For every `I_{M_D}`-reachable state with `BackE_RetRej_Top(E,c̄)`:
```text
CursorCoherent(E,c̄) ∧ (NI_c(E) xor NNI_c(E))
∧ (NI_c(E) -> ∃!x∃y∃a∃b(CurNormalState(E,c̄,a,b) ∧ NextIncomp_c(E,a,b;x,y))).
```

### Proof/refutation status

**Verdict:** **definitional schema-level complement under `CursorCoherent` +
fixed total `Lex2`/coherent `Incomp_c`; open as an `I_{M_D}`-reachability
proof. Not refuted.**

`[deduction]` *Exhausted case.* `CursorCoherent` gives `CurExhaustedState(c̄)`
xor `CurNormalState(c̄)`. If `CurExhausted(c̄)`, then `NNI_c` holds by its
first disjunct and `NI_c` is false because `NI_c` requires `¬CurExhausted`.
So `NI_c xor NNI_c`.

`[deduction]` *Normal case.* If `¬CurExhausted(c̄)`, `CursorCoherent` gives
unique `CurNormalState(c̄,a,b)`. For fixed `(a,b)` and fixed `PosF(c̄)/Incomp_c`,
the candidate set
`Cand(a,b):={(x,y) | Lex2(a,b,x,y) ∧ Incomp_c(x,y)}` is a finite subset of
`Cell×Cell` under fixed strict total `Lex2`. Either `Cand(a,b)=∅`, giving
`NoNextIncomp_c(a,b)` and hence `NNI_c ∧ ¬NI_c`; or `Cand(a,b)≠∅`, giving a
unique `Lex2`-least witness `(x,y)=min_{Lex2} Cand(a,b)`, hence
`NI_c ∧ ¬NNI_c` with unique NI witness. Thus `NI_c xor NNI_c`.

`[deduction]` *Consumer consequence (rows 10,11,12).* Under this xor, a
reachable `BackE_RetRej_Top(c̄)` state is consumed by row 10 iff `NI_c`, and by
row 11/12 iff `NNI_c` with root/non-root determinacy (T-consume). The old
uninterpreted-sentinel gap is closed at schema level because exhaustion is the
explicit first `NNI_c` disjunct and is excluded from `NI_c`.

`[open]` *Residual obligations.* `I_{M_D}` must preserve `CursorCoherent` for
BackE-relevant frames (all 19 rows, especially 5,6,10,12, BackA parents,
marker returns, init/child constructors). `Lex2/Incomp_c/PosF` coherence and
no-spurious local alignment remain inherited/open. Eval/initial cursor
coherence (row 6/`RejectEval_c`) is the separate open obligation in v2 §7.2(3).
Realization `δ_{M_D^alt}^{sch}` still lacks `CurExhausted` in BackE rows and
must be realigned.

**Explicit falsifier witness shapes:**
```text
F-COMP-1 (partition failure):
  I_{M_D}(E) ∧ BackE_RetRej_Top(E,c̄) ∧ (NI_c(E)∧NNI_c(E) ∨ ¬NI_c(E)∧¬NNI_c(E)).
F-COMP-2 (coherence failure):
  I_{M_D}(E) ∧ BackE_RetRej_Top(E,c̄) ∧ ¬CursorCoherent(E,c̄)
  (both/neither CurExhausted,CurNormalState; missing/duplicated witnesses;
   exhausted encoding read as CurNormalState).
F-COMP-3 (non-unique NI witness):
  I_{M_D}(E) ∧ BackE_RetRej_Top(E,c̄) ∧ NI_c(E)
  with two distinct (x1,y1)!=(x2,y2) satisfying NextIncomp_c(a,b;·,·).
F-COMP-4 (realization sentinel regression):
  δ_{M_D^alt}^{sch}/consumer treats ordinary cursor value without CurExhausted
  as exhausted, or treats CurExhaustedState as NI-eligible.
```

No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable instance of F-COMP-1..4 was found.

## 6. `BackE_AfterSelected_NoReselect` (rows 6,10 advance; rows 10,11,12 consume)

`[definition/open lemma target]`
If `E --D_i,S={c̄,d̄}--> E1` is an `I_{M_D}`-reachable row 6 or row 10 step
selecting unique NI witness `(x,y)` and applying
`SetParentBackE_Advance(c̄,x,y)`, and `E1 ⊢* E2` reaches
`BackE_RetRej_Top(E2,c̄)` without another cursor-overwriting BackE advance on
`c̄`, then `¬NIWitness(E2,c̄,x,y)`, where
`NIWitness(E,c̄,x,y)` means `(x,y)` is the NI witness used by row 10 at
`c̄` in state `E`.

### Proof/refutation status

**Verdict:** **not refuted; trace-sufficient at schema level under explicit
`CurExhausted` exhausted-state encoding and preservation; open as an
`I_{M_D}`-reachability/closure proof.** This closes the old last-pair sentinel
reselect shape at schema level (refutation note §3) by making exhaustion a
consumer-visible state.

`[deduction]` *Non-last case, `¬IsLastLex2(x,y)` (rows 6,10).* Row 6/10
require `NI_c(E,c̄)`, hence `¬CurExhausted(E,c̄)` and unique
`CurNormalState(E,c̄,a,b)`. `SetParentBackE_Advance` stores unique
`CurNormalState'(c̄,x',y')` with `SuccLex2(x,y,x',y')` and
`¬CurExhausted'(c̄)`. By strict total `Lex2`, `Lex2(x,y,x',y')` and
`(x,y)!=(x',y')`. If `E1 ⊢* E2` preserves this cursor (obligation 4) and
`BackE_RetRej_Top(E2,c̄)` holds, then any NI witness from `(x',y')` satisfies
`Lex2(x',y',x*,y*)`, hence `Lex2(x,y,x*,y*)` and `(x*,y*)!=(x,y)`. Therefore
row 10 cannot NI-witness `(x,y)` again.

`[deduction]` *Last case, `IsLastLex2(x,y)` (rows 6,10).* Same entry gives
`NI_c(E,c̄)`. `SetParentBackE_Advance` sets `CurExhaustedState'(c̄)`:
`CurExhausted'(c̄)`, `HasCur'`, `CurA'=CurB'=MinKey`, `¬CurNormalState'(c̄)`.
If preserved to `E2`, then `NI_c(E2,c̄)` is false (requires `¬CurExhausted`)
and `NNI_c(E2,c̄)` is true, so only row 11/12 can consume
