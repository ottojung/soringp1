## 2. C1–C4 formats

### C1 — Explicit adversary strategy tree
- **Format description:** Explicit. Root antichain; internal nodes cover **every** legal sorter move with an adversary answer and child state; depth `<=29`. This matches the dual of a sorter strategy and is the natural game-theoretic refuter.
- **Completeness requirement:** Correctly requires covering all incomparable-pair sorter moves at visited states (universal sorter quantifier).
- **State representation:** Uses transitively closed partial orders — consistent with `G(n,k)`.
- **Assessment:** Sound as a format. The “not known short” rejection is appropriate; branching factor `Θ(n^2)` over depth 29 makes raw expansion large. No overclaim of shortness.

### C2 — Pruned search certificate / replayable refutation log
- **Format description:** Record sequence/log of completed refutation search; verifier replays rules. Appropriately abstract.
- **Key audit point:** Explicitly states raw exhaustive/pruned search output is **not** a short certificate unless accompanied by explicit compression claim and compressed-form verifier. This directly satisfies the requested check.
- **Pruning lemmas caveat:** Correctly notes lemmas must be omitted or recorded as checkable proof objects; otherwise soundness is not established by replay.
- **Assessment:** Good. No misrepresentation of raw search output.

### C3 — Node-level invariant-only certificate
- **Format description:** Scalar/local invariant `I(P,c)` refuting height 29. Presented as conjecture/candidate.
- **Obstruction:** Grounded in `research-n12-nontightness.md`: node-level information bound `e(P)<=2^(29-c)` necessary but not a short standalone global refuter explanation; efficiency equivalent to information bound; non-monotonicity in `e(P)`; same `e` different `m`; forced prefixes occur at tight `n`. These citations are consistent with the local doc.
- **Assessment:** Appropriately rejects C3 as *currently realized* without claiming impossibility in principle. Does not misrepresent search proof as an invariant proof.

### C4 — Adversary strategy DAG with explicit canonicalization (proposed)
- **Format description:** Concrete. DAG/refutation object with antichain root, budget, total refuter rule `ρ_v: Inc(P_v) -> A(P_v)` for every incomparable pair, edges to transitive closures with budget decrement, explicit sharing only under declared sound keys (default identical canonical state+budget). Dominance not assumed sound unless proof rule included — good caution.
- **Verifier:** Explicit finite procedure: parse/root/budget, acyclicity or budget decrease, totality over `Inc(P_v)`, recompute `tc(P_v+ρ_v(m))`, check sharing/dominance rules, accept if all paths covered within budget. Local poset operations are polynomial in `n`; total polynomial in `|D|` plus local costs. Reasonable.
- **Soundness:** Argues that covering every legal sorter move at reachable refuter states with certified answers within budget 29 means no height-29 sorter strategy wins. This is the right game-theoretic soundness claim for the stated format.
- **Shortness:** Explicitly marked `[open]`; **no** short instance, measured size, or compression bound for `n=12,k=29` exhibited. Raw full expansion not short. This is the right status.
- **Assessment:** Strongest concrete proposal in the artifact. Verifier/soundness are plausible at the stated level; the artifact avoids claiming C4 is a demonstrated short certificate.

## 6. Issues / recommendations (non-blocking)

1. **External artifact reference not independently verified:** §0 cites `/home/lubko/soring-fronts/s12-eso-certificate-lb-20261105/docs/eso-certificate-lb-obstruction.md` as context. This audit did not read it (not in required list). No claim in the audited artifact appears to depend on it except motivation; recommend noting external dependency explicitly if integrated.
2. **Canonicalization/dominance in C4:** Default “identical canonical state and identical budget” is safe. The note correctly withholds soundness for dominance unless a proof rule is included. Any future implementation should specify the canonical form (e.g., labelled TC matrix vs. isomorphism quotient) because quotienting can change what counts as “same state” and must preserve the universal-sorter refuter property.
3. **C1 leaf condition phrasing:** “leaves … either non-total with no remaining valid move representation (impossible while non-total)…” is slightly awkward but harmless; intent is clear (non-total states always have incomparable pairs until total or budget exhausted; certificate should make failure condition explicit).
4. **Size language:** C1 says “rejected as a known short certificate absent a compression proof” — appropriate. Could optionally add that even a reduced per-node representation must still cover all sorter moves, but this is implicit.
5. **Not Lean-checked / not a proof of `S(12)>29`:** Artifact states this; audit confirms it should remain a research note unless a checkable certificate object and verifier are actually produced and validated.
