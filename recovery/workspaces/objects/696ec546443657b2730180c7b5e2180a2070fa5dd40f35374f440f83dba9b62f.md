## Falsification and remaining obligations

No concrete `I_M_D`-reachable countertrace for the requested claims appears in the audited artifacts. The explicit falsifier schemas remain useful tests, but are not witnesses: in particular, an Eval parent receiving a row-4/5 marker would refute the receiver lemma; a live Eval+RetAcc/RetRej top would refute the no-Eval-marker claim; and mutation of `CurExhausted` or cursor fields at `p` would violate the exact marker contract. Thus the appropriate verdict is **supported / no explicit reachable witness**, not “proved” and not “falsified.”
