# Exact subexponential memoization: obstruction note

**Status:** experiment + deduction, not Lean-checked, no complexity-class
separation. Reproduction: `scripts/memoization_invariants.py` (stdlib only,
21.7 s on the execution host). The module-reversal cross-check uses the
`/home/lubko/soring301` scripts named at the end.

**Question (falsifiable).** Can the exact minimax value `F(P)` — with
`F(antichain_n) = S(n)` — be computed in time `2^{O(n)}`, better than the
known `2^{O(n log^2 n)}` budgeted DFS, by memoizing over a *nontrivial
canonical class of posets* (coarser than full isomorphism) or by pruning that
provably preserves exactness?

**Answer reported here.** No such class was found, and every tested cheap
invariant fails by `n <= 7`. The obstruction is stated precisely enough to be
falsifiable in both directions: a sound memo class must satisfy (A) exactness,
(B) successor compatibility, and (C) a sub-`2^{Theta(n^2)}` class count. All
natural candidates fail (A) or (C); even the strongest exact-through-`n=6`
candidate fails (A) at `n=7` with explicit witnesses.

## 1. Why the naive state space cannot be beaten by invariants alone

[deduction] Reachable states from the antichain are exactly the labelled
posets (`A001035(n)`), and the `S_n` isomorphism quotient is exact with
`A000112(n)` classes; both are `2^{Theta(n^2)}`. Quotienting by any subgroup
`H <= S_n` is exact (equivariance) and only changes the polynomial factor
`2^{Theta(n log n)}`. This is prior art in `research-state-compression.md`.

[deduction] A memoization over a class map `kappa` (poset -> label, classes
need not be isomorphism classes) is sound only if:

- **(A) Exactness.** `F` is constant on `kappa`-classes, so `G([P]) := F(P)`
  is well defined.
- **(B) Successor compatibility.** For `P` with label `kappa(P)`, the minimax
  recurrence value `1 + min_{x,y inc} max(F(P+x<y), F(P+y<x))` is determined
  by `kappa(P)`; equivalently the reachable successor data relevant to the
  min-max must be representative-independent. A sufficient form is that the
  multiset of pairs `(kappa(P+x<y), F(P+x<y))` over all incomparable pairs is
  a function of `kappa(P)`. Full isomorphism satisfies this by transport; the
  cheaper invariants below do not.
- **(C) Counting.** For `2^{O(n)}` time the number of reachable `kappa`-classes
  must be `2^{O(n)}` up to polynomial factors per class.

Conditions (A)+(B) are exactly what makes a class recurrence well defined;
they are a bisimulation-style requirement, strictly stronger than
"`F`-invariant". They are the real reason graph-derived invariants cannot be
DP states: extending by one comparison changes the graph, so (B) fails even
when (A) accidentally holds on a small range.

[deduction, prior art] The winning-frontier theorem
(`research-winning-frontier-barrier.md`) gives
`2^{(1/4-o(1)) n (log_2 n)^2}` pairwise nonisomorphic, reachable, *winning*
posets at the exact optimal budget in one rectangular-chain family. Hence any
`kappa` that is injective on that family inherits a `2^{Theta(n log^2 n)}`
class count; to beat `2^{O(n log^2 n)}` a sound `kappa` must merge distinct
nonisomorphic winning states. Those states differ exactly in which independent
adjacent-level edges are installed, and the two children of such a pair have
different `F`; that is where (B) bites. The theorem is an enumeration barrier
only; it does **not** rule out selective/parametric algorithms, which this note
does not address.

## 2. Tested invariants (experiment: all labelled posets, n <= 6)

`F` is computed exactly from the antichain recurrence on every labelled poset;
a "collision" is a class holding two posets with different `F`. Counts are
`collisions / classes`.

| invariant | fails first at | explicit witness |
|---|---|---|
| linear-extension count `e` alone | n=5 (prior art: `research-state-compression.md`, `research-obstruction-next.md`) | two `e=8` posets with `F=3,4` |
| `(e, width)` | n=6 (prior art) | `(8,2)` with `F in {3,4}` |
| Dushnik–Miller dimension | **n=3** | dimension is `2` on *every* non-total poset for `n<=5` (smallest dimension-3 poset is the 6-element standard example), while `F` spans `1..7` at `n=5` |
| `(height, width)` | **n=4** | key `(2,2)`: `F in {2,3}` |
| `(height, width, #min, #max)` | **n=4** | 3 colliding keys at `n=4` |
| `(height, width, comparability-degree sequence)` | **n=6** | key `(3,2,(3,3,3,3,4,4))`, `F in {4,5}` |
| `(height, width, degree sequence, e)` | **n=7** | 4 collisions among the 2045 iso classes, e.g. key `(4,3,(3,3,4,4,4,5,5),30)` with `F in {5,6}` |
| comparability graph up to isomorphism | n=7 (prior art: `research-compression-next.md`) | same graph, `F` values differ |
| module-reversal quotient (iso x reversal of every Gallai module) | **n=7** | 8 `F`-violations among 22494 reversals (see §4) |

## 3. Class-count growth: still `2^{Theta(n^2)}`, never `2^{O(n)}`

[experiment] Even the continuously-exact-through-`n=6` invariant has

| n | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `(h,w,deg,e)` classes | 11 | 33 | 144 | 811 |
| `log_2 classes` (approx) | 3.5 | 5.0 | 7.2 | 9.7 |
| increment of `log_2` | | 1.5 | 2.2 | 2.5 |
| iso `A000112(n)` | 16 | 63 | 318 | 2045 |

The increments of `log_2` grow roughly linearly, i.e. `log_2 #classes =
Theta(n^2)`; the invariant is only about a factor `2.2` coarser than
isomorphism and asymptotically in the same `2^{Theta(n^2)}` regime. So even if
it had stayed exact, it could not give `2^{O(n)}` memoization; it fails (C).
The same applies to iso+dual (`12, 39, 184` classes at `n=4,5,6`) and to the
iso quotient itself.

## 4. The strongest *structurally motivated* quotient also fails

[experiment, cross-check] Reversing every Gallai/autonomous module is a
canonical operation preserving `e` and `F` on small posets. The quotient
"iso x module reversal" is exact up to `n=6` (classes `11, 33, 144` at
`n=4,5,6`) and coarser than iso+dual.

At `n=7` it is **unsound**. Running the repository check

```
cd /home/lubko/soring301
python3 -c "from scripts.orientation_modules_probe import verify_invariance; \
            print(verify_invariance(7, check_e=False))"
```

gives `2045` classes, `22494` nontrivial module reversals, `0` cyclic flips,
and **8** `F`-violations. Examples (`P`, module `S` as bitmask, `F` before ->
after reversal):

```
(0,0,2,1,1,9,59)  S={0,3,4,5}  F 7 -> 6
(0,0,2,1,1,1,59)  S={0,3,4,5}  F 8 -> 7
(0,0,1,3,5,5,37)  S={2,4,5,6}  F 6 -> 7
(0,0,1,3,5,5,5)   S={2,4,5,6}  F 7 -> 8
```

This matches the repo claim "MDT up to reversal is a sound canonical form for
`F` — false at `n=7`". The quotient has `824` classes at `n=7` (vs `2045`
iso): coarser, but unsound, so it fails (A).

## 5. The pruning lane does not supply `2^{O(n)}`

[deduction + prior art] The exactness-preserving prunings already in the repo
are the extension-count cutoff `e(P) > 2^d => not sortable in d`, the
Hanamura–Iwata base `e(P) <= 7 => F(P) <= 3`, automorphism move orbits, and
ordinal-sum additivity `F(A (+B)) = F(A)+F(B)`. Each is sound and locally
useful, but none reduces the worst-case frontier width: the winning-frontier
theorem exhibits `2^{Theta(n log^2 n)}` nonisomorphic winning states at the
optimum, and `research-obstruction-next.md` falsifies every natural
low-dimensional scalar extension of the small-budget base (`e`, `(e,width)`,
`(e,width,#min,#max)`, merge class) at `n<=6`. The result of this note is the
next step in that chain: even a *joint* structural + information invariant
(`height`, `width`, comparability-degree sequence, `e`) survives to `n=6` and
then collapses at `n=7`. Pruning therefore buys constants and small-`n`
reach, not an exponent.

## 6. Verdict

- **No construction.** Every tested `kappa` either fails exactness (A) by
  `n<=7`, or is exact only on a `2^{Theta(n^2)}`-class family (iso, iso+dual,
  and `(h,w,deg,e)` through its valid range). No `2^{O(n)}` invariant
  memoization was found.
- **Partial no-go (not a theorem about all algorithms).** Any memo label that
  factors through `(height, width, comparability-degree sequence, e)` — in
  particular through the comparability graph and `e` — cannot be exact for
  `n >= 7` (explicit witnesses). Since the comparability graph alone already
  fails at `n=7` and its one-edge transition is ill-defined at `n=5`, graph
  invariants cannot serve as DP states at all.
- **Where any future construction must work.** It needs `kappa` with (A)
  exactness, (B) successor compatibility (a bisimulation-type condition, not
  merely `F`-invariance), and (C) at most `2^{O(n)}` reachable classes. All
  candidates above die at (A) or (C); (B) is what rules out graph-derived
  labels structurally.
- **Not addressed.** Selective minimax / proof-number / branch-and-bound
  algorithms that do not enumerate or quotient the winning frontier. The
  winning-frontier theorem explicitly leaves those open.
