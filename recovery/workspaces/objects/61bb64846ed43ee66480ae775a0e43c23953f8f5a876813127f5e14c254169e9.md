# Exec report: quantified-root-adversary-proof

## Question addressed

Construct a sound, polynomial-time checkable adversary invariant that forces a comparison obstruction from the initial `n`-element antichain under **every** sorting strategy, not only from an arbitrary reachable partial order.

- **Implication (from frontier/problem statement).** `[definition]` A verified all-`n` antichain lower-bound theorem can improve numerical bounds on `S(n)`, but would not alone separate unary computation complexity classes.
- **Model.** `[definition]` `S(n)` is the minimax comparison height from the antichain `A_n` on `n` labelled distinct keys; `F(P)` is the minimax remaining height from poset state `P`; `e(P)` is the number of linear extensions; `L0(P)=ceil(log2 e(P))` is the information bound; `S(n)=F(A_n) >= C(n)=ceil(log2 n!)` (`docs/problem.md`, `docs/research-one-ply-obstruction.md`).

## Invariant/candidates examined

### Candidate 0 (baseline): global information invariant

- **Invariant/check.** `[literature]/[deduction]` `I_info(n,k) :≡ (C(n) > k)` where `C(n)=ceil(log2 n!)`.
- **Meaning.** For any strategy of height `k`, `n!` outcomes cannot be distinguished by a binary tree of depth `< C(n)`; equivalently the adversary can always answer consistently with at least one extension in any remaining leaf-count-large partition (standard leaf argument).
- **Checkability.** `[deduction]` Polynomial-time checkable in the bit-length of `n,k` (compute/compare Stirling-accurate or direct `log2 n!` bound). Also trivial at unary input scale.
- **Root quantification.** `[deduction]` Holds against **every** sorting strategy from `A_n` (it is a global tree/counting obstruction, not a reachable-gadget obstruction).
- **Assessment.** Sound but baseline; does not improve on the known information lower bound `S(n) >= C(n)`.

### Candidate 1: one-ply adversarial information invariant `L1(P) > d`

- **Definition (local).** `[literature/deduction, repo]` From `docs/research-one-ply-obstruction.md`:
  - `L1(P)=0` if total, else `L1(P)=1 + min_{i,j incomparable} max(L0(P+i<j), L0(P+j<i))`.
  - Theorems in note: `L0(P) <= L1(P) <= F(P)` for all `P`.
- **As a root-forcing invariant (attempted lift).** Hypothesis: maintain `L1(P_t) >= d-t` along some adversary response path under every sorter, starting at `A_n`.
- **Soundness if maintainable.** `[deduction]` Yes: by `L1 <= F`, an invariant forcing `L1(P_t)>d-t` would imply `F(P_t)>d-t` and hence obstruction at budget `d`.
- **Checkability.** `[deduction]` **Not polynomial-time in `n` in general** for exact `L1`, because it depends on `e` of child states (`#P`-hard in general). The note gives a safe budgeted capped-enumeration test with cost `O(2^d n^2)` per node (polynomial space) — polynomial in `(n,2^d)` but exponential in `d` / potentially exponential in input measures relevant to large budgets; therefore it does **not** qualify as an unconditional polynomial-time checkable invariant in `n`.
- **Root falsification (critical).** `[deduction]` At the antichain `A_n`, every comparison `{i,j}` splits linear extensions into exactly equal halves by permutation symmetry of the `n!` total orders: `e(A_n+i<j)=e(A_n+j<i)=n!/2`. Hence for every pair
  - `max(L0(child_L), L0(child_R)) = ceil(log2(n!/2)) = ceil(log2 n!) - 1` (for `n>=2`),
  - so `L1(A_n)=1 + ceil(log2 n!) - 1 = L0(A_n)=C(n)`.
  Thus `L1` provides **no strict obstruction at the root** and cannot, by itself, be a root-strengthening invariant. Moreover any first-step adversary answer leaves a child with `L0 = C(n)-1` equal for all sorter moves; the invariant does not force entry into a strict `L1>L0` gadget (e.g. witness `W`, `e=8, L1=4>L0=3`) because the sorter controls which comparison/state-transition graph is followed and can avoid presenting a pair that becomes the specific imbalanced local structure unless that structure is unavoidable.
- **Status.** Sound locally; **fails as a root-forcing strengthening** (root value not strict; global maintainability under every sorter not established and symmetry shows no first-ply gain).

### Candidate 2: maintainable "no balanced split exists" invariant

- **Invariant.** `B(P,d):≡` every incomparable pair of `P` has some outcome with `> 2^{d-1}` linear extensions (equivalently budgeted `L1(P)>d` via capped counts).
- **Checkability.** `[deduction]` As in Candidate 1, exact/certified checking is not known polynomial-time in `n` generally; budget-capped form is `O(2^d poly(n))`.
- **Root analysis.** `[deduction]` `B(A_n, C(n)-1)` is **false** for `n>=2` because every pair splits `n!/2, n!/2` (balanced at the information threshold). Therefore this invariant cannot be initialized at the root to beat `C(n)`.
- **Falsifier shape.** `[deduction]` A uniform sorter need not "defeat" it after initialization because it is not satisfiable at `A_n` above the baseline; any proposed local forcing claim of this form is refuted at the root by symmetry.

### Candidate 3: forced reachability of known hard reachable gadgets (structural/merge witnesses)

- **Examples.** `[literature/experiment, repo]` `docs/research-structural-lower-bounds.md` gives strict local strengthening witnesses such as `Merge(2,4)` (`n=6`, `F=5>info=4`) and block-local ceiling gains; `docs/research-obstruction-next.md` notes same-`e` witnesses with different `F`.
- **Root-forcing attempt.** Argue adversary can force a path from `A_n` to some `P` with `F(P)+t > C(n)` under every strategy.
- **Assessment.** `[deduction]` **Not established.** These are properties of *arbitrary/reachable* states (existential in the state), but the root quantifier requires that for **every** sorter move there is an adversary response keeping the invariant. The sorter chooses comparisons and may steer away from any particular certified gadget. No polynomial-time checkable invariant was found that (a) holds at `A_n`, (b) is preserved by some adversary reply to every sorter comparison, and (c) implies `F(P)` exceeding `C(n)` by a forced amount. This is precisely the gap highlighted by the frontier/issue context.

## Soundness checks

- `[deduction]` Any invariant `I(P,d)` with: (i) `I(A_n,d)` checkable in polynomial time, (ii) for all `P` with `I(P,d)` and all incomparable pairs `q`, `I(P+q^+) \lor I(P+q^-)` (adversary can maintain) and children remain consistent with budget arithmetic, and (iii) `I(P,d) => F(P) > d'` for appropriate `d'`, would be a **sound** root adversary obstruction.
- `[deduction]` Candidates 1-3 as stated do not simultaneously satisfy (i)-(iii): Candidate 0 satisfies a global form of soundness but not strengthening; Candidate 1 fails root strengthening/maintainability evidence and general poly-time exact checkability; Candidate 2 is unsatisfiable at root above baseline; Candidate 3 lacks a universal maintainability proof from `A_n`.

## Falsification attempts

1. **Antichain symmetry test (naive local lift).** `[deduction]` Falsifies root strictness of `L1` and any "force imbalance on first comparison" claim: all first outcomes give equal `e=n!/2`.
2. **Reachable-gadget-to-root transfer.** `[deduction]` Identified as non sequitur without an adversary strategy: existence of hard `P` reachable by some path does not imply forceability under every strategy. No counterexample strategy family constructed here, but the required implication is missing (inconclusive on specific gadgets, negative on transfer as a proof method).
3. **Poly-time checkability stress.** `[deduction]` Exact extension-count predicates (`L0/L1`, merge-factor certification beyond fixed tables) are not polynomial-time in `n` in general; budget-capped variants trade time for `2^d`, so they are not the requested unconditional polynomial-time root invariant.

## Findings

- **Positive (sound, root-valid, but trivial).** `[deduction/literature]` The information bound `C(n)=ceil(log2 n!)` is a sound obstruction against every sorting strategy from `A_n` and is efficiently checkable; it is the baseline root adversary argument.
- **Negative (for examined strengthening candidates).** `[deduction]` 
  - Local one-ply/structural gadgets (`L1>L0`, merge gaps, same-`e` value splits) are sound for **given/reachable** states but do not by themselves yield a polynomial-time checkable invariant that is **initialized and maintainable from `A_n` under every sorter strategy**.
  - At `A_n`, pairwise extension splits are perfectly balanced, so one-ply imbalance cannot be forced at the root.
- **Inconclusive.** Existence of *any* non-baseline, polynomial-time checkable, root-maintainable adversary invariant strengthening `C(n)` for all `n` (or infinitely many `n`) remains open. No such invariant was constructed; no general impossibility proof was found.

## Obstacles

1. **Root quantifier gap.** Must exhibit an adversary response function for *every* sorter comparison, not merely a hard state on *some* branch.
2. **Symmetry at the start.** The antichain is maximally symmetric; early comparisons are balanced in extension count, starving simple information-imbalance invariants.
3. **Checkability vs power.** Invariants strong enough to capture nontrivial local structure often depend on `e(P)`/`F(P)` features that are hard to compute exactly; capped checks are exponential in budget.
4. **No transfer theorem.** Known reachable-state obstructions (including `n=12` non-tightness evidence in literature) are not packaged as a strategy-invariant from the root.

## Next decisive question

Can one define a **polynomial-time computable** state predicate `J(P)` on transitive-closure representations (e.g. from comparability graph parameters, width/chain-antichain certificates, or degree/imbalance summaries not requiring `#P` extension counting) together with a proved adversary reply rule such that `J(A_n)` holds for all relevant `n` and `J` implies `ceil(log2 e(P))` or another certified deficit propagates to force `> C(n)` comparisons on some infinite family (or explain the `n=12` gap)? 

A decisive falsifier would be: for a proposed `J`, construct (even for small `n` or a schematic family) a concrete sorter strategy `σ` such that every adversary reply to `σ` reaches `¬J` before any super-information deficit is forced.
