  counterexample to the tail majorant would need a denominator below the
  chosen cutoff; the artifact’s conditional form avoids this by requiring
  `H` and an explicit cutoff/prefix treatment, though the loose-majorant
  absorption should be made explicit.
- The artifact’s “minimal next falsifiable step” (l.142) is appropriate:
  line-by-line Lemma 2 extraction + one concrete Lemma 4 schedule slice
  with certified `Δ` terms and a finite-basis Thm 3 one-step check. A slice
  failure would refute that effectivization route; a slice success would
  discharge a finite fragment.
- I performed no external computation and no primary PDF re-extraction;
  conclusions here are from repository artifacts and the supplied note.
