## 2. Adversarial finding: sentinel not consulted by NI/NNI consumers

`[deduction]` **Consumer gap.** Rows 10–12 consume BackE+RetRej using only
`NI_c` / `NNI_c`. Both are defined solely from the stored pair
`(CurA(c̄,a),CurB(c̄,b))` and `NextIncomp_c`/`NoNextIncomp_c` over `Lex2` and
`Incomp_c`. Neither definition mentions `SentinelLast`, `BackE_NoFurther`,
`IsLastLex2`, or any cursor-exhausted state. Therefore the normalized text's
intent "equivalent to forcing `NoNextIncomp_c` for the stored cursor" is not
enforced by the consumer formulas: it is an external semantic constraint on
how `SentinelLast`/the stored cursor must be realized.

`[deduction]` **Producer gap.** `SetParentBackE_Advance` stores `CurA'/CurB'`
satisfying `AfterSelected(x,y,x',y')`. In the `IsLastLex2(x,y)` branch this
requires `BackE_NoFurther(x',y') := SentinelLast(x',y')`. But the stored
cursor is still an ordinary `(CurA,CurB)` pair in `Cell×Cell`; `SentinelLast`
is not represented in `Fld`. Thus the schema does not supply a concrete
`(x',y')` encoding/interpretation that makes consumers read the state as NNI.

## 3. Explicit transition-trace witness shape (rows 6/10, pop 5/12, consume 10–12)

The trace is given as an explicit `I_M_D`-relevant state/transition template.
Concrete reachability of the exact `PosF/Incomp` pattern is `[inherited
assumption/open]` under the DFS discipline; the falsifier is schema-level
given that a BackE frame can select an NI witness whose selected pair is the
global `Lex2`-last element of `Cell×Cell` (or whose after-selected sentinel is
not NNI-forcing).

### 3.1 Common setup

`[definition]` Let `L = (L1,L2)` be the maximum element of `Cell×Cell` under
strict total `Lex2`. Let `p̄` be a BackE frame live on a reachable
non-halting state, with `M_BackE(p̄)`, `CtrlSort(p̄)`, `RetNone(p̄)`,
`HasCur(p̄)`, current cursor `C0=(a0,b0)`, `PosF` snapshot `Π_p` making
`Incomp_p` well-defined, and `¬Total_p ∧ Budget_p`.

Two selection entry points feed the same last-pair locus:

- **Via row 6** (`D_sort_pick`): state `E6` has
  `Top(E6,p̄)∧M_Eval(E6,p̄)∧CtrlSort(E6,p̄)∧RetNone(E6,p̄)∧¬Total_p∧Budget_p`
  and `NextIncomp_p(a0,b0; L1,L2)` (unique NI witness from `C0` is `L`).
- **Via row 10** (`D_backE_rej_next`): state `E10` has
  `Top(E10,p̄)∧M_BackE(E10,p̄)∧RetRej(E10,p̄)` and
  `NextIncomp_p(a0,b0; L1,L2)`.

In both cases `IsLastLex2(L1,L2)` holds by definition of `L`.

### 3.2 Selection step and under-specified cursor

`[falsification-shape]` **Trace T-sel (last-pair NI selection):**
```text
E  = E6 or E10,   E ⊨ I_{M_D} (assumed reachable),   Top(E,p̄).
E ⊨ NI_p with unique witness (x,y)=L=(L1,L2),   IsLastLex2(L).

E --row 6 or row 10, S={p̄,d̄}--> E1:
  Globals_push(p̄,d̄); PushChildE(d̄,p̄,L1,L2); Parent'(d̄,p̄).
  SetParentBackE_Advance(p̄,L1,L2) requires
      ∃x'∃y'(AfterSelected(L1,L2,x',y') ∧ CurA'(p̄,x') ∧ CurB'(p̄,y')).
  Since IsLastLex2(L): AfterSelected(L1,L2,x',y') = SentinelLast(x',y').
```

Schema tension: `Cell×Cell` is finite and totally ordered by `Lex2`, so no
`SuccLex2(L,·)` exists (correct). But `SentinelLast(x',y')` is only described
as "equivalent to forcing NoNextIncomp_c for the stored cursor" and is not a
component of `Fld`; consumers do not test it. Therefore `E1`'s stored cursor
`C1=(x',y')` is not fully determined by the normalized schema:
- no successor-pair instantiation exists;
- "store `L` itself" gives `CurA'(p̄,L1),CurB'(p̄,L2)`: this would make
  `NoNextIncomp_p(L1,L2)` definitionally true because no `Lex2(L,·)` exists,
  and would prevent NI-reselection of `L` (strict `Lex2(L,L)` false), but it
  is **not** `AfterSelected(L,L,x',y')` as written and is not stated by the
  schema;
- "store an ordinary non-last pair" or leave `SentinelLast` uninterpreted does
  not force `NNI_p` at consumers.

### 3.3 Child execution and reject-producing pop (rows 5,12)

`[inherited assumption/open]` The pushed child `d̄` explores the
`PushChildE(d̄,p̄,L1,L2)` branch. A reject outcome can reach `p̄` by either:

- **Row 5 path (`D_eval_rej_ret`)**: some descendant/eval frame `c̄` becomes
  `Top(c̄)∧M_Eval(c̄)∧NonRoot(c̄)∧RejectEval_c∧Parent(c̄,p̄)` (directly, or
  `p̄` receives reject from its immediate child after intermediate pops;
  intermediate pops preserve lower frames except their changed sets).
- **Row 12 path (`D_backE_rej_exh_ret`)**: a BackE descendant `c̄` reaches
  `Top(c̄)∧M_BackE(c̄)∧RetRej(c̄)∧NNI_c∧NonRoot(c̄)∧Parent(c̄,p̄)` and pops
  reject to `p̄`.

`[deduction]` **Trace T-pop5 (row 5 producer):**
```text
E1 ⊢* E4  (child/descendant work; p̄ not in changed set except as permitted
            by push/pop discipline; cursor/PosF/UsedF fields of p̄ intended
            preserved — obligation 1).
E4 ⊨ Top(c̄)∧M_Eval(c̄)∧NonRoot(c̄)∧RejectEval_c∧Parent(c̄,p̄)∧M_BackE(p̄).

E4 --row 5, S={p̄,c̄}--> E5:
  Globals_pop(p̄,c̄); FrameInactive'(c̄); MarkRetRejOn(p̄).
  E5 ⊨ Top(p̄)∧FrameLive(p̄)∧M_BackE(p̄)∧RetRej(p̄)
       ∧ CurA(p̄)=C1.CurA ∧ CurB(p̄)=C1.CurB
       ∧ HasCur(p̄),PosF(p̄),UsedF(p̄),... preserved from E1/E4.
```

`[deduction]` **Trace T-pop12 (row 12 producer):**
```text
E1 ⊢* E4' with
E4' ⊨ Top(c̄)∧M_BackE(c̄)∧RetRej(c̄)∧NNI_c∧NonRoot(c̄)∧Parent(c̄,p̄)∧M_BackE(p̄).

E4' --row 12, S={p̄,c̄}--> E5':
  Globals_pop(p̄,c̄); FrameInactive'(c̄); MarkRetRejOn(p̄).
  E5' ⊨ Top(p̄)∧FrameLive(p̄)∧M_BackE(p̄)∧RetRej(p̄) with p̄ cursor C1 preserved.
```

The consumed `NNI_c` is on child `c̄`, not on `p̄`; row 12 does not establish
`NI_p`/`NNI_p`.

### 3.4 Consumer interpretation (rows 10–12) — falsifier locus

`[falsification-shape]` **Trace T-consume (sentinel misread):** In `E5` or
`E5'`, `p̄` has `BackE_RetRej_Top(p̄)` and stored cursor `C1` produced by the
`IsLastLex2(L)` branch of `SetParentBackE_Advance`.

Consumers evaluate:
```text
NI_p  := ∃a∃b(CurA(p̄,a)∧CurB(p̄,b)∧∃x∃y NextIncomp_p(a,b;x,y)).
NNI_p := ∃a∃b(CurA(p̄,a)∧CurB(p̄,b)∧NoNextIncomp_p(a,b)).
```

**Bad realization R-bad (under-instantiated sentinel).** If `C1` is any
ordinary pair `(x',y')` such that `SentinelLast(x',y')` is asserted by the
realization but `NoNextIncomp_p(x',y')` is false under `Π_p` (e.g. because
`Π_p` changed or because the sentinel value is not actually after all
incomparables), then `NI_p(E5)` can hold and row 10 can fire:
```text
E5 --row 10--> E6' selects NI witness from C1, possibly an already-tried pair
                  (if C1 is before L or equals L under a bad encoding)
                  or a spurious pair inconsistent with exhaustion.
```
This breaks `BackE_AfterSelected_NoReselect` and/or BackE progress. It also
breaks the intended NI/NNI complement behavior relative to the selected
last-pair state.

**Bad realization R-undef (no concrete sentinel encoding).** If
`AfterSelected(L,L,x',y')` has no realizable `(x',y')` in the `CurA/CurB`
encoding, `SetParentBackE_Advance` is not total on this reachable guard and
DET-SUCC closure fails at the update-definition locus (not at rows 10–12).

**Intended realization R-intended (not supplied by schema).** A realization
must make `C1` force `NNI_p`. One sufficient encoding is to store `L` as
cursor (`C1=L`): then `NoNextIncomp_p(L)` is vacuously true, NI cannot
reselect `L` by strict `Lex2`. Another is an explicit exhausted bit
(§4). Neither is present as a named, consumer-consulted component in the
normalized schema.

## 4. Minimal `Normal|Exhausted` correction (exact witness-shape requirement)

`[deduction]` The minimal correction required by this falsification shape is
to make cursor exhaustion an explicit state consulted by both producers and
consumers, not an uninterpreted `SentinelLast` predicate on an ordinary
`CurA/CurB` pair.

```text
CursorState(c̄) := Normal(a,b) | Exhausted.
  Add CurExhausted(c̄) to Fld(c̄) (or a reserved distinguished encoding)
  with invariant: CurExhausted(c̄) xor (HasCur(c̄)∧∃!a∃!b CurNormal(c̄,a,b)).

CurNormal(c̄,a,b) := HasCur(c̄)∧CurA(c̄,a)∧CurB(c̄,b)∧¬CurExhausted(c̄).

SetParentBackE_Advance'(c̄,x,y) stores:
  (¬IsLastLex2(x,y) ∧ ∃x'∃y'(SuccLex2(x,y,x',y')∧CurNormal'(c̄,x',y')∧¬CurExhausted'(c̄)))
∨ ( IsLastLex2(x,y) ∧ CurExhausted'(c̄)∧¬∃a∃b CurNormal'(c̄,a,b)).

NI_c  := ∃a∃b(CurNormal(c̄,a,b)∧∃x∃y NextIncomp_c(a,b;x,y)).
NNI_c := CurExhausted(c̄) ∨ ∃a∃b(CurNormal(c̄,a,b)∧NoNextIncomp_c(a,b)).

Rows 5,12 MarkRetRejOn must preserve CurExhausted/CurNormal fields of p̄
(already implied if they are in preserved cursor/non-marker fields, but the
schema must list them explicitly).
```

`[open]` This correction is schema-level and necessary for the identified
last-pair trace; it is not proved sufficient for full `I_{M_D}`-deterministic
closure. It must be checked against `Enc_Component_Closure`, `I_{M_D}`
preservation, unique cursor/exhausted invariant, `ExactlyOneMode`/`Ret`
coherence, and all row reads/writes of BackE cursor fields.

## 6. Final verdict

`[deduction]` **No explicit `I_{M_D}`-reachable falsifying transition trace
was constructed from audited concrete reachable states.** However, the
normalized `Enc` is **under-instantiated and sentinel-ambiguous** at the
`IsLastLex2/BackE_NoFurther` locus: `SetParentBackE_Advance` can require a
sentinel cursor state while `NI_c/NNI_c` in rows 10–12 consult only ordinary
`CurA/CurB` and `Lex2/PosF`, and `Rbar/Fld` contains no `CurExhausted`
component. The explicit witness shape above (row 6 or 10 selects last-Lex2
NI witness `L`; child rejects; row 5 or 12 pops `MarkRetRejOn` to `p̄`
preserving the after-selected cursor; rows 10–12 consume `BackE_RetRej_Top`)
shows that a minimal `Normal|Exhausted` cursor-state correction is required
for a schema-level no-reselect/NI–NNI claim.

This is **not** a DET-SUCC discharge and makes **no** FO(PFP)/IFP, `P`,
`FO(LFP)` on the bare ordered word, `NP`, `coNP`, binary `SORT-BOUND-B`,
separation, or collapse claim.
