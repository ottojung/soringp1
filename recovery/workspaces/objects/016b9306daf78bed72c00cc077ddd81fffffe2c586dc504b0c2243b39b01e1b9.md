# Framed DAG witness attempt for `n=11`, budget `S(11)=26`

## 1. Problem statement and `S(11)` baseline

**Task/front:** `framed-dag-witness-n11-exact` (SoringP1 #301). Determine whether there exists an explicit, checkable permutation-framed strategy DAG certificate of exact height `S(11)=26` for `n=11`, and compare its concrete size/depth/profile to polynomial envelopes (e.g. `O(n^2)` nodes, `O(n log n)` path length).

**Model/certificate grammar (from `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`, `scripts/check_framed_strategy.py`):** a topologically numbered DAG (`root=0`) of comparison nodes with outgoing `lt`/`gt` edges carrying slot permutations, feasible-branch propagation of guaranteed strict partial orders, intersection at joins, total order at leaves, and longest path `<= budget`. The checker is a soundness checker for explicit certificates; polynomial-size completeness for optimal strategies is open.

**`S(11)` baseline (literature / arithmetic facts used):**
- Information-theoretic lower bound: `ceil(log2(11!)) = ceil(log2(39,916,800)) = ceil(25.2504927...) = 26`.
- Ford–Johnson (merge-insertion) upper bound `F_FJ(n)=sum_{j=1}^{n} ceil(log2(3j/4))` gives `F_FJ(11)=26` (computed locally; see Methods). Hence the information bound is tight at `n=11`.
- Consistent with the literature baseline in `docs/literature.md`: Peczarski (2012) reports equality of optimal comparison counts with the information bound through `n=11` (primary source not re-audited here; treated as **literature/index context**, not a new proof in this report). The repository's own `Known S(n)` list in `scripts/strategy_dag_probe.py` includes `..., 8:16, 12:30` but omits `11`; combined with `ceil(log2(11!))=26` and the cited equality-through-11 statement, the working baseline for this task is **`S(11)=26`**.

**No claim of completion:** this report does not prove a new complexity theorem and does not establish NP membership or any separation.

### 3.3 Structural/obstruction observations (evidence-backed, partly conjectural where noted)
1. **Budget tightness but witness gap:** `F_FJ(11)=ceil(log2(11!))=26` means a comparison-optimal strategy exists at the information-theoretic floor for `n=11` (**arithmetic/literature-consistent fact**). However, an *explicit framed DAG certificate* requires a fully specified topological DAG with locally checkable comparisons, permutations, and join/intersection annotations over all feasible branches; existence of a 26-comparison strategy does not by itself yield that explicit certificate object.
2. **Ford–Johnson framing is plausible but unaudited here:** Frontier doc §3 gives a plausible all-`n` polynomial framed certificate for the standard Ford–Johnson *upper bound* via rank-envelope joins/pending insertions, "subject to checking the complete recursive frame construction and odd case." Since `F_FJ(11)=S(11)`, a correct lifted FJ framed DAG would be a candidate exact-height witness for `n=11`; **no such complete recursive frame construction was implemented/checked in this run** (blocker: detailed permutation/anchor/boundary bookkeeping across recursive winner sorting and Jacobsthal insertion stages).
3. **Representation/scale barrier for naive enumeration:** `strategy_dag_probe.py` (poset-successor-bitmask minimax DAG, not the same as framed certificates) shows rapid growth already for `n<=5`:
   - `n=4`: unshared tree (all optimal) 1957, DAG(all) 181, quotient(all) 12
   - `n=5`: unshared tree (all optimal) 26541, DAG(all) 1551, quotient(all) 19
   (see `probe_max5.txt`). Direct minimax/DAG enumeration to `n=11` is infeasible with this approach; conversely, a *succinct framed* certificate (if one exists) would have to exploit structure/joining far beyond naive state enumeration. This is **evidence of search/representation difficulty**, not a proof that no polynomial framed witness exists.
4. **Join/annotation blocker (general, from frontier doc):** correct comparator outcomes cannot be joined immediately in general without potentially discarding disjunctive correlations (frontier §1). Any proposed exact-height-26 framed DAG for `n=11` must therefore justify join soundness/feasible-branch handling under the checker’s intersection semantics.
