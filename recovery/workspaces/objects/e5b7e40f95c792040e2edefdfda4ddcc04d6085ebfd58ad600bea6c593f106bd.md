## 1. Question (falsifiable front)

> Give a uniform parametric construction `n ↦ (P_n,h_n)` for all `n>=5` or an infinite subsequence with proof that `d_n+F(P_n)=S(n)` while `R(P_n)>=S(n)+1` (explicit forced sibling term), or show such uniform construction fails under natural gadget embeddings.

**Definitions (definition).** For a transcript/history `h` of depth `d=d(h)` from the antichain root to state `P`, with sibling (unchosen adversary outcome) states `Q_{i,h}^{sib}` at steps `i=1..d`,
- `R_h(P)=max(d+F(P), \max_{1<=i<=d} (i+F(Q_{i,h}^{sib})))` (root-prefix reachability/cost functional along `h`).
- `R(P)=\min_{h: root -> P} R_h(P)`.

A prefix on an optimal height-`S(n)` tree requires `R_h(P) <= S(n)` at every node on it (deduction: at step `i-1` the chosen comparison must satisfy `max(F(child),F(sibling)) <= S(n)-i`, hence `i+F(sibling) <= S(n)` and the tail must satisfy `d+F(P) <= S(n)`), so `R_h(P)>S(n)` witnesses that `h` is not a prefix of any root-optimal tree, and `R(P)>S(n)` witnesses that `P` is unreachable on every root-optimal tree. The local remainder `d+F(P)` is necessary but not sufficient when any sibling term exceeds the budget.

## 2. Explicit parametric construction attempt (natural gadget embedding)

**Gadget:** embed a fixed 3-element chain obstruction into `n>=5` elements by adding `n-3` isolated elements (a parallel/disjoint-union style extension on the unused elements, not an ordinal sum). Fix elements `0,1,2 ∈ [n]`.

**Construction `C_n` (`n>=5`).**
- `h_n`: `c_1=(2,0)` with chosen outcome `2<0` (state `A_n={2<0}`), then `c_2=(0,1)` with chosen outcome `0<1`.
- `P_n = A_n + (0<1)` whose transitive closure is the chain `2<0<1` on `{0,1,2}` with `{3,…,n-1}` isolated: `P_n = {2<0, 0<1, 2<1}` and no other relations.
- Sibling at step 1 (opposite of `2<0` on the antichain, up to isomorphism): `Q_{1,n}={0<2}`.
- Sibling at step 2 (opposite of `0<1` in `A_n`): `Q_{2,n} = {2<0, 1<0}` (element `0` above both `1,2`; others isolated).

This is uniform in `n` (same two comparisons on the same labelled triple, independent of the extra `n-3` elements). Any history reaching `P_n` must realize outcomes `2<0` and `0<1` on pairs `(2,0),(0,1)` (deduction: `P_n` contains `2<0` and `0<1`, and no other comparison on `{0,1,2}` can create them without contradicting `P_n`, since `0<2,1<0,1<2,2<1`-as-direct-orientation-choices except the implied `2<1` are absent/forced; in particular alternatives like `(2,1),(0,1)` leave `2‖0`), so shortest histories to `P_n` have depth 2 and are exactly `{(2,0),(0,1)}` in some order.

(For `n=7`, `F(P_7)=10, F(Q_{2,7})=11` computed directly from those states; `S(7)=13` literature. Step-1 sibling on the antichain is isomorphic to the optimal first-child, hence `F(Q_{1,n})=S(n)-1` in general by symmetry/deduction: `S(n)=1+min_{x‖y}\max(F(∅+x<y),F(∅+y<x))` and all pairs equivalent on `∅`.)

**History-minimality / `R(P_n)` for this construction (deduction + experiment for values).** The alternative depth-2 order `(0,1)` then `(2,0)` yields sibling step 2 state `{0<1,0<2}` (order-dual shape of `Q_{2,n}` on that triple). `F(P^{op})=F(P)` (deduction, order-duality invariance of the minimax, cf. `docs/research-compression-next.md`), so for `n=5,6` this gives the same sibling value `F=S(n)-1` and term `S(n)+1`. Any history using `>=3` comparisons to form the same `P_n` has `d+F(P_n) >= 3+F(P_n) = S(n)+1` for `n=5,6`. Therefore (experimentally grounded values, deductive combination) `R(P_5)=8=S(5)+1` and `R(P_6)=11=S(6)+1` for this `P_n`.

## 4. Assessment vs the front

### 4.1 What is achieved (supported)
- **Uniform parametric family of instances is explicit (definition/construction):** `n ↦ (P_n,h_n)` above is defined for all `n>=5`.
- **Theorem-level/fully verified obstruction on an infinite-in-`n` statement? no.** Verified (experiment + deduction) only for the subsequence cases `n=5,6`: `d_n+F(P_n)=S(n)` and `R(P_n)=S(n)+1 > S(n)` with an explicit forced sibling term (step 2, `Q_{2,n}`).
- **Failure of this natural gadget embedding for all `n>=5` (experiment):** for `n=7` the same construction gives `d_n+F(P_n)=12 < 13=S(7)` and `\max_i (i+F(Q_{i,7}^{sib}))=13 == S(7)`, so it neither meets the local-equality condition `d+F=S(n)` nor forces a strict sibling violation. Hence the isolated-elements extension of the `n=5` witness shape does **not** yield a uniform all-`n` (nor all large-`n`) obstruction family; it is not stable under this embedding.

### 4.2 Falsifier / obstacle status
- The prior `n=5` obstruction is **not** an isolated artifact (extends to `n=6` under the same uniform recipe), but the **specific natural gadget embedding (add isolated elements to the fixed triple/chain witness)** fails at `n=7` (and gives no evidence of recovery). This constitutes a concrete negative result for that embedding class (experiment), addressing the branch “or show such uniform construction fails under natural gadget embeddings” for this natural class.
- A uniform parametric construction for **all `n>=5` or an infinite subsequence** with proof remains **open**. The values suggest the gap `S(n)-F(P_n)` grows (2,2,3 for `n=5,6,7`) while depth stays 2 in this embedding, breaking `d+F=S(n)`; compensating would require a different, `n`-dependent path/state family, not this fixed gadget.
- No `(P,d,F)`-sufficient sibling-closed summary for the verified obstructed states was found (consistent with prior report); the obstruction here is precisely a forced sibling term invisible to `(P,d,F(P))` alone.

## 5. Obstacles
1. **Parameter sensitivity:** `F(P_n)` and `F(Q_{2,n})` co-evolve with `S(n)`; the equality/violation conditions `d_n+F(P_n)=S(n)` and `i+F(Q_{i}^{sib})>S(n)` are tight arithmetic constraints, easily broken by embedding (seen at `n=7`).
2. **Dependence on exact `S(n)` and minimax values:** proving the conditions for an infinite subsequence requires control of `F` on a parametric poset family against exact/known `S(n)` (values known only up to finite range in general; asymptotics like Sergeev give `S(n)-\log_2 n!` small on average sense but not the exact identities needed here).
3. **Global reachability `R(P)` vs a chosen `h`:** upgrading “some `h` has a bad sibling” to `R(P)>S(n)` needs an argument over all histories to `P` (manageable here by depth/duality for `n=5,6`, but nontrivial in general).
4. **Natural embeddings tested are limited:** only the isolated-elements extension of the fixed 3-chain witness was analyzed; other natural gadgets (e.g. ordinal-sum attachments, different fixed small witnesses, balanced-split prefixes) were not fully exhausted here, though none was found to close the all-`n` question.

## 6. Next decisive question

Can one construct an **`n`-dependent** (non-fixed-gadget) parametric transcript family `h_n`—e.g. tracking a comparison path whose local remainder mirrors the information/`S(n)` budget as in known exact/near-exact constructions for `S(n)`—and *prove* for an infinite subsequence that some forced sibling branch has `i_n+F(Q_{i_n,n}^{sib}) >= S(n)+1` while `d_n+F(P_n)=S(n)` (equivalently exhibits a state with `R(P_n)>S(n)` on the optimal frontier)? A decisive negative would be: prove that for all sufficiently large `n` (or for all optimal-frontier prefixes) every locally budget-tight state `d+F(P)=S(n)` admits some history with all sibling terms `<=S(n)` (a sibling-closure/sufficiency property), which would refute an infinite obstruction family of this exact form.
