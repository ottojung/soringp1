## 2. Obstacle N1 (exact-height local adequacy)

**Artifact claim.** For any polynomial-time locally checkable
DAG/certificate format `F` with local edge/join/leaf rules claiming
`S(n)<=k`, at exact height `k=S(n)` every accepted join/quotient must
preserve all adversary-relevant distinctions; otherwise split, stronger
annotation, or rejection. Therefore all-`n` `poly(N)` completeness for
`F` requires a uniform proof of a join-stable invariant system in `F`'s
annotation language with polynomially many reachable certified states.
Labeled `[deduction]`, adequacy/representation obstruction, not a known
superpolynomial lower bound for general NP verifiers.

**Soundness.** The core deduction is **sound as a conditional adequacy
constraint on locally certified strategies/joins**:

- If a *represented* strategy/certificate merges histories whose future
  optimal choices or remaining feasible depth differ in a way the local
  rules cannot justify at budget `S(n)`, then that local merge cannot be
  soundly accepted for exact height `S(n)`. Budget slack is indeed zero.
- Consequently, completeness for a fixed local-check format is not free:
  one needs either no problematic joins, polynomially many safely joined
  states with adequate invariants, or another globally sound mechanism
  captured by the format.

**Qualifications / minor precision issues.**

1. **"All adversary-relevant distinctions" is strategy-relative.** A
   certificate need not preserve every distinction present in the full
   game graph; it must preserve distinctions relevant to the *certified*
   sufficient strategy/invariant. The artifact's split/stronger
   annotation/rejection trichotomy covers this, but the prose could be
   read as requiring preservation of all game distinctions. Suggested
   reading: "all distinctions relevant to the soundness of the accepted
   exact-height claim."
2. **"Polynomially many reachable certified states" is sufficient, not
   shown necessary in that exact form.** A format could in principle use
   implicit/non-enumerative representations accepted by local/global rules;
   polynomial certificate size does not imply polynomially many explicit
   reachable states unless the format's structure forces that. N1 should
   be interpreted as: completeness requires a polynomially checkable proof
   of join-stable adequacy, not necessarily an explicit polynomial state
   enumeration.
3. **No lower-bound implication.** The artifact correctly states N1 is not
   a known superpolynomial lower bound for general NP verifiers. This is
   important and should be retained.

**Overall:** **mostly sound**, with scope wording tightened as above.

## 3. Obstacle N2 (logic-vs-certificate mismatch)

**Artifact claim.** `FO(PFP)` definability follows from `PSPACE`
membership but gives no short existential witness; `ESO` would give NP
witnesses but is exactly the open target; `FO(TC)`/`NL`-level
definability is unsupported by the game's alternation structure. Thus
descriptive-logic names alone do not resolve the question. `[deduction]`.

**Soundness.**

- **FO(PFP)/PSPACE:** Standard capture theorems (Abiteboul–Vianu, Vardi)
  give `FO(PFP)=PSPACE` on ordered finite structures. Since
  `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ PSPACE`, existence of such a definition is a reasonable
  capture consequence **once the input is encoded as an ordered structure
  in the usual descriptive-complexity setup**. This is an existence fact,
  not a short NP witness. **Sound with encoding caveat.**
- **ESO/NP:** `ESO = NP` on finite structures (Fagin). Therefore
  `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP` is equivalent to existence of an ESO definition; an
  ESO witness can be viewed as relations/certificate checked by a
  first-order sentence. The artifact correctly uses this as the
  certificate correspondence, not as evidence of membership. **Sound.**
- **FO(LFP)/P, FO(TC)/NL:** `FO(LFP)=P` and `FO(TC)=NL` on ordered
  structures are standard. The artifact's caution that direct
  `FO(TC)`/`NL` definability is *unsupported* by alternation/game
  intuition and is not an impossibility proof is appropriately hedged.
  **Sound as stated.**
- **No overclaim.** N2 does not claim any logic class separation or that
  `FO(PFP)` cannot be improved; it only blocks naming a logic as if it
  solved succinct certificates. **Good.**

**Minor issue.** The unary tally/string encoding is simple, but the note
should keep the "on ordered structures encoding the input" assumption
visible whenever invoking capture theorems; the artifact mostly does this.

## 4. Logic-vs-certificate distinctions and verifier gap

The artifact's separation in §2 is a strong point:

- **Representation language vs verifier:** enlarging annotations beyond
  PGF can avoid O1's specific meet failure but moves cost to syntax,
  entailment, join correctness, termination, and polytime local checks.
  **Sound.**
- **Program certificate gap:** a strategy/program is not automatically a
  polynomial-checkable certificate because universal correctness ranges
  over `n!` inputs unless local invariants/proof obligations are provided.
  VCG/VCG-1 captures this. **Sound and important.**
- **FO(PFP) as property definition, not NP witness:** correctly
  distinguished. **Sound.**
- **NP verifier / ESO / general nondeterministic certificate:** correctly
  identified as the broad nonframed polynomial-checkable target.
  **Sound.**

**Potential overclaim/looseness:** The text sometimes uses "ESO-style" in
a broad intuitive sense; formally the NP-certificate correspondence is
ESO. The artifact partly clarifies this, but readers should not treat
FO(TC)/FO(PFP) mentions as candidate NP witness forms. N2 already warns.

## 5. Claims about nonframed polynomial-checkable exact-height certificates

**Supported/appropriately cautious claims:**

- No known sound, complete, all-`n`, exact height-`S(n)`,
  polynomial-checkable nonframed certificate/program format. **Consistent
  with open status in `problem.md`/cert-barriers; no counterexample given.**
- Dropping PGF frames/meet removes O1 *as stated* but creates general
  local adequacy (N1). **Sound as a logical shift, not a solution.**
- Winning-frontier barrier blocks frontier enumeration but does not prove
  a lower bound against all succinct nonframed certificates. **Correct
  barrier discipline** (cf. `problem.md`/repo policy on barriers).
- An all-`n` representation theorem for a fixed nonframed class would imply
  `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ NP`; a superpolynomial lower bound for such a class
  would be a class barrier, not a separation of standard complexity
  classes. **Sound.**

**Claims needing citation/qualification:**

1. **"FJ is optimal on all known `n<=22` but asymptotically not exact; no
   all-`n` optimal family is explicitly certified"** (§5, VCG-2): the
   asymptotic non-exactness of FJ/merge insertion is literature-plausible
   given `S(n)-log2 n!=o(n)` and known FJ excess behavior, but this
   specific "optimal on all known n<=22" statement should cite a primary
   source/table. Local docs in this worktree do not establish it.
   Treat as **unverified literature claim in the artifact**.
2. **Exact values table `n=22: 71/70` etc.** Local
   `research-certificates-barriers.md` states exact `S(n)` known for
   `n<=22`; it does not list every value. The artifact should cite the
   exact-value source for the listed entries. Minor but relevant to
   falsifiable test targets.
3. **Quantitative winning-frontier `2^{(1/4-o(1))n log^2 n}`**: accepted
   only as an external local result named by the artifact; not
   re-audited here.
4. **Inline uncertainty note in §6** ("in the worst currently possible
   informative regime? note...") is editorially weak for a research note;
   it should be resolved or removed. It does not invalidate the main point
   (exact height has no/low slack).

No major lower-bound overclaim found. Specific checks:

- **No NP-hardness / no separation claim:** explicit in status and bottom
  line. **OK.**
- **No NP membership claim:** explicit. **OK.**
- **N1/N2 not sold as impossibility:** explicit. **OK.**
- **"Would place SORT-BOUND-U in NP"** is correctly conditioned on a
  sound, complete, poly-size, polytime-verifiable system. **OK.**

  claiming a major separation from the instance being in NP; it notes
  `SORT-BOUND-U` already in `PSPACE` and is a tally language. This is

  "FO(TC) impossible" — artifact says unsupported/not a proof of
  impossibility. **OK.**
