# Optimal-play state width/DAG size on height-`S(n)` strategies

**Status:** paper-level synthesis of repository results plus deduction. Not Lean-checked. No new computation run in this report beyond reading existing repo artifacts.

**Scope/model (fixed):** deterministic adaptive comparison sorting of `n` distinct labelled keys; `S(n)` is the minimax/worst-case comparison height. States are transitive partial orders `P` on `[n]` (posets). `F(P)` is the minimax remaining comparison count from `P` (`S(n)=F(∅)`). Unary decision length is `N = n+k+1` for `SORT-BOUND-U = {1^n#1^k : S(n) <= k}`; for the function/value question at the exact optimum take `k=S(n)=Θ(n log n)` so `N=Θ(n log n)`. All asymptotics below are in numeric `n` unless stated.

## 1. Precise definitions

- **Reachable state:** a poset obtainable from the empty antichain by some sequence of legal comparison outcomes (each compared pair incomparable before the comparison), with transitive closure after each outcome. `[deduction]`
- **Budget-`k` winning state (from root):** a reachable state `P` at comparison depth `t` with `F(P) <= k-t`. Equivalently, some correct continuation sorts every linear extension of `P` within the remaining `k-t` comparisons. `[deduction]`
- **Optimal (height-`S(n)`) play/path:** a root-to-total-order path `P_0,…,P_m` (`P_0=∅`, `P_m` total, `m <= S(n)`) such that every step is a legal comparison outcome and the path is realizable by a strategy achieving worst-case height `S(n)` (i.e. for the chosen minimizer moves, `1 + max(F(child_yes),F(child_no)) = F(parent)` along the path). `[definition]`
- **Single-policy strategy DAG:** hash-consed graph of states visited by one fixed optimal strategy (one chosen optimal move per nonterminal state). `[definition]`
- **Optimal-play union DAG (all optimal strategies / all optimal plays):** `U_n = {P : P` lies on some optimal height-`S(n)` play`}` with edges given by optimal moves/outcomes. This is the "states on optimal plays/DAGs" object asked for, and is **not** all reachable states and not all winning states. `[definition]`
- **Depth / layer:** `L_{n,t} = {P ∈ U_n : depth(P)=t}` (exact number of comparisons performed).
- **Width (two standard readings):**
  - *layer width* `w(n,t)=|L_{n,t}|` (labelled states), or its isomorphism quotient `w_~(n,t)=|L_{n,t}/\!\cong|`;
  - *frontier/DAG width* `W(n)=\max_t w(n,t)` and `W_~(n)=\max_t w_~(n,t)`.
  A polynomial/quasi-polynomial/superpolynomial bound is asked for as a function of `n` (equivalently `N`, up to polynomial/log factors since `N=Θ(n log n)` at `k=S(n)`).

## 2. Known small-`n` evidence (optimal-union, not winning frontier)

From `docs/research-strategy-certificates.md` / `scripts/strategy_dag_probe.py` (`[experiment]`, `n <= 6`):

| n | S(n) | single-policy DAG nodes | all-optimal-move DAG nodes | quotient(all) | n! |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| 3 | 3 | 11 | 19 | 5 | 6 |
| 4 | 5 | 47 | 181 | 12 | 24 |
| 5 | 7 | 239 | 1551 | 19 | 120 |
| 6 | 10 | 1439 | 65551 | 134 | 720 |

- A **single optimal policy** is an unshared tree with `2n!-1` nodes and exactly `n!` leaves; hash-consing saves zero because distinct reachable nodes of a reduced deterministic comparison tree have distinct labelled posets (`[deduction]`, Thm 2.6/Cor 2.7 there). Thus "optimal-play DAG size" for one fixed play is tree-sized/exponential, but in a trivial non-sharing way.
- The **union over optimal moves** is much smaller than an unshared union but still grows quickly (65k labelled states, 134 nonisomorphic at `n=6`). `log2(DAG(all))/n` increases over this tiny range (`[experiment]`), which is consistent with superpolynomial growth but **not** an asymptotic proof. No `n>=7` optimal-union width data is established here.

## 3. Main characterization: a superpolynomial optimal-play frontier exists

**Falsifiable claim (paper-level theorem, from `docs/research-winning-frontier-barrier.md`):** there are infinitely many `n` (e.g. `n=s^2`, `s=2^r`) and a depth `t` such that the isomorphism-quotient layer width on optimal height-`S(n)` plays satisfies
\[
  w_~(n,t) \;\ge\; 2^{(\frac{1}{4}-o(1))\, n\, (\log_2 n)^2 }.
\]
Equivalently, for every fixed polynomial `p`, `W_~(n) > p(n)` and `W_~(n) > p(N)` for infinitely many `n`.

**Argument (summary of cited construction):** `[paper theorem]`
1. Build `s` disjoint sorted chains of length `L` (`n=sL`) by `t0=s(L-1)` fixed comparisons, then add any `m` extra forward adjacent-level edges from `M=(L-1)(s^2-s)` possibilities. Any such set is realizable by `t=t0+m` legal comparisons in some order, and different sets give different labelled posets on the adjacent-level relations (`binom(M,m)` labelled states).
2. Each constructed state is sortable by a standard chain-merge continuation in at most `B=(s-1)+(n-1)\lceil\log_2 s\rceil` further comparisons, so `F(P) <= B`.
3. Set `m = k-t0-B` with budget `k = C(n)+d(n)`, `d(n)>=0`, `d(n)=o(n)`. For `s=L=2^r`, Stirling gives `m = n r - O(n)+o(n)=Θ(n log n)` and `M=Θ(n^{3/2})`, so `0<m<M/2` for large `n`. Then `F(P) <= B = k-t`, hence every constructed `P` is a **budget-`k` winning** state at depth `t`.
4. At `k=S(n)`, `d(n)=S(n)-C(n)=o(n)` by Sergeev (ECCC TR20-096, Thm 3, asymptotic; no effective finite threshold). Therefore for all sufficiently large `n` in this family, every such `P` lies on **some** height-`S(n)` winning play from the root (the root can achieve `S(n)` and the constructed state still can achieve the remaining budget).
5. Mod isomorphism, divide by at most `n!` labelings: `\log_2(\binom(M,m)/n!) >= m\log_2(M/m)-\log_2(n!) = n r^2 - O(n r \log r) = (\tfrac14-o(1))n(\log_2 n)^2`.

**Finite witness:** `[paper theorem + executable integer check in repo tests]` `n=224, s=16, L=14, k=C(224)=1431, t=524, B=907, m=316, M=3120` gives `binom(3120,316) > 2^39 * 224!`, so `w_~(224,524) > 2^39` even at the information budget; the asymptotic extension gives the same phenomenon at `k=S(n)`.

**Consequence for the posed quantity:** the number/width of states on optimal (height-`S(n)`) plays, in the natural *union/layer-width* sense, is **provably superpolynomial** (indeed `2^{\Omega(n\log^2 n)}` nonisomorphic at some depth, infinitely often, under Sergeev's asymptotic surplus bound). No polynomial or quasi-polynomial upper bound on `W_~(n)` or `W(n)` is possible as a uniform all-`n` statement consistent with this theorem.

## 4. Falsification and limits (what would break or narrow this)

- **Falsifying the superpolynomial claim would require** exhibiting any family of uniform bounds `W_~(n) <= n^{O(1)}` or `<= 2^{(\log n)^{O(1)}}` for all sufficiently large `n` (or for the specific family above), contradicting the constructed `2^{(1/4-o(1))n\log^2 n}` layer. `[deduction]`
- **Finite-threshold caveat:** the specialization to `k=S(n)` uses Sergeev's asymptotic `S(n)-C(n)=o(n)` and is not an effective finite `n_0`; the finite `n=224` witness is stated at `k=C(224)`. Any finite claim "at exact `S(n)` for this `n`" needs either that asymptotic regime or a direct value/upper bound. `[paper theorem, stated limitation]`
- **Model sensitivity:** this bounds the *union* of states on some optimal plays (a frontier). It does **not** say every single optimal strategy must enumerate or traverse all these states; a single policy remains a tree. It does **not** give a uniform time/space lower bound for evaluating `S(n)`. `[deduction]`
- **Tighter characterization open:** the exact growth of `W(n)`/`W_~(n)` for all `n`, the worst-case depth profile, and whether the exponent can be improved beyond `\Omega(n\log^2 n)` or matched by a general upper bound below the `2^{\Theta(n^2)}` reachable-poset count, remain open. Small-`n` optimal-union data (`n<=6`) are too sparse to discriminate growth rates. `[open]`

## 5. Consequence for framed-DAG / NP-certificate plausibility

- **Soundness/checking:** a permutation-framed strategy DAG/certificate, if given explicitly, is checkable in time polynomial in its explicit size and `n` (`[paper theorem/implemented checker]`, `docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md`). So polynomial size would imply a polynomial-time verifier.
- **What the barrier rules out:** any proposed **framed DAG certificate (or any certificate representation) whose correctness requires explicitly materializing/enumerating the optimal-play winning frontier or all optimal-play states at a critical depth** cannot be polynomial-size in `n` or `N`: some depth requires `2^{\Omega(n\log^2 n)}` nonisomorphic states on optimal plays. This is a precise obstruction to "enumerate optimal states, then frame/share them" approaches. `[deduction from §3]`
- **What remains plausible/open:** NP membership of `SORT-BOUND-U` would follow only from a different kind of certificate: a polynomial-size object that is **sound but intentionally forgetful/compressive**, representing some height-`S(n)` strategy without one node per optimal-play frontier state (e.g. via joins/invariants/rank envelopes) while remaining locally verifiable. Existing constructive polynomial framed DAGs cover non-optimal schedules (bubble sort, merge-sort schedule, plausible Ford–Johnson upper-bound framing pending audit) but **not** an all-`n` exact-`S(n)` completeness theorem; Sergeev-compatible optimal or near-optimal compression is open. `[paper constructions/open, framed-certificate-frontier]`
- **Net assessment:** the posed question resolves to a **barrier/characterization** rather than a polynomial bound: optimal-play *frontier width* is superpolynomial in the union sense, so framed-DAG/NP plausibility hinges on non-enumerative compression with exact optimality, which is currently unproved and constrained but not refuted outright. `[deduction]`

## 6. Bottom line

- **Bound/characterization:** `W_~(n)=\max_t |(\text{states on some height-}S(n)\text{ play at depth }t)/\!\cong| = 2^{\Omega(n\log^2 n)}` infinitely often (paper-level, asymptotic at exact optimum). Therefore not polynomial, not quasi-polynomial. `[paper theorem + deduction]`
- **Barrier identified:** superpolynomial optimal-play layer/frontier prevents any certificate method based on enumerating optimal-play states/layers. `[deduction]`
- **Certificate consequence:** polynomial framed-DAG witness for `SORT-BOUND-U` is possible only via strong compression beyond frontier enumeration; existence at height `S(n)` remains open. `[open]`
