# Exec report: structural explanation / generalization barrier for the `n=12` obstruction

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Workspace:** `/home/lubko/soring301-exec-n12-gap-invariant-20261117b` (SoringP1)  
**Scope:** unary/active problem as in `AGENTS.md`, `docs/problem.md` (`S(n)`, `SORT-BOUND-U`); no Lean, no repo-wide edits beyond this report, no blind re-enumeration.

## 1. Falsifiable question

Can we identify and justify an invariant or structural explanation for the `n=12` obstruction
`S(12)=30 > C(12)=29` (`C(n)=⌈log₂ n!⌉`) that generalizes to an **all-`n` or infinitely-often** lower-bound witness/gap condition stronger than the information (leaf-count) bound alone, or else exhibit a **precise barrier** to such generalization from known counterexamples/invariants?

## 2. Hypothesis (tested)

**Hypothesis H (structural, local-strengthening route).** The `+1` gap at `n=12` is explained by a reusable local invariant stronger than leaf counting — e.g. a scalar/monotone function of extension counts `e(P)`, a small fixed-depth strengthening of the node information bound (one-ply/adversarial split), forced-prefix balance constraints, symmetry/efficiency windows, or merge/ordinal-sum factors — and that invariant yields a general gap certificate `S(n) ≥ C(n)+δ(n)` beyond isolated cases.

This is the natural generalization direction: lift the node condition `e(P) ≤ 2^{h-c}` to a stronger but still locally checkable witness.

## 3. Evidence / derivation (from repo notes + primary literature)

### 3.1 Baseline facts `[literature]`
- `S(n)=C(n)` for `1 ≤ n ≤ 11`; `S(12)=30 > C(12)=29` (Wells 1966/1971, reported in Peczarski 2012). Exact values known for `n ≤ 22` with `S(16..18)=46,50,54` (Stober–Weiß, ALENEX 2023, arXiv:2206.05597); `n=23` open. `S(n)-log₂ n! = O(n/(log n)^{1/5})` (Sergeev). (`docs/literature.md`, `docs/research-plan.md`)
- Information bound `[definition/deduction]`: `e(P) ≤ 2^{h-c}` after `c` comparisons in a height-`h` tree, hence `S(n) ≥ C(n)`. Efficiency pruning used in searches is equivalent to this node-level condition, not a new invariant (`docs/research-n12-nontightness.md` §4).

### 3.2 What the `n=12` proof establishes `[literature]+[deduction]`
- Wells/Stober–Weiß certify `S(12)>29` by exhaustive admissible-state search (one-sided forward search empties a level for `n=12`; Stober–Weiß Table 2: `sortable=no`, search-effort figures only). There is no published short closed-form certificate; the obstruction is not localized to a small named bottleneck set by those figures (`docs/research-n12-nontightness.md` §4, §8).

### 3.3 Candidate local/structural strengthenings and their limits
- **Scalar slack `2^{C(n)}/n!` (H1).** `[experiment]` Falsified as predictor: `n=5` tight with less slack than `n=12`; `n=20` tight, `n=22` not, with opposite slack extremes (`research-n12-nontightness.md`).
- **Monotonicity / function-of-`e` only (H2,H3).** `[experiment]+[literature]` `m(P)` is not monotone in `e(P)` (smallest `n=6`: `e=13,m=5` harder than `e=14,m=4`; Peczarski’s `n=13` `P16/Q'16`). Same `e`, different `m` already at `n=5,e=8` (`m=3` or `4`); local info bound first fails at `e=8` while `e=7` is tight (Hanamura–Iwata 2011). Thus no invariant depending only on `e(P)` (or efficiency/slack) can determine sortability or explain the global gap (`research-n12-nontightness.md` §5,§8).
- **Forced balanced prefix (H4).** `[deduction]+[experiment]` Real at `n=12,h=29` (only `1/2` splits admissible early; chain-free `2/3`, cross-chain `5/6` inadmissible), but the same forced-prefix phenomenon occurs at tight `n=5,10` — necessary shape constraint, not a predictor of a gap (`research-n12-nontightness.md`).
- **Symmetry (H6).** `[experiment]` Neither necessary nor sufficient for local tightness (loose posets with `|Aut|>1` at `n=6`) (`research-n12-nontightness.md`).
- **One-ply adversarial bound `L1(P)` (`L0 ≤ L1 ≤ F`).** `[deduction]+[experiment]` Strictly stronger than leaf counting on an infinite family (smallest witness `n=5`, `e=8`, `L0=3,L1=F=4`, and `W ⊕ C_t` for all `t≥0`, Prop. 3 in `research-one-ply-obstruction.md`). However it is a **local, pairwise-split** strengthening and does **not** lift the antichain root: for any `n,h=C(n)`, some comparison splits extensions as evenly as possible, so `1 + max(L0(children)) = 1 + ⌈log₂⌈n!/2⌉⌉ = C(n)=L0(P0)`. Hence `L1` cannot by itself certify `S(n)>C(n)` at the root.
- **Compositional/merge-factor bound `Σ info(P_i)+Σ δ_i`.** `[deduction]+[experiment]` Sound and can beat global leaf counting when certified merge blocks or rounding-before-multiplication appear (smallest strict `Merge(2,4)`, `n=6`; `n=7` rounding witness) (`research-structural-lower-bounds.md`). But `antichain_n` is a single incomparability component with no certified merge factor, so this bound collapses to the information bound at the root; it explains *poset/block* gaps, not the antichain gap at `n=12`.
- **Efficiency frontier / bandwidth (H5).** `[literature]+[deduction]` Algorithmic search guide equivalent to info bound at nodes; bandwidth/counts are search effort, not a measured structural bottleneck invariant (`research-n12-nontightness.md`).

## 4. Generalization attempt

A general gap condition stronger than `C(n)` would need, for the antichain root (or some forced reachable prefix), a **uniformly checkable certificate** that every height-`C(n)` strategy must enter a state/subtree where remaining budget is below a provable local requirement. Candidate general forms:
1. *Root-local lift:* `L'(P0) > C(n)` for some efficiently characterizable `L'∈{L1, merge/series, scalar(e), ...}`.
2. *Infinitely-often scalar law:* gap determined by `n!`, slack, or another function of `n` alone.
3. *Strategic frontier invariant:* a property preserved/forced along all admissible trees implying emptiness of some level at `h=C(n)`.

**Outcome:** (1) fails for all examined local strengthenings at the root (antichain admits balanced first ply; no merge decomposition; `e` non-determinative). (2) is contradicted by known values (`n=5,10,11,20` tight under varying slack; `n=12,22` gaps at different slack). (3) is exactly what exhaustive search establishes but no reusable, non-search-equivalent invariant is known.

## 5. Falsification / precise barrier to generalization

**Barrier (supported, precise):** The `n=12` obstruction is **strategic and global, not reducible to any known local scalar or fixed finite-ply function of `e(P)`/balance/symmetry of individual states**. Concretely:
- `[literature/experiment]` Sortability is non-monotone and non-functional in `e(P)` (H2/H3, Peczarski `n=13`), so *any* invariant that is a function/monotone transform of extension counts alone cannot be complete and cannot generalize to a gap certificate.
- `[deduction]` One-ply (and any root-balanced local ply scored by `L0`) is tight to `C(n)` at `antichain_n`, so finite local leaf-strengthening at the root gives no `+1` witness for `S(n)`.
- `[literature]` Known non-tight points (`n=12`, and later `n=22` per exact tables; information-bound misses also analyzed for `n=13,14,15` by Peczarski) are established by exhaustive/case-analysis search methods, not by a compact invariant; local forced-prefix conditions appear at both tight and non-tight `n` (H4), and search-effort metrics do not constitute a bottleneck theorem.

Therefore, under currently known counterexamples/invariants, a general all-`n` or i.o. gap condition stronger than `C(n)` would have to be a **tree-level/strategic** invariant (tracking routability of all `n!` permutations through the admissible frontier) rather than a state-local or `e`-scalar one. No such reusable invariant is identified in the audited sources/notes; producing one would amount to a new lower-bound technique (open). Conversely, the absence is evidenced but not proved impossible in absolute terms except against the specific families above.

## 6. Consequence for bounds on `S(n)`

- **Lower bounds:** No new uniform `S(n) ≥ C(n)+1` law or i.o. witness condition follows. The leaf-count bound `S(n) ≥ C(n)` remains the baseline; the only certified strengthenings are *instance/poset-local* (`L1`, merge/rounding factors) and do not upgrade the antichain/root bound for `n=12` without strategic search. Explaining/predicting gaps like `n=12,22` remains an open structural problem.
- **Upper/evaluation relevance:** This reinforces that pruning by local `e`/balance/merge structure is sound but, by itself, cannot remove the strategic obstruction; consistent with known barriers to shrinking the exact-evaluation exponent without avoiding frontier enumeration (`docs/research-plan.md`, winning-frontier barrier). Any future improvement must supply a *non-scalar, strategy-aware* certificate or argument.
- **Claim status:** all consequences above are **deductions from cited literature and repo experiments**; no new numerical value of `S(n)` is claimed here. A paper proof of a reusable gap invariant (or a proof that none of a broad strategic class exists) would be required for integration under `AGENTS.md`.
