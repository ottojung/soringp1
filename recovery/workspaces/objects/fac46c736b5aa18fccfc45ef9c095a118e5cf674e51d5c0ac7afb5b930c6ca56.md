## Verdict (summary)

The artifact **does not** provide a complete discharged fixed syntactic
FO(IFP)/FO(PFP) sentence proof from first principles. It **partially discharges
the stated packing residual**: §2 gives an explicit fixed-arity single
configuration relation `Cnf` of arity `1+A=9` with FO tag projections
`Decode_i`/`Encode_i` and canonical padding; §3 gives the systematic
decode/encode translation `Θ^#` and `Step^# := Δ_{M_D}^#`; Lemma 3.2 proves the
exact one-step correspondence
`Step^#(Cnf;Cnf') iff Δ_{M_D}(decode(Cnf);decode(Cnf'))` and states the packed
one-step operator is exactly the operator induced by `Δ_{M_D}` up to
decode/encode. This addresses the core simultaneous-to-single-relation packing
and one-step operator-induction part of `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK]` as stated by
delta-d-dfs-stack.

However, the artifact's final sentence/acceptance construction (§4) is **not a
correct formal discharge** of whole-configuration reachability/acceptance under
that packed operator: `ψ_reach`/`Init^#_{tuple}`/`Step^#_{tuple}` are schematic
and, as written, conflate tagged tuples of a single packed configuration with
an IFP relation accumulating tuples across all reached configurations. `Δ_{M_D}`
and `Step^#` are operators on *whole* configuration interpretations (all
component relations / all tagged tuples of one configuration), so a correct
fixed-point acceptance proof must use simultaneous IFP/PFP over the component
family, or an equivalent standard normal form that codes whole configurations
and deterministic successor/acceptance on ordered structures. Because of this
gap, and because the end-to-end chain inherits paper-level correctness of
`Δ_{M_D}`/`M_D^alt` and project-specific semantic equivalences, the note is
**not** a complete discharged fixed syntactic FO(IFP)/FO(PFP) sentence proof.

**Single minimal failing lemma (isolated):**
> **[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK-REACH] (whole-configuration IFP/PFP acceptance normal
> form over the packed `Δ_{M_D}` operator).** Give an explicit, fixed
> FO(IFP) or FO(PFP) sentence/operator over fixed `A(w)` that, using the packed
> single-relation/decoded component representation and the already-discharged
> one-step correspondence
> `Step^#(Cnf;Cnf') iff Δ_{M_D}(decode(Cnf);decode(Cnf'))`, correctly expresses
> acceptance by iterated whole-configuration reachability from `Init_{M_D}` to
> `HaltAcc` under the deterministic `Δ_{M_D}`/`M_D^alt` computation (in the
> standard Vardi/Immerman/Abiteboul–Vianu configuration-capture normal form, or
> an explicit simultaneous fixed-point normal form with proof of the standard
> simultaneous-to-single equivalence for this operator), and prove
> `A(w)|=Φ_{M_D}` iff the packed `HaltAcc` configuration is reachable iff
> `M_D^alt` accepts `w`.

Lemma 3.2's one-step packing/operator correspondence is retained as a
discharged sublemma; `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK-REACH]` does not reopen local move
semantics, `Δ_{M_D}` stack-discipline cases, or A001035/`PACK-REPR`-style
global `Pos` naming.

No `P`, no `FO(LFP)` on the bare ordered word, no `NP`, and no `coNP` claim is
made by this audit.

## 2. Detailed check of the claimed `[open:CKS-AV-Δ_D/PACK]` discharge

Delta-d-dfs-stack stated the residual as:

> Give the standard finite-model-theory packing (explicit simultaneous-to-single
> PFP, or an explicit single configuration relation with FO projections/updates)
> of the fixed finite family of configuration relations in §2 into the exact
> fixed-arity FO(PFP)/IFP sentence form used by the chosen
> Vardi/Immerman/Abiteboul–Vianu presentation, and prove that the packed
> one-step operator is exactly the operator induced by the case-closed
> `Δ_{M_D}` of §5.

**Discharged parts.**
- Explicit single configuration relation with FO projections/updates: yes,
  `Cnf`/`Decode_i`/`Encode_i`/padding/tags.
- Packed one-step operator exactly induced by case-closed `Δ_{M_D}`: yes, at the
  one-step level, by `Θ^#` structural translation and Lemma 3.2:
  `Step^#(Cnf;Cnf') iff Δ_{M_D}(decode(Cnf);decode(Cnf'))`.

**Not fully discharged as a complete sentence-form proof.**
- "Exact fixed-arity FO(PFP)/IFP sentence form..." in the strong end-to-end
  sense: the artifact supplies a sentence shape, but the acceptance fixed point
  does not correctly implement whole-configuration reachability of the packed
  operator. The standard capture normal form for a deterministic PSPACE
  computation/configuration graph requires a correct treatment of whole
  configurations and their one-step successor relation or operator; this is not
  completed by §4.1.
- The end-to-end equivalence in §5 therefore remains conditional, not a
  discharged proof.

Thus the packing residual is **substantially advanced** (one-step operator
packing and correspondence are explicit), but not fully discharged as a
complete fixed syntactic FO(IFP)/FO(PFP) sentence proof.

## 3. Inherited assumptions (must remain visible)

The PACK artifact explicitly inherits, and this audit does not discharge:

1. **`Δ_{M_D}` correctness/discipline.** Delta-d-dfs-stack gave a case-closed
   FO schema and paper-level determinism/totality/invariant/no-spurious
   arguments; its audit found those plausible but not formally discharged and
   noted schematic helper/marker/encoding details. PACK's `Step^#` translates
   `Δ_{M_D}` syntactically, so it inherits any incompleteness in `Δ_{M_D}`.
2. **`M_D^alt` semantics/halting.** The end-to-end chain uses acceptance and
   halting of the deterministic DFS stack evaluator `M_D^alt`.
3. **CKS/AV and game equivalences.** `M_D^alt` accepts `w` iff alternating DFS
   evaluation accepts the initial `M_G/G(n,k)` state iff sorter wins `G(n,k)`
   iff `S(n)<=k` iff `w ∈ SORT-BOUND-U` are project-specific/semantic
   equivalences drawn from prior artifacts and the project model.
4. **Standard descriptive-complexity semantics.** FO(IFP)/FO(PFP)=PSPACE on
   ordered structures, IFP/PFP semantics, simultaneous fixed-point normal forms
