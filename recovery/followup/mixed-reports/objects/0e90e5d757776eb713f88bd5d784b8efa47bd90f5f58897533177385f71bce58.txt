# 3. Specific obstacles blocking the 5 open questions

1. **Shrinking the exponent** (`2^{O(n log n)}` or `2^{O(n)}`): the winning-frontier count rules out any method that *materializes* winning states (≥`2^{Ω(n log²n)}`); the transcript-DFS family is provably stuck (Θ(n log n) levels × Θ(log n)-bit cursor, cursor not recoverable from transcript); Aut-quotient fails on staircase posets with trivial Aut and Θ(n²) singleton move orbits; ordinal-sum/duality/module reversals are exact but leave the root antichain series-prime and the `2^{Θ(n²)}` exponent unchanged; poly-reachable optimal trees still need `2^{Ω(n log n)}` states. **Gap:** no non-enumerative representation; catalytic/reversible/symbolic paradigms unexamined.
2. **Certificates** (poly-size framed DAG at exact S(n)): rank-envelope joins are sound only at Ford–Johnson height `S(n)+Θ(n)`; weak rank windows are too coarse for Sergeev's `o(n)` surplus while coupled containers force non-mergeable states; starved optimal-play states (n=8/9/10) break exact-height joins locally; hash-consing a single optimal policy yields `2n!−1`; 0/1 principle shrinks the test universe to 2ⁿ but not verification work; coNP explicit adversary refuter needs `2^{Θ(n²)}` states. Completeness / proof-size lower bound both open.
3. **Space** (`O(N)` or polylog): within transcript DFS, Ω(log n) bits per level × Θ(N) depth is unavoidable (cursor history lost), and representing a d-edge poset costs Ω(d log(n²/d)) = Θ(N log N) bits; full closure is Θ(n²). Winning-frontier enumeration does **not** convert to a workspace lower bound; tally/sparse barriers give no lower bound; no communication-complexity or space-hierarchy reduction is known; catalytic/reversible paths unexamined.
4. **Bound gap** (effective Sergeev, explain n=12): concrete constants **cannot** be extracted from the primary source (audit refuted; proof uses unquantified O(·) and "sufficiently large"); all scalar invariants falsified; D(n)≤1 certified only to n=24 (first FJ allowance D≥2 at n=25); n=12 localizes to the depth-2 state FF_n but no amplification to infinitely many gaps; certifying a defect needs the #P-hard test `e(child) > 2^{L(parent)−1}`; S(23) unsettled (width-1 window).
5. **Descriptive complexity**: FO(LFP) captures P, so an FO(LFP) definition of SORT-BOUND-U ⟺ SORT-BOUND-U ∈ P — unproved, and proving non-definability would give P≠PSPACE; FO(PFP) capture gives *existence* only, no explicit formula; winning-frontier excludes only width-1 LFP, not polynomial width; the known DP is `2^{Θ(n²)}` with no polynomial-time bridge.

**1. Turing-reduce exact F(P) to a #P oracle for linear extensions.** Is there a poly-time (or subexponential-query) algorithm computing F(P) — hence S(n) — given an oracle for e(·) on *derived* posets?
- *What changes:* places S(n) in FP^#P; shows the sole obstacle is #P counting rather than game-tree breadth, redirecting evaluator work to query-efficient oracle algorithms.
- *Why untried / not ruled out:* `research-hardness-boundaries.md` Cor. 5.2 explicitly says the one-shot `f(e(P))` route is dead (n=5 witness) but "a more elaborate Turing reduction that queries e(·) on derived posets is **not ruled out**." `defect-pruning-certificate-completeness-alln` only shows the natural greedy test is #P-hard — complementary, not a reduction impossibility. **Flag: distinct from the e(P) non-reduction; verify no older ledger front on "#P oracle".**

**2. Polynomial/constant bound on increments S(n+1)−S(n) and local self-reducibility.** Is there an explicit c with S(n+1)−S(n) ≤ c for all n (data max is 4 at 11→12), or an unconditional O(log n) bound?

- *Why untried:* recent additive-constant fronts concerned `B(n)−n` and `M(n)−C(n)`, not increments of S. **Flag: low overlap; check older ledger for "increment/monotone-gap".**

**3. Complexity placement of the binary threshold SORT-BOUND-B in bit-length m.** Is SORT-BOUND-B in EXPTIME (i.e. `2^{poly(m)}`), or many-one NP-hard?
