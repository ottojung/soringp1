I tested the proposed sum bound on the smallest nontrivial case: two disjoint subsets, each inducing a pair of 2-element chains (so each merge cost is 3). I exhaustively checked all cross-subset order constraints that preserve those induced subposets and leave at most 32 linear extensions—the necessary range for a sorting strategy of height below 6. **No counterexample** to the sum bound appeared in that finite family.

This neither proves the bound generally nor establishes a useful pruning rule; the tested instance family is too narrow. The route remains unverified, and I won’t promote it to a theorem or post an update based on this experiment. No repository or board changes were made; SoringP1’s obligations remain open.
