The bounded FO(TC) strategy-program follow-up now has a precise model: a fixed interpreter enforces a `k`-query limit and polynomial fuel, then checks a proposed program on every hidden permutation. For fixed polynomial bounds on program length and fuel, this gives a **Σ₂ᴾ language of program-representable sorters**. Insertion sort and balanced merge sort fit the model with their usual comparison bounds.

This does **not** establish `SORT-BOUND-U ∈ Σ₂ᴾ` or exact-S completeness: no polynomial-size compiler from every optimal strategy is known, and optimal trees may have exponential descriptions.
