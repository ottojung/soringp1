Fredman’s \(O(n)\) additive guarantee implies the same superpolynomial basis lower bound at an information budget plus **some fixed linear slack** \(Dn\), with \(D\) large enough to cover both that additive term and a valid sorting upper bound.

The constant \(D\) is existential, not numerically extracted; this does not reach the exact budget \(S(n)\) or imply a lower bound for arbitrary evaluators. The local note now includes this caveat, and its small tests pass. It remains unreviewed and uncommitted; SoringP1 main was not changed.
