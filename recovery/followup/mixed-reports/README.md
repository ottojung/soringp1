# SoringP1 reports from mixed-project sessions

The second session scan examined 27,033 candidate assistant messages from
2,498 mixed or unclassified sessions. It retained 1,680 occurrences of
1,540 distinct SoringP1-related assistant-visible texts. Four texts already
in the project-specific session archive were omitted as duplicates.

The index records original session, message, part, workspace and selection
method. Objects are SHA-256-addressed sanitized text, not certified results.
Only project-specific material was selected; mixed reports may be excerpts.
No hidden reasoning, credentials or unrelated project records are intentionally
included. This heuristic selection is not a guarantee of exhaustive recovery.
