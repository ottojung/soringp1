Mature source-grounded complexity classification boundary, unary-only. Berman & Hartmanis, *On Isomorphisms and Density of NP and Other Complete Sets* (SIAM J. Comput. 6(2), 1977, DOI 10.1137/0206023) prove no polynomially sparse language is EXPTIME-complete under polynomial-time many-one reductions. `SORT-BOUND-U` has at most `N` valid/accepted strings of each input length `N` (and at most `O(N²)` up to length `N`), and the existing exact DFS puts it in EXPTIME. Thus it is **unconditionally not EXPTIME-hard under polynomial-time many-one reductions**. This repairs an earlier research comment mistakenly saying 'at most one string per length'.

Carefully separates this unconditional EXPTIME non-hardness from conditional NP sparsity barriers (Mahaney), and from polynomial-time Turing reductions (not excluded). No P/NP membership or separation is claimed, and the result is marked literature + deduction rather than Lean-kernel-checked.
