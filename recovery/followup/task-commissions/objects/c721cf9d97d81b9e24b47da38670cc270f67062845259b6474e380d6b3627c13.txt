"Single precise target for SoringP1 #301. Prove or refute:

LEMMA A. Let P be a finite poset with exact minimax comparison depth m=m(P)>=2 (m(P)=1+min over incomparable pairs {a,b} of max(m(P+ab),m(P+ba))). Then some incomparable pair {a,b} has BOTH children with minimax >= m-2.

Why it matters: I have shown (and you should check) that Lemma A implies e(P) >= F_{m(P)+2} for all posets (e = # linear extensions, F_1=F_2=1) by induction on e, since e(P)=e1+e2, every pair has max(m1,m2)>=m-1, and the Fibonacci identity F_{m+1}+F_m=F_{m+2} closes it. That would give the all-r threshold theorem T(r)=F_{r+3}-1 and hence the open T(6)=33 (fence S_r supplies matching witnesses e=F_{r+1}, m=r-1).

Known status: unproved. A previous agent reported the exact obstruction: for an optimal pair (max child depth m-1) the other child may have depth <= m-3, and it could not show that either some other pair is balanced, or that P is then sortable in m-1 comparisons. Falsification so far (exact minimax, zero counterexamples): all posets n<=9 with e<=33 and m>=2 (14,474 posets, incl. 3,393 with m=6); all posets n<=8 with e<=200 (11,206); fence family S_n, m<=10. Note C_{n-1}+isolated is NOT a counterexample: querying the isolated element against a chain element leaves ~n/2 extensions of depth ~log2(n/2) = m-2.
