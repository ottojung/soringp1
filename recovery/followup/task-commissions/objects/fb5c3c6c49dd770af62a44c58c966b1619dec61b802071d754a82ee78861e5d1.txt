# SoringP1 research front: framed-DAG certificate completeness at n=6

## Question (falsifiable)
Can a polynomial-size permutation-framed DAG of height F_FJ(6)=12 sort 6 distinct keys? Recent work showed n=5 admits a prefix-determined framed DAG at depth S(5)=7 (239 nodes) but the Ford-Johnson joined rank-envelope insertion cannot be encoded polynomially with prefix-determined frames at n=5. The general question—optimal-size completeness of framed DAGs for sorting—is open. n=6 is the next case after n=5.

## Why it constrains S(n) complexity
If polynomial-size framed DAGs can certify optimal sorting strategies, SORT-BOUND-U would be in NP (a polynomial-size checkable witness). If framed DAGs require superpolynomial size at optimal height, that's a barrier to succinct certificates. Either outcome changes what we know about SORT-BOUND-U's complexity class.

## Method
Study docs/research-framed-certificate-frontier.md and the existing framed-DAG code (scripts/fj_cert.py lineage, scripts/check_framed_strategy.py). Extend the exact-position framed DAG construction from n=5 to n=6: key shared nodes on the full frame signature (compare indices + both perms), generate certificates using the FJ schedule with block-transposition frames, verify with check_framed_strategy.py. Test whether polynomial node count is achievable at height F_FJ(6)=12, or whether exact-position sharing forces exponential blowup.
