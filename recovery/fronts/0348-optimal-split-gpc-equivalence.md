## question

Is the single-optimal-branch property - every finite poset with m(P)>=2 has a minimax-optimal comparison whose child depths are m(P)-1 and at least m(P)-2 - equivalent, for all finite posets, to the Gold Partition Conjecture?

## basis

Newest board #301 optimal-fibonacci-split-alln reports finite checks through e=70, calls the property stronger than Lemma A, and leaves the maximal-insertion extension lemma open; Peczarski 2006 and Gupta 2026 supply the GPC side.

## falsifier

An explicit finite poset satisfying GPC but violating the single-optimal-branch property, or a finite poset satisfying that property while violating GPC, with exact minimax and extension-count values for every comparison.

## implication

An equivalence would transfer GPC's width-two, semiorder and through-14-element results to the blocked optimal-split front; a separation would show that minimax-optimal splits require a strictly stronger invariant than extension-count balancing.
