## question

For the partial-order minimax DAG of states reachable from the n-antichain, is its pebbling number polynomial in n for some optimal strategy ordering, or does every legal pebbling strategy require super-polynomial pebbles regardless of edge orientations?

## basis

Budgeted DFS gives space O(n log^2 n) bits in numeric n but no matching lower bound exists; the state DAG is a DAG of posets with known structure.

## falsifier

Exhibit a family of poset states requiring Omega(n^c) pebbles for all c and all valid DAG pebblings, or construct a uniform ordering with pebbling number polynomial in the unary input length.

## implication

A super-polynomial pebbling lower bound would give a space lower bound for uniform exact evaluation of S(n) without relying on circuit lower bounds, while a polynomial pebbling strategy could inform improved work-space algorithms.

4. **Concrete obstruction / failed approach.**
- **Cannot directly lift general-poset hardness.** Brightwell–Winkler (#P-completeness of counting linear extensions) is for arbitrary posets/counting; certificate soundness vs completeness is distinct. There is no known reduction showing the *antichain-root* minimax value is hard for general posets, and AGENTS.md explicitly warns against this transfer.
- **No obvious symmetry/width argument gives super-polynomial for this family.** The poset lattice states are highly structured (all partial orders, but generated by comparison process from antichain). Permutohedron/weak order has structure; pebbling lower bounds are hard to come by without strong separators and this family is unusual.
- **Tree vs DAG:** the “expensive” part in nodes is the tree ($2n!-1$) but it has no merges; merges only appear if you consider all-optimal policies together. Pebbling the all-optimal DAG is different from pebbling a single tree.
- **Edge orientations red herring:** if interpreted literally as orienting edges differently, you can’t change directions in this DAG (dependencies are fixed). More plausibly means “regardless of strategy/order” — in that case, the question becomes “is there some good pebbling order or is every order bad?” But even then, recomputation is a different model.

5. **Honest limits.**
- I found no existing reference in this repo to “pebbling number” of this DAG, no scripts/tests about it, and no board #301 matches. The front given is a precise hypothesis but not obviously established or refuted by current repo knowledge.
- The strongest existing space result is the transcript DFS upper bound $O(n\log^2 n)$ bits (recomputation). A *nontrivial* pebbling lower bound (super-polynomial) would be a new result about this specific family and would need to account for recomputation vs memoization carefully; it’s not ruled out by current evidence, but it’s also not obviously true given the structure and existing upper bounds.
- Conversely, showing polynomial pebbling is trivial if you allow the transcript (tree) strategy? Or if you must use the DAG with memoization, different story. But “legal pebbling strategy” is a bit ambiguous here.
- Most directly: **I cannot produce a concrete all-n family requiring $\Omega(n^c)$ pebbles for all $c$ and all valid DAG pebblings from first principles in this session, nor can I construct a uniform polynomial pebbling ordering** — both would require deeper structural analysis of this specific poset-lattice DAG than I can do conclusively now. The clean obstruction is the transfer problem (general posets != antichain-root family) plus the recomputation trick already giving polynomial space.

**Pragmatic conclusion:** The question is well-posed, but as stated it conflates pebbling (memoization) with recomputation strategies. Given transcript DFS already achieves $O(n\log^2 n)$ space, a super-polynomial pebbling lower bound in the pure memoization sense would be interesting but the complexity implication needs care; there’s no evidence of it in this repo’s literature/notes, and lifting from general partial orders is not permitted by the contract. No repo changes recommended. The exact obstruction is: distinguishing “space with recomputation” from “pebbling number (memoization)” is necessary before claiming either direction as proved/disproved.
